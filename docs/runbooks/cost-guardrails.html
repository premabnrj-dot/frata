Three locks

Lock 1 · Hard stop

The vendor stops charging

Vercel pauses production at your budget. Supabase’s spend cap blocks usage past the plan. Mux has no hard stop.

Lock 2 · Early warning

You hear before it’s big

Vercel emails at 50% and 75% of the budget and sends an SMS at 100%. Mux emails at up to 3 amounts. Supabase emails only after a limit is passed.

Lock 3 · Our own switch

Frrata stops the cause

Admin › Infrastructure alerts on a 3× day, a runaway shop or a viral video. Admin › Modules pauses video for everyone. Every shop has upload caps.

A pause is an outage: every shop goes offline until you resume it. It is set at a level a real month shouldn’t reach, so it only fires on a flood or a bug. The alerts at 50% and 75% give you time to act before it does.

Set these today

Vercel (after upgrading to Pro)

  1. Settings › Billing › Spend Management: turn it on, On-Demand Budget $50, Pause Production Deployments on. The budget covers usage beyond the $20 credit. Vercel checks every few minutes, so expect a few dollars past it.
  2. Settings › My Notifications › Spend Management: email and web at 50%, 75% and 100%; SMS at 100% with your phone verified.
  3. Settings › Billing › Flat Rate CDN: stay on the included tier (1M requests, 1 TB). Vercel moves you up a tier from the next month if a month goes over, so watch the requests meter.
  4. Settings › Members: only you hold Owner or Billing. Anyone else is a free Viewer. Each extra member costs $20 a month.
  5. Don’t add Observability Plus, Speed Insights Plus, Web Analytics Plus, Password Protection or other add-ons unless we decide to. They are monthly charges outside the budget.
  6. Move the other 11 projects to a separate Hobby account. Otherwise they share Frrata’s budget, and a bug in one pauses every shop.
  7. Project frata › Firewall: know where Attack Mode is. In a flood, switch it on: challenged and blocked traffic is free.

Supabase

  1. At launch, move to Pro ($25 a month). Free pauses after a week with no activity, and past its limits can make the database read-only or take it offline.
  2. Organization › Billing › Cost Control: keep the Spend Cap on (the default). Past the plan’s disk, egress, monthly active users or storage, that item stops until next month instead of being charged.
  3. Compute size, point-in-time recovery, IPv4 and log drains are not under the cap. Change none of them without deciding first.

Mux

  1. Settings › Billing › Spending alerts: three alerts, $10, $25 and $50, by email. These need a paid Mux plan; on Free, uploads stop at 10 videos instead.
  2. Mux never stops on its own. Our Admin pause of Product video (built with the shop side) is the stop.
  3. Create a separate read-only token for the dashboard. The current token can write and must not be reused.

Your card

  1. Pay all three from one card used for nothing else, with a monthly limit set in your bank’s app (₹20,000 covers the bound above).
  2. Send billing emails from all three to an inbox you read every day.
