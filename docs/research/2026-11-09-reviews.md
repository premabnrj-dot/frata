- **Timing**: triggers created/paid/fulfilled/**delivered**; 0–365 days; up to 3 reminders; one request per product (1–7

  collect from phone-only (COD) customers [S].

- **Widgets**: list, grid/masonry, media gallery, carousel, star badge on cards, all-reviews page, floating tab,
  featured review; styling: star shape/colour, type, theme matcher.

## 2. Shoppers (Baymard)
53% seek negative reviews; **90% who wanted a rating tried clicking the histogram** (61% of sites allow it); a collapsed
filter used by 7% [V, updated 2025]. 95% rely on reviews; 37% weigh replies to negative ones; 87% of sites don't reply
[V]. Handling negative reviews (#785) and contextual subscores (#767: fit, comfort) Essential, updated Jul 2026 [V].
Apparel: **one combined fit bar** "too small ↔ too large" + a fit bar per review; 33% fail [OLD 2024]. On D2C sites users
suspect curation; photos and negative filters raise trust [OLD]. A perfect 5.0 looks suspicious to 46% [S, June 2025].
Amazon's "Customers say" summarises verified text only [OLD].

## 3. Google
- Review snippets [V]: `Product` is valid; the self-serving rule covers only LocalBusiness/Organization (no shop-wide
  stars); AggregateRating needs ratingValue + count; **visible on the page**; no reviews from other sites. **Added
  2026-07-24: no fake or undisclosed incentivised reviews** in page or markup (money, discounts, vouchers, free products
  unless prominently disclosed).
- Merchant Center product ratings [V]: 50 reviews across the catalogue; monthly XML; **send all reviews incl. low
  stars**; incentives only if not sentiment-based and flagged `is_incentivized_review`; no AI, employee or syndicated
  reviews; Merchant API review data sources since 2026-09-18 [S].

## 4. India
- **BIS IS 19000:2022 — voluntary** (Lok Sabha USQ 5415, 25.03.2026 [V]); build to it (the CCPA benchmark). It asks:
  invite **all** purchasers; verify the reviewer; no paid or staff reviews; **rewards independent of content, marked, and
  excluded from the average**; published moderation criteria applied equally; **never edit a review** (author edits
  marked "edited"); **newest first by default**; date shown; proof of purchase marked and filterable; privacy-protective
  defaults; publish regardless of sentiment **within 72 h, ≤ 1 month**; free seller reply, moderated alike; anyone may
  flag and gets a reason; removal on the author's request; **keep removed reviews and reasons 180 days**; mark imports.
- **E-Commerce Amendment Rules 2026** (G.S.R. 789(E), notified 9 Sep 2026, **in force 1 Jan 2027**): dark-pattern
  guidelines binding with a yearly self-audit certificate; a brand's own site is covered [S]. "Disguised advertisement"
  covers ads posing as user content; hiding negatives / burying the filter / undisclosed incentives fall under dark
  patterns.
- ASCI: a free product is a material connection → disclose [S, Apr 2025].
- DPDP (core obligations ~May 2027): notice at submit; phone/email for request and moderation only; **"First L." by
  default, Anonymous possible**; photos only by the reviewer's upload; deletion on request.
- WhatsApp: an **order-specific feedback request can be a Utility template** (₹0.115); a promo ("10% off for a photo
  review") makes it Marketing (₹0.8631) → put incentives on the thank-you page, not the message [V].

## 5. Abuse
Review gating is prohibited (IS 19000, Merchant Center, Google Maps, FTC) → no "ask only happy customers", no star-
threshold publishing. Verified = linked to a delivered order line. Signals: one phone/device/IP many reviews, no order,
bursts, before delivery, near-duplicates, staff accounts.

## 6. Analytics (derived)
Rating trend (30/90/365); **review rate = reviews ÷ delivered orders asked**; photo share; histogram; reply rate and time
on ≤ 3★; topics; **fit by size** (feeds the size chart and returns); product-page conversion with vs without reviews.

## Recommended set
**MVP**: delivery-timed requests (default 5 days, 1 reminder, never to RTO/cancelled/returned, everyone asked) via the
outbox; WhatsApp Utility template, email fallback; form: stars, optional text, ≤ 5 photos, fit (apparel), size from the
order, "First L."/Anonymous, consent; verified badge, imports labelled; product widget (average, count, **clickable
histogram**, fit bar, filters photos/stars, sort Newest default, photo strip, reply, Report; card star badge);
moderation (auto-publish default or hold-all ≤ 72 h; auto-hold only profanity/links/phones/spam for every rating; hide
only with a listed reason; audit 180 days; no editing); one merchant reply; JSON-LD aggregateRating + reviews on product
pages only; CSV import/export (Judge.me format).
**Later**: photo incentive (thank-you page, badge, out of the average, flagged); carousel/grid + all-reviews page;
analytics; Merchant Center ratings (≥ 50); Q&A; AI summary (verified, ≥ 10, labelled); topics; more attributes;
syndication. **Never**: gating, star-threshold publishing, asterisk edits, seeded reviews, a cross-brand network.
