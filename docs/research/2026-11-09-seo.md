# Ecommerce SEO for Frrata stores — research (checked 2026-11-09)

Current sources only (last 12 months; page date beside each). [P] primary, [S] secondary, [U] unverified or outside
the window. Google Search Central "Last updated" stamps of 2025-12-10 look like a site-wide refresh.

## 1. Technical SEO Google wants
- **Faceted navigation**: filtered URLs that need not rank → block in robots.txt or use fragments; canonical/nofollow
  "less effective long term"; indexed filters: `&` separator, fixed order, real 404 for empty combinations and pages
  past the end [P, crawling-managing-faceted-navigation, 2025-12-18].
- **Pagination**: unique URL per page, `<a href>` links in sequence, never canonical to page 1; rel=next/prev unused;
  load-more needs crawlable pages behind it [P, ecommerce/pagination, 2025-12-10].
- **URLs**: `?key=value`, no repeated keys, no tracking params in internal links, fragments ignored, `?color=` fine
  [P, ecommerce URL structure, 2025-12-10]. Canonical strength: redirects > rel=canonical > sitemap; never robots.txt
  to canonicalise [P, consolidate-duplicate-urls, 2026-07-10]. URL changes: server 301/308 kept ≥ 1 year
  [P, site-move-with-url-changes, 2026-08-20].
- **Variants**: distinct URL per variant (path or preselecting param); `ProductGroup` + `productGroupID`, `variesBy`,
  `hasVariant`; each variant its own sku, offers, color, one size; `SizeSpecification` sizeSystem/sizeGroup
  [P, product-variants, 2026-09-08].
- **Out of stock**: price still shown; availability consistent page/checkout/markup [P, Merchant Center 7052112,
  undated]; Buy button greyed, not hidden [S, ppc.land / SEL, 2026-03-22]. Discontinued → redirect/410 [U, 2020].
- **Sitemaps**: 50,000 URLs / 50 MB per file; priority/changefreq ignored; lastmod used only if consistently accurate
  [P, build-sitemap, 2026-07-08]. Image sitemaps may list CDN images; descriptive filenames and alt
  [P, image-sitemaps 2025-12-10; google-images 2026-03-02].
- **AI controls**: Googlebot rules are the only control for AI Overviews/AI Mode; nosnippet/max-snippet/noindex limit
  them; Google-Extended governs Gemini training and grounding [P, ai-features, 2025-12-10]; Google will honour the
  IETF AIPREF token; allowing Google-Extended lets a site appear in Gemini grounding [P, blog, 2026-10-30].
- **hreflang**: every version lists itself and the others, both ways, plus `x-default`; `en-IN`, `hi-IN`
  [P, localized-versions, 2026-09-21]; no auto-redirect by language, no boilerplate-only translation
  [P, multi-regional, 2025-12-10]; automated translation to manipulate ranking = scaled content abuse
  [P, spam-policies, 2026-08-28].
- **Merchant listing markup**: required name, image, offers (price, priceCurrency); recommended availability,
  itemCondition, shippingDetails (deliveryTime, shippingDestination, shippingRate), hasMerchantReturnPolicy
  (applicableCountry, returnPolicyCategory, merchantReturnDays), priceValidUntil, aggregateRating, review, brand, GTIN,
  mpn, sku, color, size, material, pattern, audience, isVariantOf, inProductGroupWithID [P, merchant-listing,
  2026-09-08]. One global return and shipping policy on Organization/OnlineStore, product-level only to override;
  precedence: Merchant Center feed > settings > product markup > organisation markup [P, 2026-09-08].
  BreadcrumbList and WebSite/site names [P]. Structured data not needed for AI features but must match visible text
  [P, AI optimisation guide, 2026-07-10].
- **Core Web Vitals**: LCP ≤ 2.5 s, INP < 200 ms, CLS < 0.1 [P, 2025-12-10]; "used by our ranking systems", relevance
  wins [P, page-experience, 2026-09-22]; ~48% of mobile origins pass [S, Web Almanac 2025].
- **JavaScript**: render queue; SSR "still a great idea… not all bots run JavaScript"; canonical in the initial HTML,
  real status codes [P, javascript-seo-basics, 2026-03-04]. Cached SSR storefronts are right.
- **IndexNow**: Google does not support it [S, 2026]; Bing, Yandex, Naver, Seznam, Yep do [S, SER 2026-04-20]; Wix pings
  on publish [S]. Google's Indexing API is for JobPosting/BroadcastEvent only [P, 2026-11-07].

## 2. Google now
- **AI Overviews / AI Mode** are grounded in core ranking (query fan-out); eligibility = indexed with a snippet; ignore
  llms.txt, chunking, rewriting for AI; Merchant Center feeds and Business Profile feed product answers; Search
  Console has a Generative AI performance report [P, ai-optimization-guide, 2026-05-15 / 2026-07-10].
- AI Overviews: ~36% of informational, 8% commercial, 5% transactional queries [S, Seer 2026-02]; position-1 CTR on
  AIO keywords 1.6% (Dec 2025) vs 7.3% (Mar 2024) [S, Ahrefs]. Product and collection pages are least exposed.
- India: AI Mode shopping upgrade [S, Business Today 2026-04-07]; Flipkart buy button tested in Gemini/AI Mode
  [S, TechCrunch 2026-09-26]; UCP native checkout not in India [S, ppc.land 2026-05-20].
- **Free listings**: India supported [P, Merchant Center 13692890, undated]; India-only MRP attribute [S].
- **Merchant API v1** only: Content API sunset 2026-08-18, v1beta gone 2026-02-28 [P, 2026-09-10 / 2026-10-28].
  AI-written titles/descriptions go in structured_title/structured_description with
  `digital_source_type=trained_algorithmic_media`; AI images keep IPTC metadata [P, 14743464; using-gen-ai-content
  2026-10-01].
- **Search Console "platform properties"**: the Google queries that reach a brand's Instagram/YouTube/X posts; global
  since 2026-07-29 [P, blog 2026-07-07 / 07-29].
- **Business Profile**: online-only businesses are not eligible [P, 3038177].
- A new low-authority store ranks first on brand searches (Instagram makes them), long-tail product and collection
  queries, and Merchant Center listings (no authority needed); collections are commonly 40–70% of ecommerce organic
  sessions [S, unverified range].

## 3. Competition and keywords
- **Shopify**: auto sitemap (products with image, collections, pages, blogs), product markup in themes, default title,
  manual alt [P, help.shopify.com, undated]; Agentic Storefronts into ChatGPT/Copilot/AI Mode [P, 2026-03-24; no India].
- **Wix**: auto JSON-LD, auto 301 on URL change, AI Visibility Overview, llms.txt, IndexNow on publish [S, 2026].
- **Dukaan**: an SEO plugin for titles/descriptions, a sitemap URL to submit by hand [P, undated]. **Instamojo**: meta
  tags only [P, undated]. WooCommerce: relies on Yoast/Rank Math [U].
- India: women's wear ~719 lakh searches/month, ethnic the largest (~166 lakh), Delhi/Bengaluru/Mumbai 67.8%
  [S, Techmagnate ~2025-07, U]; festive peak Sep–Oct, Tier-3 growth [S, Fynd 2025-11]. Hinglish and "under ₹999" shares:
  no current data [U]; the pattern product + fabric + occasion + price + city is practice.
- Data: Search Console API (searchanalytics.query 25k rows; sitemaps.submit; Site Verification API) [P, 2025-08-28];
  Google Trends API alpha, gated [S, 2026]; Ads Keyword Planning needs an Ads account and developer token
  [P, 2026-10-30].

## 4. Automation
- Default title/meta/alt templates (Shopify, Wix); auto 301 on URL change (Wix); guards: canonical in initial HTML, one
  URL per variant, filters out of the index, 404 for empty filters.
- AI drafts must be reviewed by a person ("manually factcheck and review", including titles, meta, structured data,
  alt) — mass pages without value are scaled content abuse [P, using-gen-ai-content, 2026-10-01].
- Search Console per shop: verify (meta/DNS), submit sitemaps, show queries and the AI report [P].
- llms.txt: Google ignores it ("neither harm nor help") [P, 2026-07-10]; low priority.

## 5. Accessibility
- WCAG 2.2 = ISO/IEC 40500:2025 (2025-10-21) [P, U by a few days]. Essentials: alt, contrast, focus not obscured
  (2.4.11), 24 px targets (2.5.8), accessible authentication (3.3.8), consistent help (3.2.6), labelled errors,
  pickers not colour-only.
- **India**: draft RPwD (Amendment) Rules 2026 (notified 2026-07-16, gazetted 07-20) would make IS 17802 Parts 1–2
  mandatory for e-commerce; an Accessibility Conformance Report (human- and machine-readable); 12 months (turnover
  ≥ ₹500 cr) or 18 months; platforms must guide users to accessible content at upload within 6 months
  [S, nasscom / ISOC 2026-07-29; gazette not opened]. Supreme Court (digital access under Art. 21) hearing 2027-01-20 [S].
- **EAA** (2025-06-28) applies to shops selling to EU consumers; microenterprise exemption for services [S, 2026].
- Alt and semantic HTML help screen readers and AI agents (accessibility tree) [P, 2026-07-10].

## 6. Off-page
- Instagram builds brand searches; platform properties measure them [P]. Genuine mentions count, inauthentic ones are
  discounted [P, 2026-07-10]. Influencers, marketplaces, PR, Pinterest: no current primary source [U].
- Trust: who is behind the site, first-hand expertise, original photos [P, creating-helpful-content, 2026-10-05] →
  About, real photos, size guide, returns/COD visible, genuine reviews with photos, contact.
- Long tail: lookbooks, fabric care, styling and size guides — first-hand, not templated at scale [P].

## Ranked: what a platform should do for its merchants
1. Server-rendered, cached HTML with canonical/price/stock in the first response, real 404/410, green mobile CWV.
2. Merchant Center through Merchant API v1, one-click (free listings + AI Mode products; no authority needed).
3. Complete merchant-listing JSON-LD (variants, brand, GTIN, real reviews; org-level return/shipping; breadcrumbs,
   WebSite), always matching the page.
4. A strict URL model (canonical per product, URL per variant, filters out of the index, self-canonical pages).
5. Search Console set up per shop (verify, sitemaps, queries and the AI report in the console).
6. Accurate sitemaps (images, real lastmod) + IndexNow for Bing.
7. Auto 301 on every handle, collection and domain change, ≥ 1 year; broken-link checks.
8. Collections as landing pages (intro copy, internal links, occasion/fabric/price collections from real queries).
9. Product templates that capture fabric, fit, measurements, care, colour, size (markup + feed).
10. AI-drafted alt and descriptions the merchant approves; labelled AI in the feed.
11. A trust kit: About, size guide, returns/COD, contact, photo reviews from delivered orders.
12. Accessibility by construction (WCAG 2.2 AA / IS 17802) and a conformance report.
13. Hindi versions only when reviewed, with two-way hreflang; no auto-redirect.
14. The Instagram → Search loop (platform property, sameAs, links).
15. AI crawlers allowed (Googlebot, Google-Extended); optional llms.txt; AI-referred visits reported.

Open: gazette text not read; no current data on Hinglish/price-modifier query shares; WooCommerce and Pinterest
unverified; out-of-stock button rule and CWV pass rates secondary only.
