# Research: Ship (M2·2), checked 2026-11-08

Two passes: Shiprocket's API and terms (facts and sources in `frata-integrations` § Shiprocket, 1.6.0) and the UX of
shipping, failed deliveries and tracking. Spec: `docs/specs/2026-11-08-ship.md`.

## Shiprocket, in one paragraph
Per-seller API users only (no OAuth, partner token or platform programme found): the merchant makes an API user and
gives Frrata its email and password, which Frrata seals; a login gives a 10-day token. Orders, rates (a quote with a
recommended courier), AWB, pickup, label, manifest, cancel (before "Out for Pickup"), tracking (≤ 50 AWBs a call) and
NDR actions (re-attempt with a date, phone or address; return) are all in the API. Actual freight is in the account
statement by AWB (rupees). COD remittance has no API. The tracking webhook is set by the merchant in Shiprocket's panel,
per account, with an optional static token and no signature; retries, ordering and duplicates are undocumented; the
URL may not contain "sr" or "kr". The API reference is undated: everything from it is primary but [U].

## UX (each source dated on its page; NCSF = no current source found)
- **Shiprocket panel and app** (blog 2026-05-01, 08-24, 10-05): Ready to ship split by next step (Courier assigned,
  Pickup scheduled, Manifested); NDR rows with one-tap Call buyer / Act / Mark RTO; addresses scoring low flagged
  before a re-attempt; failed pickups escalated on WhatsApp; a buyer's past delivered/RTO counts before shipping;
  WhatsApp updates at dispatched, out for delivery and delivered, plus a delay alert. Courier list labels
  (recommended, cheapest, fastest): NCSF.
- **Shopify** (Fall '26; changelog 2026-06-17, 07-03, 08-03): fulfilment and payment status side by side on the
  orders list; actions at the top of the order summary; "fulfillment not required" as its own status; several
  tracking numbers per order. The Fulfill items screen itself: NCSF.
- **Shopper tracking** (Baymard 2026-05-05, 08-04, 09-09): the expected date as a banner above a progress bar, the
  courier, the tracking number as a link, the scans, what is in the parcel, and a link back to the shop; shoppers who
  had to go to the carrier's site missed the date; tracking is the top account feature for 51%.
- **Sendcloud** (2026-11-06) and **ShipStation** (2026-09-10): bulk labels as a background job with progress, failed
  rows kept for a retry while the rest go through; exceptions raised early (delays, pickups about to expire, parcels
  gone quiet).

## Taken into the design
One primary button that changes with the next step (Ship via the chosen courier → Schedule pickup → Print label);
the courier pre-chosen, others one tap away; failed deliveries first, as work, each with one action and a required
field for a re-attempt; shipping it yourself as a peer path; the shopper's tracking on the shop's own order page,
date first; bulk as a job that never lets one bad order block the rest; amounts converted to paise at the edge.
