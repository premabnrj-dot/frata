## How others do it
- **Shopify** [Vu unless marked]:
  - Three discount classes, applied in order: product, then order (on the reduced subtotal), then shipping.
  - Each discount has a combines-with set.
  - Limits: 5 product/order codes + 1 shipping code per order; 25 active automatic discounts; 20M codes a store;
    250 per bulk call [V, Admin API 2026-10].
  - Fields per code: total uses, once per customer, minimum subtotal or quantity, start/end, eligible buyers.
  - Recent changes: same-item stacking by tag [V, changelog 15 Jul 2026, plan unclear]; discounts per market
    [V, 7 May 2026]; A/B testing discounts with Rollouts [V, 2 Oct 2026].
  - "Sales by discount" counts an order once per discount applied.
- **Stripe** [Vu]:
  - A coupon holds the terms. A promotion code is the shopper-facing string, and many codes can point to one coupon.
  - A code's limits can't exceed its coupon's.
  - Codes are case-insensitive and unique among active codes; a used-up code is never reactivated.
- **India checkouts:**
  - Razorpay Magic Checkout [V, lastmod 23 Oct 2026]:
    - auto-applies the best coupon;
    - shows "unavailable" coupons greyed until the cart qualifies;
    - a prepaid-only flag removes COD;
    - limits: a money budget, total uses, uses per mobile or email, a customer list;
    - personalised coupons are hidden from the list.
  - Razorpay prepaid discount on COD orders [V, 8 Oct 2026]: flat or %, with a cap and a minimum, excluding shipping
    and the COD fee.
  - Shopflo [S, 2026]: payment offers apply automatically when that method is picked; set the free-shipping
    threshold just above AOV.
  - Shiprocket Checkout [S]: tiered, BXGY, bundle and bank offers. Its blog says a 2–5% prepaid discount "can
    encourage" online payment.
  - GoKwik [S]: a free gift at a cart value.
- **UX research:**
  - Baymard [V, 1 Jun 2026]: a visible promo field sends shoppers off to hunt for codes, so collapse it and
    auto-apply. 83% of sites don't auto-apply.
  - Baymard [V, 2 Nov 2026]: show free-shipping qualification in the cart summary. 40% abandon over extra fees.
- **Abuse and leakage** [S]:
  - Coupon extensions and coupon sites scrape codes. KeepCart sells "protected links", where a code works only
    through its link.
  - Controls: single-use unique codes, once per phone, a total cap and a money budget, codes tied to a link, and an
    alert on a redemption spike.

## India's law and tax
- **GST (apparel):**
  - 5% up to ₹2,500 a piece, 18% above, from 22 Sep 2025 [V PIB 17 Sep 2025; S Lok Sabha Q2509, 15 Dec 2025].
  - The slab is checked per piece on the transaction value after an invoice discount [S]. A coupon can move a ₹2,600
    piece to 5%. **CA to confirm.**
  - s.15(3)(b), substituted from 1 Nov 2026 (Notification 03/2026-CT) [V by two sources, gazette unread]: a
    post-supply discount needs a credit note and the buyer's ITC reversal.
  - BOGO is two supplies at one price, with the rate per s.8 (Circular 92/11/2019) [V]. So price each item as its
    own line and never sell a bundle at one price over mixed rates.
  - GST on a shipping or COD-fee discount: no source found [U].
- **Dark patterns:**
  - The CCPA Guidelines 2023 list 13 patterns (false urgency, drip pricing, basket sneaking, bait and switch…).
    A self-audit advisory followed on 5 Jun 2025, and the CCPA has acted on "misleading display of MRPs and
    discounts" [V Lok Sabha Q5415, 25 Mar 2026].
  - Fines so far [S, Bar & Bench, 1 Sep 2026]:
    - a recurring 24-hour countdown, treated as false urgency;
    - Zepto's fees added at checkout (drip pricing);
    - FirstCry's GST added after "prices include tax".

    Penalties were ₹1–7 lakh, against a cap of ₹10 lakh, then ₹50 lakh.
- **E-Commerce (Amendment) Rules 2026,** G.S.R. 789(E), notified 9 Sep 2026, in force **1 Jan 2027** [V by two
  sources, gazette unread]:
  - a price cut must show a **prior price = the lowest price in the previous 30 days**;
  - a yearly dark-pattern self-audit, with a certificate displayed.
  - Who the "e-commerce entity" is on a hosted storefront (Frrata or the merchant): **counsel**.
- **Legal Metrology** [V, consolidated to 13 Feb 2026]:
  - never sell above MRP, which includes all taxes (r.18(2), r.2(m));
  - no two MRPs on identical packages;
  - breaches carry up to ₹5 lakh (Jan Vishwas Act 2026) [S].
- **Payment-method offers:** discount prepaid, never surcharge COD or cards (debit-card MDR is not passed on, RBI
  2017). No current rule on discounting was found [U].
- **Games:** a purchase-linked contest or game of chance is an unfair trade practice unless prescribed (CPA
  s.2(47)(iii)(b)) [S]. **No spin-the-wheel.**
- **Data:**
  - RTO Jan–Aug 2026: COD 30.1% vs prepaid 1.5%, across 416,887 fashion orders [S, Qikink, 4 Nov 2026].
  - Festive Nov 2025 RTO 39.2% [S, Unicommerce, Apr 2026].
  - No source isolates whether coupon orders return more, or what a prepaid discount alone does.

## The codebase today (file:line in the planning session's map)
- **No discount anywhere.**
  - `quote()` (`src/domains/checkout/quote.ts`) is the one pricing engine. The server re-quotes at placement and
    returns `changed` if the total moved.
  - `order_lines.line_discount` exists, unused.
  - CHECK `orders_total` has no discount term.
  - `rateFor` tests the undiscounted unit price.
  - `shippingFor` and `codFor` test the gross subtotal.
- **Code paths that would break on a discounted order:**
  - `switchToCod`, which requires the same subtotal;
  - `retryPayment`, which compares `onlineTotal`;
  - `amounts.charged()`;
  - the invoice, which ignores `line_discount`.
- **Fee:** the Frrata fee base is `merchandise_net` "after discounts" (Phase 1 plan; frata-unit-economics).
- **Modules:**
  - The registry has `rto_shield` ("Prepaid nudge"), `cart_flows` (abandoned bag), `true_profit` and
    `communication`.
  - There is no offers module, and core checkout has no hook point (`check:modules`).
- **Storefront:**
  - Pages are cached for 60 s.
  - The themes work (T0) will add an announcement-bar section and the module slots `card.badge`, `pdp.buybox` and
    `bag.summary` (agreed with the modules session, 2026-11-08).
- **Earlier calls:** the checkout spec left out "coupons (first merchant who asks)" and "prepaid saving" (Phase 2).
