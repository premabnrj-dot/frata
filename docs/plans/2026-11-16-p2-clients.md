## Goal
Ranked problem (5), a premium storefront built by partners ("partners get the builder first"). A partner who can see,
on one screen, which brand is waiting on whom and which of their own invoices is unpaid keeps more brands moving to an
opened store, and opened stores are what Frrata bills. **Measure:** median days from a partner's store created to its
first opening (`partner_links.created_at` → `share_starts_at`), before and 60 days after; share of partner owners who
record at least one bill in 30 days (Frrata Admin counts rows per partner, never reads them, see Security).

## Job
A partner owner opens Clients to answer "what is the state of my business": what needs me now, who I'm waiting on,
what's unpaid, what I earn from Frrata. A Lead asks only "which of my stores needs work"; a Builder only "which stores
do I build".

## The owner's rules this phase obeys (2026-11-15, Admin feedback, applied here)
ONE job in view at a time; a waiting task leads; details behind tabs or open on their own; rarely used or destructive
actions in More, confirmed in a Dialog; no help text; never everything at once. So the approved v2.3 drawing's nine
filter chips, six columns, totals strip and five-section drawer are cut down (see Deviations).

### A client's page (`/partner/clients/[link]`, replaces the drawer)
- `PageHeader`: back "Clients", the store's name and stage `Badge`, meta (its address, the next step in words), More
  (Edit content, Website keys), Open store, and the next step's action as the primary ("Remind Sana", "Hand over",
  "Record bill"; "Remind" when no client name is known).
- **Tabs** (owner): `Your billing` · `Store`, opening on Your billing; a Lead sees the Store facts only, no tabs.
- **Your billing:** the deal line ("Retainer · ₹12,000 a month", "Next bill 1 Dec") with Change billing; bills as rows
  (label, the number in mono, due or paid day, amount, `Badge`); an unpaid bill: Mark paid (toast with Undo; focus
  goes to the toast's Undo, then the row); its More: Delete bill. A paid bill's More: Mark unpaid, Delete bill. Record

- **Store:** `Facts`: Started, Invite sent, Owner joined, Opened, Removed (as they exist), Your share ("20% · ₹704.80 in

- **Record bill** opens filled from the deal (label "December retainer", the amount, due = the deal's next bill);

  **Delete:** `ConfirmDelete` ("Delete 1 bill?", "PT-0412 · ₹12,000. You can’t get it back.", "Keep the bill").

## Data (as revised after the plan reviews, 2026-11-16)

### Two new tables (platform scope, like `partner_members`) — migration 0144
```
partner_client_deals  (partner_id, tenant_id) PK · kind text CHECK in ('project','retainer') · amount bigint CHECK 1..1e10
                      · renews_on date (CHECK kind = 'retainer' or renews_on is null) · updated_at · updated_by → partner_members.id
partner_client_bills  id · partner_id · tenant_id · label text (1–60) · number text (≤ 20, null) · amount bigint (1..1e10)
                      · due_on date · paid_at timestamptz · paid_by → partner_members.id · created_at · created_by → partner_members.id
                      index (partner_id, tenant_id, due_on); partial index (partner_id, due_on) where paid_at is null
partner_bill_tries    user_id · at · index (at) and (user_id, at): the write counter, 60 a person a minute
```
Money is integer paise (ADR-0004); ₹1 to ₹10 crore in the column CHECK, Zod and the definer alike (1e10 paise).
FKs to `partners` (restrict, like `partner_links`) and `tenants` (cascade: a purged store takes its bills with it).
`partner_links` has no unique (partner, tenant) (a store can leave and come back), so "a link exists" is the definer's
check, never an FK. **Locks:** RLS on; `create policy definer_only … as restrictive for all to public using (false)
with check (false)` on each (the 0129 pattern, `check:tenant` § 4); revoked from public, anon, authenticated,
service_role, `frata_app`, `frata_builder`, `frata_admin`. **No Frrata role reads a bill** (the spec: Frrata doesn't see
the partner's own billing). `check-tenant.mjs` gains the `partner_passkey*` rule for `partner_client_*` and
`partner_bill_tries`: any table grant on them fails. A deleted bill is deleted (the partner's own record). Retention:
bills live while the partner does; a purged store cascades; Frrata Admin's partner purge (when it exists) deletes them.

### Definers — migration 0145 (`--custom`)
Every one `set search_path = ''`, `revoke execute … from public, anon, authenticated, service_role, frata_builder`
(the 0131 list; Supabase grants new functions to the first three by default), then one grant. § 85 proves
`has_function_privilege` false for every other role.
- **`app_partner_clients(uid, session, stepup)`** (called by `/partner`, `/partner/team` and the **console layout on
  every console page a partner opens**): stays as lean as today. Two columns added, both null for a Builder and once
  `l.removed_at` is set: `owner_since` and `invite_sent_at`; the owner's email, `has_owner` and `owner_since` come from
  one lateral over `members` (no third subquery); `invite_sent_at` from the existing invites lateral. Plus `link_id`
  (the drawer's key: a store relinked to the same partner has two rows).
- **`app_partner_client_shares(uid, session, stepup)`** (new; `/partner` only; owner only, else no rows): one row per
  link with the link's own snapshotted `share_bps`, `share_months`, `notice_days`, and **the previous IST month's**
  invoice for that store (`period_start = monthStartIST(now) − 1 month`) **only when that month overlaps the link's
  window**, computed in SQL per `l.id`: from `l.share_starts_at` to the earlier of `share_starts_at + share_months` and
  `removed_at + notice_days`; nothing when the store never opened or was removed before it opened. An invoice counts
  when `period_end > starts_at and period_start < ends_at` (the opening month counts whole; no pro-rating — owner Q6).
  Returned: only what `invoiceState` takes (issued, has a number, total, paid, credited) and the **net taxable**
  (taxable − the non-test credit notes' taxable). Never the number, buyer, GSTIN or lines. Issued, non-test rows only;
  a `rolled` row is never counted (its taxable is in the next month's carry-in). The lateral runs under `me.role =
  'owner'` and reads `frrata_invoices_tenant_period` as a range; payments and credit notes through their
  `(tenant_id, invoice_id)` indexes. The SQL window is the lock; `shareWindow` in TS shows the end date.
- **`app_partner_bill_summary(uid, session, stepup)`** (owner only): one row per link with the deal and aggregates
  (unpaid total, earliest overdue `due_on`, earliest due within 7 days and its amount, last paid). No bill rows.
- **`app_partner_client_bills(uid, session, stepup, link_id)`** (owner only): the bills of one link, only when that link
  belongs to the caller's partner; the last 13 months plus every unpaid, at most 200. Called only when `?client=` names
  a link the page already listed.
- **Writes:** `app_partner_deal_set(…, link_id, kind, amount, renews_on)`, `app_partner_bill_add(…, link_id, label,
  number, amount, due_on) → id`, `app_partner_bill_paid(…, bill, paid boolean)`, `app_partner_bill_delete(…, bill)`.
  Each: the caller is the partner's active, not-suspended **owner** member (a Lead or Builder gets `not_owner`); the
  link and the bill are matched on `partner_id = caller's partner` (another partner's id is `not_found`); the live
  12-hour check (`app_partner_need(…, false)`); bounds re-checked; the 500-bills-a-client cap counted under
  `pg_advisory_xact_lock(hashtextextended(partner || tenant, 0))`. The counter is its own statement before the write
  (`app_partner_bill_try`, 60 a person a minute, failing closed), so a refused write still counts; `app_partner_try`
  and its passkey limits stay byte for byte as they are.
- **`admin_partner_bill_stats()`** — `frata_admin` only (revoked from `frata_app`): partner id, first bill's date,
  count. Never a bill. Backs the measure; no screen yet.

### Engines (pure, `src/domains/partners/`)
- `bills.ts`: `billState(bill, today)` on `istDayKey` → paid · overdue (due before today) · due (within 7 days) · later;
  `dealLine`; `unpaidTotal` (**removed stores count**: a final bill is still owed); `retainersMonthly` (**retainer deals
  of stores not removed only**); `renewsSoon(deal, today)` (≤ 14 days).
- `next.ts`: `clientNext({ stage, role, summary, deal, ownerSince, inviteSentAt }, now)` → `{ who: 'you' | 'client' |
  'nobody', step, since?, action? }`, in this order for the owner: a bill overdue → you · Remind; building → you · Hand
  over; invite sent → client · Remind; with the owner (not opened) → client · Remind; retainer renews ≤ 14 days → you ·
  Record bill; a bill due ≤ 7 days → client; opened → nobody; removed → nobody ("Share ends …"). A Lead: building →
  you (no action; Hand over is the owner's), invite sent / with the owner → client, else nobody. The tab counts read
  the same function.
- `share.ts`: `shareOf(netTaxable, bps)` (floor, integer paise), counted only when `invoiceState` says `paid`; a
  `closed`, `credited` or missing month reads "Nothing billed", an unpaid one "Due" — both left out of the header total.
  Golden-month tests: a partial credit note, a rolled month then its carry-in, an opening on the 1st, 12th and 31st, a
  removal plus 90 days, terms snapshotted unlike `PARTNER_TERMS`, a store relinked to another partner.
- **Remind:** `waText(text)` joins `src/domains/settings/whatsapp.ts` ("THE ONE wa.me LINK"), numberless; `remind.ts`
  holds only the drafted words, built on the server from the store's name and the bill's label with control and bidi
  characters (U+202A–202E, U+2066–2069) stripped, each part capped, `encodeURIComponent`; the link opens with
  `rel="noopener noreferrer"`.
- The share's (i) paragraph on today's page is removed (owner, 2026-11-15: no help text).

### The client page's address
`/partner/clients/<link id>`: the page reads `app_partner_clients` and finds the link among the rows it returned (for a
Lead: assigned, not removed); anything else is the page's own "not found" panel. It never sets a tenant context.
`app_partner_client_bills(…, link_id)` re-checks the link belongs to the caller's partner. The page is a server
component; the bill dialog, the billing dialog, Mark paid / Undo and Delete are client islands, the dialogs loaded on
open. Reuse: `ConfirmDelete`, `Facts`, `DataTable`, `PageHeader`, `ViewTabs`, the toast's Undo, `src/domains/time/`.

## Design review (frata-planning § 3)
- **UI/UX:** above; states listed; keyboard: tabs are links, rows are one target (`data-row-link` opens the drawer by
  `?client=<id>` in the address so Back closes it), the drawer is a `Dialog` (Esc, focus back to the row). Norman:
  the next step names who acts (icon + words), Mark paid has Undo, Delete confirms, the deal is a default (retainer
  renews monthly when set).
- **Tenancy:** no tenant data is read through `withTenant`; every read is a partner definer keyed on the caller's
  `partner_members` row from `requirePartner` (uid, session, step-up), never an id from the page. The tenant id in a
  write comes from the form but is accepted only where a link exists for the caller's own partner (as
  `app_handover_invite` today). The Frrata invoice figures cross tenants by design (partner terms: 20% of what Frrata
  collects) and reach the partner **owner** only, for linked stores, inside the share window. `tenancy-auditor`.
- **Modularity:** engines above (no I/O); `src/lib/db/partners.ts` gains the definer wrappers (it is 236 lines; the
  bills move to a sibling `src/lib/db/partner-bills.ts`); actions in `src/app/(partner)/partner/(workspace)/actions.ts`
  (new); rendering in `_components/` (list, drawer, bill dialog). The page stays under 200 lines.
- **SSOT / DRY:** a bill's state, the next step, the share, the stage: each one function, read by the page, the tab
  counts and the header totals. Paid-ness of a Frrata invoice is `invoiceState` only. No stored status anywhere
  (`paid_at` is a timestamp).
- **YAGNI:** no PDF invoices, GST lines, payment links or reminders sent by Frrata (the partner's own records; signal:
  partners ask in Admin feedback); no CSV yet (signal: a CA asks); no Studio credits until C1; no At risk until
  consent at linking exists.
- **KISS:** two tables, six definers, four engines, one page with a drawer. The deal could live as bills only, but
  the retainer's amount and renewal date drive "Renews soon" and the header's monthly figure.
- **Scalability:** ≤ 50 stores a partner today (`MAX_PARTNER_PEOPLE` is people; stores capped by unopened 10 and
  practice); bills ≤ 500 a client. Index (partner_id, tenant_id, due_on). 500 partners × 50 × 500 = 12.5 M rows worst
  case; real use far less.
- **Performance:** the owner's page runs three definers in `Promise.all` (`app_partner_clients`, `…_client_shares`,
  `…_bill_summary`), plus `…_client_bills` when the drawer is open; a Lead's and a Builder's page run one, as today. No `withTenant`, no cache (force-dynamic,
  a person's private list). EXPLAIN both at 50 stores × 500 bills before merge; budget < 100 ms. Partner first-load JS:
  the drawer and the bill dialog are client islands loaded on open. `speed-reviewer`.
- **Reliability:** no outbound call (Remind is a link the partner's phone opens). Writes are single statements.
- **Security:** each action: `requirePartner` → owner role (`partnerCan(role, 'seeClientTerms')`; the definer
  re-checks); Zod (label ≤ 60, number ≤ 20 `[A-Za-z0-9/-]`, amount in rupees 1–10,00,00,000 → paise, a real date
  2020–2100, kind enum, uuid ids); replies are `{ ok }` or a named code; the rate limit fails closed. The drafted
  WhatsApp text is built on the server from the store's name and the bill's label, URL-encoded, never HTML. A bill's
  words render as text. No PII beyond what the partner types. `security-reviewer`.
- **Motion and visuals:** the drawer is the standard sheet (320 ms in on the spring, 200 ms out); tabs as `ViewTabs`;
  Mark paid's toast; nothing new. The next step's who-icon: `you` (person), `client` (hourglass), `nobody` (check).
  The empty state keeps its `PictoTile` (`build`).
- **Unit economics:** nothing a merchant or partner pays changes, but the rupee share discloses the brand's Frrata bill
  (owner Q1); the share figure is read from Frrata's own
  invoices and `PARTNER_TERMS` (20%, 24 months, 90 days). It shows what Frrata will owe a partner, so it must be
  exact: ex GST, issued non-test invoices paid in full only, floor to the paisa. `unit-economics-reviewer`.
- **Storefront API:** n/a (nothing it answers changes).
- **Blast radius:** `app_partner_clients` changes its return type (drop + create, grants re-stated). Its wrapper
  `partnerClients()` has three callers: the Clients page, `/partner/team` (`team/page.tsx:31`) and **the console layout
  on every console page a partner member opens** (`console/layout.tsx:106`, the building banner). That is why the
  invoice work lives in its own definer called only by `/partner`. Flows `40`, `80`, `86` cover all three.
