Spec: `docs/specs/2026-11-08-billing.md` (approved by the owner, 2026-11-08; built to test mode until Frrata's GSTIN,
SAC and Razorpay account exist) · Status: in progress

## Review findings (2026-11-08) — binding; they override the spec where they differ
Reviews: architecture-reviewer, security-reviewer and unit-economics-reviewer.

**Data and SSOT**
1. **A record per order billed.** `frrata_invoice_fees` (tenant_id, invoice_id, order_id **unique**, billable_at,
   base, fee_bps, fee) and `frrata_fee_credits` (order_id **unique**, …).
   - A run bills billable orders that have no fee row.
   - It credits fee rows whose order is no longer billable and has no credit.
   - This makes the run idempotent by construction.
2. **One billable rule, with a date.**
   - `billableAt(o): Date | null` in `src/domains/orders/outcomes.ts`: delivered, else shipped + 30 days; null after
     came back or Lost, or when never shipped.
   - `billableDelivered` is redefined as `billableAt ≤ now`.
   - Its SQL twin goes beside `stateSql` in `order-predicates.ts`, with a parity test.
3. **One fee rule.**
   - `feeForOrder(history, order)` = `deliveredFee(merchandiseNet, plan in force at billableAt .feeBps)`, in
     `src/domains/plans/entitlements.ts`. No separate `billing/fee.ts`.
   - The base is `merchandise_net`.
   - Profit (`discounts.ts` `feeBpsIn`) and the order page move onto it in B0.
4. **One "orders this month"**: billable orders. It is used by `usage()`, the merchant meter and Admin › Merchants.
   The included orders come from the plan in force at month end.
5. **Names and numbers.**
   - Tables `frrata_invoices` and `frrata_invoice_lines` (the word "bill" already means a merchant's bill of supply).
   - `platform_counters(kind, fy)` is written only by the issue definer. Numbers are assigned at issue, never at draft.
6. **Immutability.**
   - Triggers refuse UPDATE or DELETE of money columns and lines once `issued_at` is set; proved in `verify:tenancy`.
   - No void after issue: a correction is a credit note.
   - State is derived from timestamps (`issued_at`, captured payments, `mandate_revoked_at`), not status columns.
7. **Late outcomes are credit notes** that cite the original invoice and carry into the next payment. This keeps the
   owner's "credit next month" and is cleaner for GST s.34. The CA confirms before real invoices. Place of supply
   comes from `gstinState(gstin)` when there is a GSTIN. Reuse `gstHeads`. A trial ending mid-month is charged by days.

**Roles and tenancy**

8. **Who writes what.**
   - **Draft:** the cron, as `frata_app` inside `withTenant`, reads the shop's orders and upserts drafts. RLS allows
     INSERT and UPDATE only while `issued_at is null`.
   - **Issue:** one definer, called from `src/lib/admin/billing.ts` with `StaffOk` (`ADMIN_ONLY`), numbers, freezes
     and enqueues in one transaction. This is recorded as an ADR-0007 amendment.
9. **Money is visible to owners and admins only.**
   - Billing is read in `src/lib/queries/billing.ts`. Each export takes the member and refuses outside `CAN_MANAGE`.
   - A gate allows only `console/settings/billing/**` and the layout's banner to import it.
   - Column grants keep gateway token and mandate columns away from `frata_app`.
10. **Billing details.**
    - `frata_app` may UPDATE only legal name, GSTIN, address, state and email on `billing_accounts`, through
      `asMember(CAN_MANAGE)`.
    - The mandate changes only through definers.
    - A support visit can neither set up autopay nor pay.
11. **Proofs (checks 39+).**
    - Each shop sees only its own rows.
    - INSERT and DELETE are denied.
    - UPDATE is allowed only on the detail columns.
    - Composite foreign keys `(tenant_id, invoice_id)`.
    - The issued-invoice trigger.
    - The definers' grants.
    - `bill_runs` and `billing_events` have no `frata_app` select.
    - vitest: staff and partner see nothing.

**Collections**

12. **Frrata's own webhook is separate.**
    - Route `/api/webhooks/billing`, provider `frrata_razorpay`, with its own secret. The current and previous secret
      are both accepted.
    - A 256 kB cap, then HMAC on the raw body.
    - Events stored in `billing_events` (no `frata_app` grant) through a definer that only inserts.
    - Applied by `app_apply_billing_event(event_id)`, which reads the stored row. The bill is found by the gateway
      order id we created.
    - Status only moves forward. The payload is trimmed.
13. **No double charge.**
    - One outbox row per attempt (`bill:attempt`). A Razorpay order is created with `receipt = number:attempt`
      before charging, and looked up before creating another.
    - A timeout becomes "unknown", settled by the gateway or webhook, never resent.
    - The debit is scheduled ≥ notice `sent_at` + 25 h.
    - A bank transfer is recorded under a unique UTR.
    - Billing outbox kinds are never paused by a module switch.
14. **Mandate setup trusts no browser.** Checkout's signature is verified, the order is matched to this shop's
    pending mandate row, and the token is fetched from Razorpay on the server.
15. **Secrets.**
    - `RAZORPAY_BILLING_KEY_ID`, `_KEY_SECRET` and `_WEBHOOK_SECRET` are Production-only and Sensitive.
    - They are read only in `src/lib/billing/gateway.ts`.
    - Test mode uses a fake gateway (e2e `fakes/razorpay`).
    - **S1 (previews on the production database) must close before real money.**
16. **Rendering at issue.** Issue commits rows only. The invoice PDF renders on download from the frozen rows (the
    D0 renderer).
17. **Admin.**
    - "Issue all" takes the run id plus the expected count and total, and refuses on any mismatch; the typed
      confirmation is checked on the server.
    - Waivers and credits are capped at the invoice total, audited in the same transaction.
    - The GST CSV escapes `= + - @`.
    - Everything is `ADMIN_ONLY`.

**Money rules (unit-economics-reviewer)**

18. **Base and rounding.**
    - The fee base is `orders.merchandise_net`: taxable after offers, excluding GST, delivery and the COD fee, as
      stored at billableAt. It is never the GST-inclusive total; on Launch that would overcharge ₹97 a month.
    - The fee is rounded **per order** with `applyBps`, half up, then summed, so a credit reverses exactly what the
      order was billed.
19. **Orders over the plan** are a ₹0 line: the count and a flag only. Every delivered order already pays the fee.
    An upgrade is suggested only when it is cheaper. Launch and Growth break even at ₹3.5 lakh of delivered value;
    Growth and Scale at ₹20 lakh.
20. **GST heads.**
    - Each head is computed on the invoice's taxable value; one head is never derived from another.
    - The total is not rounded to the rupee.
21. **Rolling small bills.** A bill rolls only when its taxable value is under ₹100, at most once in a row; it then
    invoices whatever the amount (Rule 47's 30 days, CA to confirm). A negative total carries forward and is never
    refunded.
22. **Collection method.**
    - Growth and Scale default to eNACH: their bills exceed ₹15,000, so UPI or card would need approval every month.
    - Card and pay links are fallbacks; card costs 2% + GST.
23. **Flags and partner share.**
    - Flag a shop whose hand-marked Lost rate is over 1%.
    - The partner share is 20% of the taxable value of **paid** invoices after credits. The service period decides
      the 24 months. A credit note is clawed back on the next statement. Gateway fees are not deducted from it.
    - Recompute the skill's stale share figures: a Launch store pays ₹687.16, not ₹704.80.
24. **Returns after delivery** (a customer return, not a Came back) are not credited. This is the recommended rule;
    the owner may change it.

**B0 review findings (2026-11-08; tenancy-auditor, impact-reviewer) — fixed or carried, binding**
- A credit names its fee within its own shop: `frrata_fee_credits (tenant_id, order_id)` → `frrata_invoice_fees`
  `(tenant_id, order_id)` (shop A could otherwise block B's credit). Proof check 39 holds it.
- The freeze triggers are invoker functions with `search_path = ''`, revoked from the API roles, plus a TRUNCATE guard;
  proof check 18 now covers every definer in `public`, not only `app_*`.
- Admin › Billing is `ADMIN_ONLY` on the page and in `src/lib/admin/billing.ts`.
- **The window:** a run drafts this month (until issued) and next only; a fee is billed at most one invoice late
  (billableAt ≥ the 1st of the month before last) and never before it happens (≤ now). A shop's first invoice is
  therefore not its history. An order billable before any plan row is neither billed nor counted.
- The cron gives billing at most 15 s (shops not reached are counted and drafted next run).
- Place of supply: the GSTIN's state, else the state typed. Heads: `supplyKind`; each head on the taxable value
  (finding 20 wins over "reuse `gstHeads`", which splits one amount).
- Admin starts the run, which writes as `frata_app` inside each shop with ids from `app_shops_to_bill`: record it with
  issue in the ADR-0007 amendment.
- **Carried to B1 (done):** Admin › Merchants and the shop's page count billable orders: both read the billing run's
  draft of next month's invoice (`orders_billed`, with its `drafted_at`), so they agree and nothing restates the rule;
  no definer migration was needed. `app_admin_order_counts` has no reader since and goes with the next Admin migration. **Carried to B2:** issue redrafts first, under a row lock,
  then numbers; past drafts never issued are redrafted or discarded before go-live; a billing start date per shop.

**B1 as built (2026-11-08; security-reviewer and impact-reviewer findings applied)**
- Console › Settings › Plan and billing: this month so far is the billing run itself in a transaction that is always
  rolled back (proved with and without an existing draft); it waits at most 1 s for the run's row lock and otherwise
  says the estimate is being updated. Paying and Invoices say plainly that nothing is charged yet.
- Billing details: owners and admins (a support visit may correct them, audited); membership checked before parsing;
  every field capped before any pattern; the email pattern is linear (it backtracked: 7.7 s at 80k characters); no web
  addresses in the legal name or address; control and bidi characters removed. frata_app holds INSERT and UPDATE on the
  detail columns only (finding 11's "INSERT denied" applies to invoices, not billing details).
- check:billing-read: only console/settings/billing/** imports src/lib/queries/billing.ts.

**B2–B4 as built (2026-11-08; security-, impact- and tenancy reviews applied; test mode only)**
- B2 (0054–0055): the issue definer checks the reviewed count and total and numbers rows itself; test mode issues in
  the T series only with BILLING_TEST_ISSUE; test invoices are never collected by hand nor in the GST CSV.
- B3 (0056–0057, fixed by 0059–0060): one sender per debit (app_billing_attempt_claim, sending_at); nothing after the
  claim throws, so the outbox never resends a charge; the daily run applies stored webhook events, settles open
  attempts from the gateway after 2 h, or closes them when no payment was made. Pay now and a debit never overlap
  (Pay now refused while a debit is open; a debit not begun while a Pay-now order is open, 2 h; one Pay-now order is
  reused); the amount due is re-checked before charging; a capture beyond what was due is kept in over_paid and shown
  in Admin for a refund. The gateway is never called inside a transaction; 10 starts a member per 10 min. The billing
  webhook keys events by a hash of the signed body and caps a chunked body while reading it.
- B4 (0058, 0061): app_change_plan is bound to the current shop and to an owner or admin member, locks the shop,
  audits each change and cancel, and marks the shop's own rows by_shop (Admin cannot write by_shop: trigger). A
  change Frrata makes in Admin replaces the shop's scheduled one (app_admin_clear_shop_plan_change). The difference is
  ONE rule, planChangeOwed (src/domains/billing/bill.ts) — since trials TR0 the one "made up by days" rule for any month
  (a plan change, or the plan after a trial that ran on its 1st) — billed as its own line kind 'change'; the dialog shows
  planChangeAdds, the same rule, read from planBilledIn (src/lib/billing/draft.ts) — proved equal on a real database.
  Paid modules from the console are deferred (signal: the first shop asking to add one itself).
- Owner calls (2026-11-08): each order's fee is charged at the rate of the plan it SHIPPED under (feeForOrder's
  rateAt; feeRateAtSql in SQL, read by billing and ₹ kept alike; an order shipped before the shop's first plan takes
  that plan's rate), so a short upgrade cannot buy a lower rate; an upgrade during a trial waits until the trial is over
  (planChangeStarts, the definer's twin). A downgrade starts on the 1st.
- Open before real money: system definers on their own role, not frata_app (S1); eNACH token confirmation before a
  mandate counts as active; the webhook's amount checked against the attempt; Admin plan changes mid-month are now
  billed by days on the next invoice (the Admin form should say so).

**Owner calls raised by the reviews:** an email provider for the notice and invoice email (B3; until then, the
