## Owner calls — accepted as recommended (owner, 2026-11-09: "go with your recommendations")
1. **Price shape.** `addon_prices` holds one price per module, not per tier. Recommended: **included in Scale, ₹499/mo
   add-on on Growth**, 30-day trial through `tenant_addons.trial_ends_at`; no free cap (the messages are the merchant's
   cost — a cap saves Frrata nothing). Launch stays out (owner); serving it would cost a Launch shop ~₹33/mo in messages.
2. **WhatsApp opt-in.** No opt-in is recorded anywhere today. Meta requires one before a business-initiated message.
   Recommended: a checkout line "Order updates on WhatsApp" (on by default, the shopper can untick), stored on the order;
   only opted-in orders are asked on WhatsApp.
3. **Returned orders are asked too.** The spec said "never returned"; IS 19000 says invite every purchaser and the
   unhappy ones carry the fit signal (North star 2). Recommended: ask returned lines; never RTO or cancelled ones.
4. **Email waits.** Orders keep no shopper email and the email spec is a draft (no sender). Recommended: R1 asks on
   WhatsApp only; a shop without Communication connected sees "Connect WhatsApp to ask for reviews" on the Apps card.
5. **Adding the app.** Add-ons are Frrata-granted today (Apps › Ask). Recommended for R1: keep "Ask" (we grant, with the
   trial); self-serve add with Razorpay autopay is its own small spec.

## Speed and security reviews (2026-11-09) — what they changed
**Needs the owner (asked 2026-11-09):**
- **Opt-in unticked** (security H3, reverses call 2): DPDP s.6 needs a clear affirmative act, so a pre-ticked box is
  not consent; Meta needs an opt-in naming the business and opt-outs honoured. Proposed: an unticked "Send me order
  updates and a review request from <shop> on WhatsApp"; the order stores `wa_opt_in_at`, notice version and language;
  `wa_opt_outs(tenant, phone_hmac, at)` fed by a Stop button and a link on the form, checked before every send.
- **Sending capacity** (speed 2): today's outbox drains ~30–50 sends a day (one daily cron, one row at a time); 500
  shops need 31k–110k. Tasks 6–7 wait for Vercel Pro and a 5-minute cron (P5's open item), with `drainSoon()` after
  each enqueue and `eachLimited` across shops (~57k an hour).
- **Previews hold the production database** (security H4, S1 reopened): before task 6 merges, the owner gives Preview
  its own database in Vercel; draining runs only when `VERCEL_ENV === 'production'`; a missing secret defers a row.

**Folded in (no owner call):**
- Statement budget restated: the cold product page is 16 data statements today (measured); reviews adds at most one
  transaction and two statements. Module facts move into `coreShop`'s cached `Promise.all` (`shop:<t>`), access computed
  at render; summary and first page are one statement.
- Tags: each product's summary `shop:<t>:reviews:<productId>`; the ratings entry alone `shop:<t>:reviews`. A shopper's
  published submit purges the product tag only (card stars on the 60 s backstop); owner actions purge both; held,
  the 72 h crossing and the purge purge nothing. Summary and ratings `revalidate` 60. ProductGroup keyed by group id.
- The ask sweep: a fixed window `[now−N−2d, now−N)`, limit 500 per shop (13 ms measured); its own cron entry.
- Indexes: reviews `(tenant, product, created desc) where hidden_at is null and author_deleted_at is null`; unique
  `(tenant, order_line)`; `(tenant, hidden_at) where hidden_at is not null`; held queue `(tenant, created) where held
  and not approved and not hidden`; review_requests unique `(tenant, order)`, unique `(tenant, token_hash)`,
  `(tenant, sent_at) where reminded_at is null`; photos and reports `(tenant, review)`.
- JS: 3 kB headroom on the product page; filters and "more" load through `next/dynamic` on first interaction.
- Link token: HMAC over `tenant|order|request.token_nonce` (16 random bytes) with a key id, so a link can be reissued;
  `history.replaceState` after reading the fragment; no third-party script on `/review`.
- Submit checks the line belongs to the token's order and is delivered or returned. An edit clears `approved_at` and
  runs moderation again, never clears `hidden_at`.
- Photos: `tenants/<t>/reviews/<requestId>/<uuid>-<w>.webp`, upload tokens for that folder only, unique `(tenant, base)`;
  a new `reviewPhotosOk` that fails closed (sharp, pixel limit, width matches the path, EXIF/XMP refused); photos are
  held for every shop alike until approved; orphans swept after 24 h; the route accepts only client-token requests.
  Blobs of a hidden review are deleted after the undo window (at once only for unlawful content).
- Rate limits (per IP /64): reviewView 60/10 min open; reviewSubmit shop+IP 20/h and token 10/h closed; reviewUpload a
  lifetime cap per request closed; reviewReport 10/h and one per review per IP closed.
- Report IPs: HMAC (HKDF info `review-reports v1`) over `tenant|review|ipBucket`, purged with the 180-day log; reports
  never hide a review on their own.
- Roles: `CAN_MODERATE` (owner, admin, staff, partner; never the accountant); Settings, import and export `CAN_MANAGE`;
  a support visit may Hide (audited), never Reply; the template is created by the owner or admin's Turn-on, never by a
  sender or a grant.
- WhatsApp statuses become the request's times, then each is trimmed to `{wamid, status, at, code}` (no recipient
  phone kept); `message_template_status_update` added to the subscribed fields; a re-categorisation stops sends.
- Import: its own route (`CAN_MANAGE`, Origin check, 5 MB, 20k rows, cells capped), names stored as "First L.", no URLs.
  Export: formula-escaped cells (incl. full-width), `attachment`, `no-store`, no phone or email column.
- Text: React only (no linkify, no HTML); Zod with NFC and bidi/zero-width/control stripped; title ≤ 100, body ≤ 2000,
  reply ≤ 1000; rating 1–5; fit an enum; the display name a choice, never typed. The console preview shows published
  reviews only.
- Orders typed in by hand (DM orders) have no opt-in and are never asked.

## Design decisions from the reviews (no owner call needed)
- **State from timestamps** (no status column): `held_at` + `held_reason` (set at submit), `approved_at`, `hidden_at` +
  `hidden_reason`, `edited_at`, `author_deleted_at`. `reviewState(r, now)` in `src/domains/reviews/state.ts`; held and
  `now ≥ created + 72 h` reads as published (no job; the 60 s cache meets it), with a parity-tested SQL twin. Links and
  phone numbers are hidden with a listed reason, never auto-published.
- **The link**: one token per ORDER (one message), HKDF info `review-links v1` (never `orderToken`), carried in the
  fragment: `/review#t=…`, one static page per shop (as `/orders#t=`). Submits POST through `shopRequest`; the tenant
  is the host's (`requireShop`); stored as a hash, looked up by `(tenant_id, token_hash)`; wrong shop and unknown token
  answer the same 404. Valid 60 days from delivery; one review per line, editable by its author until then.
- **Outbox payload is ids only** (`{ requestId }`; the outbox read policy has a null-tenant branch). The sender opens
  `withTenant`, re-reads the request, re-derives (still delivered or returned, not reviewed, module usable, opted in),
  rebuilds the link; `last_error` never holds a phone or a Graph body. Dedupe `review:<order>:<n>`; the request row and
  `enqueue()` in one transaction.
- **The WhatsApp Utility sender** (Communication gains its first `outboxKinds`): template created in the merchant's WABA,
  status from `message_template_status_update` (H-utility: it must stay Utility), sent with `fetchWithTimeout`. The
  `SENDERS` map moves into a file `check:modules` lists as a hook.
- **Tables** (tenant first everywhere; composite FKs; `frata_admin` no grant; proof rows per table): `review_requests`
  (tenant, order, sent/opened times), `reviews` (tenant, order_line → order_lines on delete set null (order_line_id),
  product from the line on the server, rating, text, fit, size, display_name stored at submit, the timestamps above,
  reply text + replied_at as columns), `review_photos` (tenant, review; CHECK base in `tenants/<tenant>/reviews/`),
  `review_reports` (tenant, review, reason, ip hash). Unique `(tenant_id, order_line_id)`.
- **Photos**: `reviews` folder in `PATH_SHAPE`/`acceptedBase`; the console upload route refuses it; a shopper upload route
  (token verified in the host's tenant first, own fail-closed limit, no row from the callback, orphans swept). Hiding or
  deleting a review deletes its blobs. The share-card route keeps refusing the `reviews` folder.
- **Storefront**: one storefront hook (listed in `check:modules` HOOKS) returns `{ below, rating }` from ONE cached
  summary read; `rating` is passed into `productJsonLd` (widget and markup cannot disagree; no `aggregateRating` at 0;
  on a ProductGroup it is the group's). Card stars from a separate `shopCached` ratings entry (~12 kB at 600 products)
  under a new `reviews` scope tag — a review never purges the catalogue. Named columns only, published only, no PII in
  any cached object. Filter and "more" go through `shopRequest`, `no-store`. Cold product page ≤ 12 statements.
- **Plan-restricted add-on**: `addOnFor: ['growth','scale']` in the registry, `planCode` in the module facts; `grant()`
  refuses off-plan (a downgrade stops sends and hides the widget); `offerFor` says "In Growth and up"; `giveTrial` and the
  billing draft refuse it too.
- **Daily run**: the ask sweep gets its own time slot (the 60 s run is spent); batch × shops written against 10× peak,
  EXPLAIN on `orders_tenant_delivered`; the 180-day purge of removed reviews.
- **Import**: Judge.me CSV, text only — no `reviewer_email`, no outside `picture_urls`; labelled "Imported". Export
  owner/admin only, `no-store`, cells escaped against formula injection.
- **Words**: the module's shopper words (en, hi) live in the module, not `words.ts`. Chips and segmented controls become
  primitives named in `frata-design-language` before the console screens.

## Economics (unit-economics-reviewer, 2026-11-09; illustrative shapes)
WhatsApp is billed by Meta to the merchant's own number: ~₹271/mo at Growth, ~₹943/mo at Scale (one message per order
plus one reminder; never one per product). Frrata's own cost ~₹20–80/mo (Blob). Break-even for the merchant: +0.13–0.27%
orders or −0.5–0.9% size returns. Hypotheses with tests: H-lift (product split at 2–3 partners, 4 weeks), H-fit (size
returns with vs without ≥ 10 fit answers, 8 weeks), H-utility (template category, 30 days of status updates), H-rate (8%
in month 2).
