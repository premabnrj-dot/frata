## Finding: a never-opened store is billed (task 5 of the brief, confirmed in the SQL)
- `drizzle/0066_trial_partners.sql:84-89`: `app_shops_to_bill(p_before)` returns every tenant with a `tenant_plans` row
  starting before `p_before` and an `owner` member. **No opening condition** (`tenants.first_opened_at`,
  `shop_settings.published_at`).
- `drizzle/0065_trial_signup.sql:83-85`: the trial is refused when there is no claim key or the email or phone was
  claimed; `:97` inserts the plan row with `starts_at = now()` at creation either way.
- `src/lib/billing/run.ts:26` drafts every id from `shopsToBill` daily; `src/lib/billing/draft.ts:78-82` reads every plan
  row with no opening fact; `src/domains/billing/bill.ts:103-112` prices each row from `max(startsAt, monthStart)` by
  paid days, and `:166-171` bills month M in advance unless a trial runs on the 1st.
- **So:** a store refused a trial is drafted from the day it is created, and its first invoice (the 1st of the next
  month) charges the creation month by days and the next month in advance, open or not. A store **with** a trial that
  never opens is billed from sign-up + window + length (`0063_trials_access.sql:50-61`, the cap; trials rule 1 meant it:
  "stops never open, keep a free console"). Under call 2 both are billing before the first opening.
- **Fix (O2·5):** 0169 adds `first_opened_at is not null` to `app_shops_to_bill`, and one pure rule,
  `fromOpening(plans, firstOpenedAt)` in `bill.ts`, read by the draft and by Plan and billing's estimate, starts the
  plan at the first opening. **Flagged for `unit-economics-reviewer`:** a never-opened store then pays nothing however
  long it stays; the 45-day cap no longer bills (it still ends the trial of a late opener); a fee on an order made before
  opening (an assisted DM order) finds no plan and is not billed; a trial store that never opens still reaches grace and
  pause (`src/lib/queries/trial-pause.ts`, open question 3).
- **Trials rule 3 is superseded in writing** by one amendment line under rule 3 in `docs/specs/2026-11-08-trials.md`
  (O2·12): "Superseded 2026-11-23 (owner, call 2 of `docs/specs/2026-11-23-merchant-onboarding.md`): the plan is chosen
  at Open your store, Launch preselected, the trial on that plan; a store is not billed before it first opens. Built in
  `docs/plans/2026-11-23-o2-setup.md`." Rule 1's cap sentence gets "(billing waits for the first opening since O2)".

## Practices recorded in skills (landed in O2·12, versions bumped)
- **UI/UX** (`frata-design-language` § Feedback 4, 10.6.0): "A **setup popup** is the second kind of popup: one short
  job of up to five fields that saves at once, its values and `version` loaded when it opens, its code with
  `next/dynamic`. A job with a test step, a long form or many items stays a page (the product editor, Razorpay, GST, the
  website). A popup never opens another popup; ✕, Esc and Back close it; on a phone it is the sheet." Also § Graphic
  language names.
- **Architecture** (`frata-architecture` 3.11.0): a popup saves through `parseFields` + `SETUP_FIELDS` (server-named
  subsets), never a whole-section parse of a partial form; a definer that changes a plan names its refusals as
  `app_change_plan` does; billing starts at the first opening.
- **Performance**: a page's popup reads on open, never on the page (`reference/speed.md`, one line).
- **Testing** (`frata-verify`): a plan pinning a known hazard writes it as a test named "(flipped in <task>)" and flips
  it in the fixing commit.
