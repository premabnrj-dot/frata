## Design (simplest that fits)

### Engine
`discounts.ts`, pure, with the order of application unchanged:
1. product class: product and collection % or ₹ off, BXGY, free gift;
2. order class: % or ₹ off, tiers, first order;
3. thresholds;
4. payment.

- **Product % / ₹ off.** Targets are products or collections. Each eligible line gets `min(value per piece × qty, line)`, or
  `% × line` with an optional cap for the whole offer. The best product offer per line wins (a line takes one product
  offer).
- **BXGY.** "Buy X, get Y at P% off (100 = free)" from the targets. The cheapest Y pieces of every X+Y group are
  discounted, as their own line discount. Priced per line, never as a bundle price (Circular 92: two supplies).
- **Free gift.** "Above ₹N, this piece is free": the gift variant's line gets a 100% discount (up to one piece) when the
  bag (other lines) is at least N.
  - The bag progress line offers "Add your free {gift}" as a button.
  - The gift is never added by the server.
  - Its GST is 0 (taxable 0), per plan decision 2.
- **Tiers.** An order offer with up to 4 steps (`discount_tiers`: min, % or ₹). The highest step reached applies. The
  progress line names the next step.
- **First order.**
  - A flag on any order offer. The quote cannot know the phone, so the offer is listed with "for your first order" and
    applied in the quote.
  - Placement drops it when the phone has a standing order in this shop. The shopper sees the usual `changed`, the same
    answer as any limit (tenancy review).

### Data
Migration `0033_offers_more` and `0034_offers_more_access`.
- `discounts` gains:
  - `buy_qty`, `get_qty` (BXGY);
  - `gift_variant_id` (composite FK to variants, ON DELETE SET NULL, and the offer then stops applying);
  - `first_order` boolean;
  - `targets` (`all` | `products` | `collections`).
  - CHECKs per style.
- **`discount_targets`:** tenant_id, discount_id, product_id or collection_id (exactly one), with composite FKs (cascade
  when the product or collection is deleted).
- **`discount_tiers`:** tenant_id, discount_id, min_subtotal, percent_bps or amount_off; unique (discount, min).
- `discount_codes` gains:
  - `kind` (`shared` | `unique`);
  - `label` (the creator, at most 40 characters);
  - `link_only` boolean.
  - Unique codes are single use: a use counted by `code_id` through the same `discountUses` (by code).
- **Styles** gain `product_off`, `bxgy`, `free_gift`, with class `product`. `tiered` is an `order_off` with tiers.

### Codes
- **Unique codes.**
  - "Make N codes" (up to 5,000 a batch: plan limit O·4), 10 random characters (no I, O, 0, 1).
  - Downloaded as CSV once (a console download, not stored as a file).
  - A typed code is looked up by itself, on `(tenant_id, upper(code))`, inside the host's tenant. It is never listed.
- **Creator codes.**
  - A shared code with a label.
  - Its link is `https://<shop>/?code=CODE`.
  - `link_only` makes the code work only when it arrived by its link: the client sends `via: 'link'` when it captured the
    code from the URL. This is soft and forgeable, and the console says so ("stops copy-paste sites, not a determined
    shopper").
- The leak alert waits for O·3.

### Showcase
After themes T0 lands on main, through its section props: `badge` on the card, and `offers` in the PDP buy box.
- **Card badge:** the best product offer for that product ("10% off", "Buy 2 get 1", "Free gift above ₹2,999"), from the
  cached shop offers (`shopCached`). It is decided live at render (`offerStatus`). It never shows a price.
- **PDP block:** the product offer for this product, order offers with their minimum, and the pay-online saving, each
  with its code when listed.
- **Prior price (from 1 Jan 2027):**
  - a struck "was" price on a card or PDP is `min(price_history.price)` over the previous 30 days, when it is above
    today's price;
  - today's `compareAt` becomes "M.R.P. ₹X (incl. taxes)", never struck through;
  - this lands with the badge, inside the T0 sections, and the themes session is told.

### Console
- The style picker gains Product sale, Buy X get Y, Free gift, and Tiers (on % off the order); first order becomes a tick
  box.
- Targets come from a product and collection picker (the collections editor's search).
- On an offer's page, a **Codes** card:
  - the shared code;
  - make unique codes (CSV);
  - creator codes (label, link, link-only), each with its own uses and ₹ given.

## Plan review (architecture-reviewer, 2026-11-08): these override the sections above
1. **Per-line product discounts are stored, never re-spread.**
   - `order_lines.product_discount` (paise) is written at placement.
   - `reprice` takes it off each line first, then spreads the order and payment amounts over what is left.
   - `order_discounts` is unique on (order, discount_id), and `class` gains `'product'`.
   - `storedPricing`, `writeReprice` and the `order_lines` column grant change with it.
   - Int test first: an order with a product discount and a gift, switched to COD and captured, column by column.
2. **Code-only offers.**
   - `DiscountTerms` gains `byCode`: an offer reached only by a code.
   - `liveOffers` joins only the offer's public shared code.
   - A typed code is looked up separately (`codeFor(tx, tenant, typed)`), so a private code is never listed and a
     code-only offer never turns automatic.
3. **Migrations are 0039/0040 (journal 39/40) and tenancy check 35** (agreed with themes 2026-11-08: themes T1 took 0033/0034 and
   check 32, T2 takes 0035/0036 and check 33, T3 0037/0038 and check 34). Generate on top of main after merging what has landed.
4. **The gift FK is `on delete set null (gift_variant_id)`** (Postgres 15+), in the custom migration, with a verify case
   (`retireVariants` hard-deletes never-ordered sizes).
5. **YAGNI cuts.**
   - **Unique codes wait** for Messages campaigns, which will send them.
   - **`link_only` is cut,** because it can be forged. `order_discounts.via` (`typed` | `link` | `auto`) is recorded
     for O·3's leak alert instead.
   - **Creator codes stay:** a shared code with a label and its `?code=` link.
6. **First order.**
   - `pg_advisory_xact_lock(tenant:phone)` when a first-order offer could apply.
   - "Has ordered" = standing or holding, leaving out this order's own id.
   - The answer is the usual `changed`. It does reveal that the phone has ordered here, slowly (the place limit is
     5 an hour per phone): accepted.
7. **GST on Buy X get Y (decision 5).** The group's discount is spread over its X+Y pieces by value (one price for
   the group, per Circular 92), and each piece's rate is decided on its share. It never sits whole on the free piece.
   Tested at the slab.
8. **How product offers combine.** Greedy: the product offer with the largest total takes its lines, then the next,
   and so on. A line takes one product offer. Stable order, tested.
9. **Collection targets** reuse the collections' own matching (`collection-rules.ts` for rule collections,
   `collection_products` for hand-picked ones).
10. **Struck prices and MRP.**
    - Product offers never strike a price: a badge, and a line in the bag.
    - A struck "was" price is the prior price: the lowest of the price carried in at the start of the 30 days and every
      price within them, shown only when it is above today's price. This applies from now (no 1 Jan gate).
    - `compareAt` becomes the editor's "M.R.P. (printed on the tag)", shown as "M.R.P. ₹X incl. taxes", never struck,
      with a CHECK that price ≤ MRP.
    - "Save ₹N" is shown only against the prior price.
11. **The showcase** uses themes T2's props: a ProductGrid `badges` map into ProductCard `badge?: { label }` (shown only
    where no availability tag shows), and ProductView/BuyBox `offers?: { label, ends }[]`. Labels come from offer-text.ts. The themes session owns the
    `05-shop-look` baselines; O2·5 regenerates them on its own branch and says so.
12. **A tier** is copied into `order_discounts` as the step reached (min and value). Unit-economics review on Buy X
    get Y and the gift before O2·1 ships.
