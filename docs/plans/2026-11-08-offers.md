## Decisions taken for the owner (2026-11-08; revisit if a CA or counsel disagrees)
1. **GST on a discounted piece:**
   - The rate is decided per piece, on the value after the invoice discount, per payment method.
   - Grounds: CGST s.15(3)(a) excludes a discount given before or at supply and recorded on the invoice; the slab
     entries apply to "transaction value" (CBIC FAQ).
   - Each invoice line shows gross, discount and taxable value.
2. **Free gift (O·2):** its own invoice line at its price with a 100% line discount, so its taxable value is 0. Any
   ITC reversal on gifted stock is the merchant's books (s.17(5)(h)); the console says so when a gift offer is
   created.
3. **Free shipping:** the shipping charge is ₹0, not a discounted charge line, so there is nothing to tax.
4. **"E-commerce entity" for the 2027 self-audit:** Frrata designs the checkout and the offer surfaces, so Frrata
   holds itself to the dark-pattern rules for every shop.
   - The guardrails are built in, and merchants can't turn them off.
   - Frrata runs the yearly self-audit before 1 Jan 2027 and shows the certificate on its own site.
   - The merchant stays the seller.

## Design in code (from the spec, as it will be built)
- **Engine:** `src/domains/checkout/discounts.ts` (pure).
  - `DiscountTerms`:
    - `style`: `order_off` | `free_shipping` | `pay_online`; the class is derived from the style;
    - `percentBps` | `amountOff`, `cap`, `minSubtotal`;
    - `listed`, `combinesWithPayment`;
    - `code` (string | null).
  - `offerPrice(lines, terms[], code, rules)` returns, per method:
    - the line discounts (order share + payment share via `apportion`);
    - shipping after free-shipping offers;
    - the applied list (class, discount id, amount);
    - listed-but-locked offers with what's needed;
    - the code result: `applied` | `not_applied` | `beaten` (a valid code that saves less than the automatic offer;
      accepted leak, documented).
  - Order of application: order class → thresholds (shop free shipping, free-shipping offers, COD min/max) on the
    bag after the order discount → payment class (prepaid only).
- **`quote()`:**
  - Takes `offers: DiscountTerms[]` and `code`.
  - `MethodTotal` gains `discount`, `lines: { discount, taxable, gstBps, gst }[]` (sold lines, in order),
    `merchandiseNet` and `applied`.
  - The GST rate for each line is tested on the discounted value per piece.
  - `QuoteLine`'s tax fields and top-level `merchandiseNet` are removed. Every reader moves to the method's figures,
    so there is no second copy.
- **Repricer:** `reprice(stored, method)` in `src/domains/orders/amounts.ts` (pure).
  - `stored` holds each line's gross, qty and HSN, the order's applied discounts (class, amount, terms), shipping,
    whether prices include GST, and the shop's COD charge.
  - It returns the lines' discount, taxable value, rate, GST and charge shares, plus the order's discount, GST total
    and total.
  - It replaces `charged()` and is used by switching to COD, marking paid after a switch, and retrying a payment.
- **Data** (one migration, `drizzle/0031_offers.sql`, plus its `--custom` RLS part):
  - **`discounts`:**
    - id, tenant_id, title, style, percent_bps, amount_off, cap, min_subtotal;
    - limit_total, limit_per_phone, budget;
    - starts_at, ends_at, started_at (null = draft), paused_at, ended_at;
    - listed, combines_with_payment, announce;
    - created_by, created_at.
    - Unique `(tenant_id, id)` for composite FKs. Partial unique `(tenant_id) where announce and ended_at is null`.
    - CHECKs: exactly one of percent or amount for `order_off`/`pay_online`, neither for `free_shipping`;
      `ends_at > starts_at`; amounts ≥ 0.
  - **`discount_codes`:**
    - id, tenant_id, discount_id, code, created_at.
    - Unique `(tenant_id, upper(code))` forever. FK `(tenant_id, discount_id)`. CHECK `code ~ '^[A-Za-z0-9]{4,20}$'`.
  - **`order_discounts`:**
    - id, tenant_id, order_id, discount_id, code_id, class, amount;
    - the terms copied: style, percent_bps, amount_off, cap, min_subtotal;
    - created_at.
    - FKs `(tenant_id, order_id)` and `(tenant_id, discount_id)`. Index `(tenant_id, discount_id)`. Unique
      `(tenant_id, order_id, class)`.
    - The payment row stays after a switch to COD: whether it applies is derived from `currentMethod`.
  - **`orders`:**
    - `discount` bigint default 0;
    - `online_total` bigint null, backfilled from `checkout->>'onlineTotal'`;
    - CHECK `orders_total` becomes `total = subtotal − discount + shipping + cod_charge (+ gst)`, plus
      `0 ≤ discount ≤ subtotal`.
  - **`order_lines.line_discount`** is written.
  - **`price_history`:**
    - id, tenant_id, variant_id, price, from_at.
    - Written by an AFTER INSERT/UPDATE OF price trigger on `variants` (SECURITY INVOKER, tenant_id copied from the
      row), and backfilled with today's prices.
    - Display (prior price, MRP) waits for O·2, before 1 Jan 2027.
  - **Grants:** `frata_app` gets select/insert/update on `discounts` and `discount_codes`, and select/insert on
    `order_discounts` and `price_history`. No delete anywhere. Each table gets RLS and its policy, and a row in
    `scripts/verify/tenancy.sql`.
- **Uses:** counted at placement only, for offers with a limit or budget, under `SELECT … FOR UPDATE` on those
  `discounts` rows (after the variants, in id order).
  - A use is an `order_discounts` row whose order is standing and not abandoned:
    `stateSql not in ('cancelled','payment_abandoned')`.
  - For the payment class, it must also be currently paid online or awaiting payment.
  - Per phone: the same, joined on `orders.phone`, inside the tenant.
  - Budget: Σ amount of those uses + this order's amount ≤ budget. Otherwise the offer drops out and the shopper gets
    `changed`.
- **Codes:**
  - The quote API takes `code` (Zod `^[A-Za-z0-9]{1,20}$`).
  - A code that doesn't apply counts against `codeIp` (`<tenant>:<ip>`, 20 / 10 min) and `codeShop` (`<tenant>`,
    600 / 10 min), fail-closed. Once over the limit, every code reads `not_applied`.
  - `requestHashOf` includes the code.
- **ADR-0003:** `publicQuote` adds only these:
  - `offers.applied` [{label, amount}] per method;
  - `offers.listed` [{label, needs}];
  - `offers.code`;
  - `discount` per method.

  No budget, use count, limit or id is sent. `check-client-bundle` adds `budget`, `limit_total`, `limit_per_phone`,
  `limitTotal`, `limitPerPhone`.
