## Plan review (architecture-reviewer, 2026-11-08): these override the tables below
0. **Process:** the freight rule and the S·0 schema were drafted before this review landed; they are reworked to it
   and committed only after (task S0·2/S0·1), never as written first.
1. **S0·0 is its own commit:** e2e 49 (S·0 steps), int baselines for `queueCounts`, `offerStats` ₹ kept and the
   shopper view's states; the "a packed order leaves every work tab" step flips in S0·5.
2. **Freight:** `forward_shipping` drops its default and every existing 0 becomes null (no writer ever set it), tested
   from the migration before it. `freightOf` chooses by the shipment's provider: `self` (and orders with no shipment)
   use the confirmed estimate; `shiprocket` uses the statement's actual lines, or **pending** (never ₹0) while a line it
   needs (forward, or return on an RTO) has not arrived.
3. **Counts:** `inFlightSql` (packed, no outcome, not cancelled) matches `orders_inflight_idx`; counts read the work
   tabs over `openSql()` and the shipping tabs over `inFlightSql()`; the parity test covers the four tabs and the
   counts; `enteredAt`/`enteredAtSql` gain the shipping tabs (oldest first: packed, shipped, the latest failed
   attempt, the return started).
4. **Undo of an outcome** is guarded by the roles of the action it undoes (SHIPPERS for Shipped, CAN_MANAGE for
   Delivered, Came back, Lost), whatever route calls it, with a test. "Anything later" ignores notes, edits and prints.
5. **Shipments** store only their own endings: `ended_at`, `ended_reason` in (cancelled, lost, undone). Undoing Shipped
   ends the `self` shipment (reason undone), so the order can be shipped again and core Cancel works again. Hand-typed
   tracking numbers are never unique (S·1's AWB index is partial to Shiprocket).
6. **Came back** writes `rto_initiated_at` and `rto_delivered_at` together (it is back at the shop) and offers "Put
   stock back", through `stock_taken_at` as Cancel does, tested. **Lost by the courier** (CAN_MANAGE, on a shipped
   order) ends the shipment as lost; `billableDelivered` never bills it. Its SQL twin lands with its first reader
   (billing, Phase 6), marked KNOWN COPY until then.
7. **No catalogue refresh on a delivery:** Best sellers already revalidate hourly (themes review); the writer refreshes
   nothing.
8. **The three costs are their own Settings section,** editable by CAN_MANAGE only (Shipping's editors include
   partners); their fields join the client-bundle sentinels' review (they are the merchant's own estimates, not a
   per-order cost, and never reach the storefront).
9. **One IST month:** the undo window and `billableDelivered` live in `src/domains/orders/outcomes.ts` and read
   `src/domains/time/month.ts`; `freight.ts` keeps only `freightOf`.
10. **Event kinds widened once:** shipped, delivered, rto, lost, ndr, shipment_cancelled (S·1's included).
11. **The packed order's primary action in S·0** stays Print slip; Mark shipped sits beside it (SHIPPERS). A shipped
   order's primary is Delivered for managers only; staff see no cost figure (the COD amount to collect stays).
12. **Moved:** the default box to S1·3 (no S·0 reader); the Couldn't deliver Today tile to S1·4 (nothing sets `ndr_at`
   before S·1, so the tab stays empty in S·0, which is said); adopting panel-shipped orders out of S1·4 into its own
   task; `shipment_charges` gets its migration and tenancy check in S3·2; S·2 and S·3 each end with a close task.
