## Goal
North star (5) a premium-looking storefront, and (4) mobile conversion / order value: a product page that tells the
piece's story (annotated photos, "1 / 6") and offers two add-ons from the page. Measure: add-to-bag rate and items per
order on Signal product pages with stories or pairings vs without, after 30 days (Analytics).

## What a merchant does (board "Product editor")
Products › a product, two cards under Details (shown when the shop's live or draft look offers them):
- **Stories** (up to 6): each a photo (upload, or one of this product's photos), a title (≤ 60), text (≤ 300) and up to
  3 numbered notes (≤ 60 each); move up/down, remove; empty: "Add a story".
- **Pair it with** (up to 2): find a piece by name; each shows photo, price, stock; remove. Empty: "Pick up to 2 pieces".

## What a shopper sees (board "Product page", approved)
- **Stories**: with the stacked gallery (Signal's), stacked under the photos on the tile — title, photo, numbered notes;
  with another gallery, a row of slides, each saying "n / N", prev/next as links to the slide (no script).
- **Pair it with**: under Add to bag, two rows (photo, title · price, **Add**). Add puts the piece in the bag when it has
  one buyable variant; a piece with sizes says **Choose** and opens its page. Sold out or unlisted: not shown.

## Design
- **Storage — on the product, not a new table (KISS; spec said a table):** `products.stories jsonb not null default '[]'`
  (Zod: ≤ 6 × {image, title ≤ 60, text ≤ 300, notes ≤ 3 × ≤ 60}, ≤ 6 kB) like `products.label`, and
  `products.pair_with uuid[] not null default '{}'` (≤ 2). Covered by the products row policy already; tenancy.sql
  proves a pair id from another shop is refused (a definer-free check in the save: the ids must be this shop's
  products, not this one) and that the column grant is the products one. One migration (`0085`), CHECKs for the
  sizes.
- **Photos:** a story photo is one of this product's photos or an upload into the shop's `products` folder (the existing
  upload token); the save rebuilds it with `acceptedBase` (the photo check, same as the look's).
- **Reads:** stories ride in the product's cached entry (`coreProduct`; +6 kB worst case, the entry stays small); pair
  ids resolve against the catalogue the product page already loads (no new query), listed and buyable only.
- **Theme gate:** `allowed.productStories` and `allowed.pairWith` (Signal 1.3.0); other themes and older Signal versions
  show neither (the data stays). The editor shows the two cards when the live or draft look offers them.
- **Budgets:** the product page is at 120.5 of 121 kB JS. Stories are server-drawn (0 JS); "Add" reuses the buy box's
  bag call (≤ 0.3 kB in the buy box chunk), measured; if it doesn't fit, Add becomes a link to the piece.
- **Accessibility:** stories are a list of figures (photo + figcaption); notes an ordered list; slides a list with
  "n of N" text and prev/next links; Add buttons named "Add Strap Tag to the bag".

## Out of scope
A compare table across products, Colours/Compare tabs (cut in S1), stories on other themes (they come with a version
that offers them), a bundle price for a pairing (an offer, not this).
