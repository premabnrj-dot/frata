# Plan: Signal S3 — product stories and "Pair it with" (Signal 1.3.0)
Status: active (reviews folded in 2026-11-09) · Date: 2026-11-09 · Spec: docs/specs/2026-11-09-theme-signal.md § S3 ·
Mockup: https://claude.ai/artifact/9XNT3KFJziXEyaKyDa48dr — "Product page" (shop, approved v3) and **"Product editor"
(approved by the owner 2026-11-09, v5)** · Reviews: architecture-reviewer, tenancy-auditor (2026-11-09)

## Goal
North star (5) a premium storefront and (4) order value: the piece's story on its page (annotated photos, "1 / 6") and
two add-ons from the page. Measure (analytics has no add-to-bag event): on Signal 1.3 shops, the share of orders holding
a piece and one of its pairings, and product-page-to-order rate on pieces with stories vs without, after 30 days.

## Owner decisions (2026-11-09)
- The "Product editor" board is approved as drawn (v5: states, phone, icons as controls).
- **Stories live on the product** (`products.stories`, like `label`), not in a `product_stories` table; the spec is
  updated. **Pairs get their own table** (`product_pairs`) so the database refuses another shop's product.

## Decisions taken from the reviews
- **Pairs resolve inside `coreProduct`** (arch B1): one select of ≤ 2 paired products with their variants and first
  photo, in the existing `Promise.all` — never the 880 kB catalogue. Raw status and `publishAt` kept; listed and
  buyable decided at render (a scheduled drop goes live by the clock). The entry is tagged `catalogue`, so a paired
  piece's price change (`refreshShop`) or sell-out (`refreshSoldOut`) purges this page too. Preview reads the same way
  and still shows only listed pieces. Cache key `product` → `product-v2` (the shape changes).
- **`product_pairs`** (tenancy M1, arch 3): (tenant_id, product_id, paired_id, position), composite FKs on
  (tenant_id, product_id) and (tenant_id, paired_id) → products (tenant_id, id) with cascade, CHECK paired ≠ product,
  unique (tenant_id, product_id, paired_id), ≤ 2 per product (checked in the save, under the product's version check);
  grant, revoke, RLS, policy, tenancy.sql §20b (A can't see or write B's pairs; a pair naming B's product raises a
  foreign-key violation; self raises; the proof fails when the FK is dropped on purpose).
- **The save checks pairs like collections** (tenancy M2): uuids only, deduplicated, ≤ 2, never itself, selected with an
  explicit `tenant_id` and the row count must equal the id count; one error for "not yours" and "doesn't exist".
- **The piece search** (tenancy M3, arch 7): a reader in `src/lib/queries/catalogue.ts`, tenant from `requireMember()`,
  `withTenant` with an explicit `tenant_id`, query ≤ 60 characters, `limit 8`, ILIKE on the title (fine at today's
  catalogue sizes; P10), drafts excluded, this product excluded; the collection editor's picker reused.
- **Stories' photos** (arch B2, tenancy L): stored as `{base, widths, width, height, alt, decorative}`; each rebuilt with
  `acceptedBase(…, tenantId, 'products')`, widths from `WIDTHS`, uploads WebP-checked, a picked product photo checked
  against this product's images. `editPhoto`'s in-use check also reads `products.stories`, so deleting a product photo
  a story uses keeps its files. An uploaded story photo whose story is removed or never saved is left in the folder
  (like the look's), never deleted without re-checking `acceptedBase`.
- **Size caps** (arch 4): Zod counts characters and is the limit (≤ 6 stories; title ≤ 60, text ≤ 300, ≤ 3 notes ≤ 60);
  the database CHECK is a looser backstop (`jsonb_typeof = 'array'`, length ≤ 6, `octet_length(stories::text) < 16384`).
  A test saves the largest valid Hindi stories.
- **Zero JS for stories, a tiny island for Add** (arch 5): stories and the pair rows are server-drawn and passed into
  `BuyBox` as ReactNode slots (`belowGallery`, `belowBuy`); Add is its own small island (`bag.add` + `openBag`), measured
  against the 121 kB ceiling.
- **Add only with no options** (arch 6): a piece with no options (one variant) and buyable says **Add**; anything else
  **Choose** (its page) — a sized piece is never put in the bag without its size. Price through `priceOf`/`offerFor`.
- **Modules** (arch 7): Zod in `src/domains/catalogue/stories.ts` (pure), called by `cleanProduct` like `label`; pair and
  photo checks in `src/lib/catalogue/extras.ts`; `actions.ts` (390 lines) gains only the calls.
- **Editor cards** (arch 9): shown when the live or draft look offers them OR the product already has stories or pairs
  (so they can always be removed); "offers" decided by one function in `src/lib/queries/look.ts`, read inside
  `productForEdit`'s transaction. A support visit's writes are audited through `asMember` (ADR-0007).
- **Slides** (arch 11): a scroll-snap row (no fragment links: no history entries); "n / N" text on each slide; one story
  shows no counter or controls; none renders nothing. Stacked under the gallery with Signal's `stack` gallery.
