## Design
- **S2-0 Room first: the shop's own stylesheet.** The shop loads the whole app's Tailwind (console included) and sits
  at its 22.5 kB ceiling; the speed review measured a shop-only build at ~13–14 kB. Split: `globals.css` keeps tokens
  and the shop's rules with `@source` limited to `src/components/storefront`, `src/app/s`, `src/domains/themes`;
  a `console.css` with the console's sources is imported by the console, admin, partner and public layouts; the look
  preview (`(lookpreview)`) loads both. Budget reset to the measured value + 1 kB. Verify: every e2e flow (the console
  must look the same: its screenshots), shop-look 0 px.
- **Sections** (in the section library, offered only by Signal 1.2.0 via `allowed.sections`; older themes never render
  them — the T1 narrowing rule; `made` lists them for Signal):
  - `spotlight` {label ≤ 24, heading ≤ 60, text ≤ 200, link {label ≤ 32, shop path}|null, image|null, video?, side
    left|right}: tile background, text column .8fr, media 1.4fr (16/10), phone stacked. Video as V1 (allowed.videoSections
    gains `spotlight`); the photo or the loop's first frame is the first paint.
  - `story_fan` {heading ≤ 60, text ≤ 160, cards 3–7 [{image, title ≤ 40, link shop path|null}]}: the middle card
    forward, the rest fanned (CSS transforms on a list; no script); phone: a snap row; reduced motion: side by side,
    still; a card with a link is one link (its title the name).
  - `statement` {eyebrow ≤ 24, wordmark ≤ 32, image}: full-bleed photo under a dark scrim, eyebrow in the grotesk,
    wordmark in **Caveat 700** (OFL, self-hosted, latin, one weight, ≤ 25 kB, `preload: false`: fetched only on a page
    that renders it), rotated −6°; `aria-hidden` rotation only (the text stays text).
- **S2-3 Type scale:** under `data-voice="grotesk"`, section h2s `clamp(1.375rem, 2.4vw, 2.125rem)`, 600, −0.02em,
  `text-wrap: balance`. A merchant's own text styles (Site › Text styles) still win (same rule as S1).
- **S2-4 Signal 1.2.0's home:** hero (with "Shop now" → /products), spotlight, bestsellers (tabs), new arrivals (rail),
  band, story fan, statement, collection tiles — the board's order. Samples fill the photos in the preview only.
- **Readiness:** spotlight needs a photo or a video; story fan 3+ cards each with a photo (alt or decorative);
  statement a photo and a wordmark. `cleanImages` (exhaustive switch) covers every new photo.
- **Customizer:** three forms (fields above; cards add/move/remove, ≤ 7), library entries with blurbs and tags,
  "Made for Signal".
