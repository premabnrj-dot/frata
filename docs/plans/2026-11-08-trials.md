## Regression net first (rule 5)
Before any change: `e2e/flows/62-billing.mjs` (plan, estimate, issue, autopay, plan change), the sign-up flow
(`e2e/flows/*start*`, creating a shop), the storefront checkout flow, partner handover; unit: `bill.test.ts`,
`entitlements.test.ts`; int: `draft.int.test.ts`, `plan-change.int.test.ts`; proof: `scripts/verify/tenancy.sql`.

## The data (one stored fact each)
| Fact | Where | Written by |
|---|---|---|
| The platform's trial terms for new sign-ups: `days` (7–90), `open_window_days` (0–30), who/when/why | `trial_settings` (one row, platform data, no tenant_id) | `app_admin_set_trial_settings` (frata_admin, ADMIN_ONLY, audited) |
| One shop's trial terms, frozen at sign-up: plan chosen, `days`, `open_window_days`, `signed_up_at`, `started_at` (open or handover), `extended_at`, `ended_by_staff_at` | `shop_trials` (tenant_id PK; RLS: the shop reads its own; written by definers only) | `app_create_shop` (self-serve), Admin create, `app_admin_set_shop_trial_days` (before opening only) |
| When the trial ends — **the one fact billing reads** | `tenant_plans.trial_ends_at` (exists) | provisional at sign-up = `signed_up_at + window + days`; set to `least(started_at + days, signed_up_at + window + days)` when the clock starts; + 7 days on the one extension |
| One trial per business | `trial_claims` (kind, salted sha256 of email / phone / GSTIN / payout account; unique) | definers; no grant to any app role |
Derived, never stored: `trialState` (`building | trial | ending | grace | paused | paid | none`), days left, grace end
(= `trial_ends_at` + 7 d), eligibility to extend, the paused shop. `src/domains/plans/trial.ts`, pure.

## Tasks

### TR0 — engine and data (no visible change for a brand)
1. **`src/domains/plans/trial.ts`**: `trialTerms(setting)` validation (7–90, 0–30), `trialEndsAt(terms, signedUpAt,
   startedAt)`, `trialState(now, …)`, `daysLeft`, `mayExtend(delivered, extendedAt)`, `isPaused`. Unit tests on a calendar
   of cases (month ends in IST, 90-day trial over a year end, the cap, the extension, grace). *Verify:* `npx vitest run
   src/domains/plans`.
2. **Migration (generated + custom access)**: `trial_settings` (seeded 30/15), `shop_trials`, `trial_claims`; RLS,
   grants, policies in the same migration; definers `app_admin_set_trial_settings`, `app_admin_set_shop_trial_days`,
   `app_trial_start_clock` (trigger on `shop_settings.published_at` first set, and in `app_accept_handover` for partner
   shops: the clock starts at the LATER of opening and handover), `app_trial_extend` (owner/admin member, once, only
   when `mayExtend`), `app_admin_extend_trial` / `app_admin_end_trial` (frata_admin, reason, audited),
   `app_trial_claim(kind, hash)` (refuses a second). Proof rows 45–47: a shop reads only its own trial; no app role
   reads claims or writes settings; the clock starts once; an extension once. *Verify:* `npm run verify:tenancy`.
3. **Billing — the fee waiver (Q1), in the one rule**: `computeBill` waives the fee on orders whose `rateAt` (shipped)
   falls inside the trial of the plan row in force, up to that plan's `includedOrders` (counted per trial, in shipping
   order, across invoices: the draft passes how many were already waived, read from `frrata_invoice_fees` with
   `fee = 0` and a new `waived` flag). `feeBucketSql` (₹ kept) takes the same waiver so Analytics shows ₹0 Frrata fee
   for those orders. Unit + int tests: 151st order in a Launch trial is charged; an order shipped on the trial's last
   day and delivered after is waived. *Verify:* `npx vitest run src/domains/billing`, `draft.int.test.ts`.
4. **Admin**: a "Trial" column on Merchants (state, days left) and the Settings › Trials form (mockup § 7). *Verify:*
   `npm test`, typecheck; e2e step in `62-billing.mjs` (Admin saves 90 days; a new sign-up shows 90).

## Review findings (each binding on the tasks above; they override a task where they differ)

### tenancy-auditor (2026-11-08) — all accepted, fixed in the plan
- **T1 CRITICAL — no oracle.** At sign-up only VERIFIED keys are claimed: the email from `getUser()` and the phone from
  the stored code record. The GSTIN is claimed only once verified (GST check) and the payout account at Razorpay KYC; a
  later match ends the trial with a notice. One generic message whichever key matched ("This business has had a free
  trial before"), rate-limited per IP and per user. Never a GSTIN typed in a form.
- **T2 HIGH — `app_trial_claim` is not granted to any app role.** Claims are made only inside `app_create_shop`,
  `app_accept_handover` and the payments-connect definer, from verified sources.
- **T3 HIGH — extend checks `members`, not the reported role** (a support session reports as owner): the 0058 pattern —
  `app_current_tenant()` + a `members` row with role owner/admin; the definer counts delivered orders itself (never a
  parameter), locks the tenant, requires `extended_at is null`, writes the audit row.
- **T4 HIGH — opening twice.** `closeShop` nulls `published_at`, so the clock trigger fires only when
  `shop_trials.started_at is null`, and uses `now()`, not `new.published_at`. Proof: close, reopen → clock unchanged.
- **T5 HIGH — a scheduled downgrade must not stretch the trial.** Starting the clock, extending and ending update the
  trial end on EVERY current and future `tenant_plans` row of the tenant (`app_change_plan` copies it into the future
  row). Proof row.
- **T6 HIGH — the expiry note's "take 7 more days" opens Plan and billing**; the tenant comes from `requireMember`;
  never a token or id in the link.
- **T7 HIGH — partner shops:** the claim is made in `app_accept_handover` with the new owner's verified keys and the
  invite's tenant; `signed_up_at` is the build time, so the open window counts from then.
- **T8 MEDIUM** — every new table and definer revoked from `service_role` too; `trial_claims`: an Admin-only policy,
  no app grant; `trial_settings`: `OPEN_FOR_APP` with its reason (platform terms, no tenant data); `shop_trials`:
  select only + a `tenant_rows` policy, no `is null` branch.
- **T9 MEDIUM** — Admin definers take the staff uid and check `platform_staff.role` (ADMIN_ONLY) themselves, write their
  own audit row, executable by `frata_admin` only.
- **T10 MEDIUM** — the daily run lists tenant ids through a definer (S4), then works per shop under `withTenant`.
- **T11 MEDIUM** — a support visit may not open or close a shop during a trial (refused, as plan changes are).
- **T12 LOW** — claim keys are HMAC-SHA256 with a server secret and a key version (rotation keeps uniqueness).
- **T13** — "paused" is decided at render, outside the cache, and checkout checks it again, uncached, inside the order
  transaction. Proof rows: support visit refused on extend; reopen; scheduled-downgrade row; `frata_app` cannot run the
  claim; the handover claim; a second sign-up with the same email.

### unit-economics-reviewer (2026-11-08)
Verdict: helps the merchant; neutral for Frrata at 30 days; at 90 days it costs Frrata on Launch and small shops, and
on Growth/Scale shops with a high order value, unless the waived fee has a ₹ cap. Figures (repo engine, illustrative
shop shapes): forgone revenue per converted shop at 25% conversion — Launch 30 d ₹7,671 (payback 2.6 months), Launch
90 d small shop ₹11,451 (6.5 months), Growth 90 d ₹38,799 (2.1), Scale 90 d festive at ₹2,500 per order ₹1,14,811
(2.5). Cash cost per trial ₹1,047–1,829, nearly all the ₹1,000 onboarding placeholder. The spec's "₹800 per converted
trial" left out the plan price and the trials that never convert: corrected to ₹6,437 on its own small shop.
- **U1 — PROPOSED, AWAITING THE OWNER (changes Q1):** a ₹ cap per trial on the waived fee, the same whatever the trial
  length: included orders × a ₹1,000 reference order × the rate = **₹3,000 Launch / ₹15,000 Growth / ₹25,000 Scale**.
  Typical shops keep almost all of it; a Scale festive trial at ₹2,500 a order goes from ₹62,500 waived to ₹25,000.
  Until answered, task 3 builds the cap as a setting beside the trial length, off by default.
- **U2 accepted:** the allowance is **per trial, not per month**; one counter across a downgrade, at the plan the
  order shipped under; returned parcels (RTO) never use it up.
- **U3 accepted:** the default stays 30 days; longer is the owner's setting, and Admin › Settings › Trials shows the
  payback note beside it. Cheapest test: alternate 30 and 90 days by weekly cohort (TR3 measures contribution per
  sign-up).
- **U4 accepted:** Admin's extension has the same "fewer than 3 delivered orders" guard as the owner's, or a written
  reason; the free window is at most length + 7 (extension) + 7 (grace).
- **U5 accepted:** the hypotheses go into frata-unit-economics with their tests (trial → paid 25%, 10–15% fallback;
  cash cost per trial; 40 delivered orders in 30 days; waived revenue is incremental; 90 converts no better than 30;
  festive clustering; Growth default holds; grace bad debt). The onboarding minutes of the first 20 trials replace
  the ₹1,000 placeholder.
