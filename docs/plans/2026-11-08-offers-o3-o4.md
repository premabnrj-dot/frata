## O·3 · Did it make money? (derived only: no new table, no stored number)
- **One read:** `offerStats(tenantId, now, days = 30)` in `src/lib/queries/discounts.ts`. Everything is derived from `orders` +
  `order_discounts` through the existing predicates (`discountUseSql`, `stateSql`) and `orderProfit`.
- **Per offer:**
  - orders;
  - ₹ given;
  - AOV with the offer, against the shop's orders without one in the same window;
  - prepaid share;
  - new vs repeat buyers (a phone with an earlier standing order in this shop);
  - delivered and RTO counts, and the RTO rate against non-offer orders;
  - ₹ kept per delivered order (`orderProfit`, known costs only);
  - ₹ given per delivered order.
- **Per creator code:** orders, delivered, RTO, ₹ given, and the share that arrived by link.
- **Leak alert (one rule, pure, `src/domains/checkout/leak.ts`).** A creator code is flagged "leak?" when either:
  - (a) of at least 10 uses in the last 7 days, under half arrived through its link (`via = 'link'`); or
  - (b) its last 7 days run at more than 3× its average 7 days over the previous 28, with at least 10 uses.

  The alert says which. It never acts by itself: the merchant can end the offer or remove the code (codes can't be
  deleted; ending the offer is the remedy, so the alert says so).
- **Console:**
  - Offers list: a 30-day strip with ₹ given, the prepaid share of offer orders, AOV with/without, and ₹ given per
    delivered order.
  - An offer's page: the numbers above, and creator codes ranked by delivered orders, each with its RTO and leak flag.
  - "Too few to judge" under 30 orders.
- **Tests:**
  - `leak.ts` unit tests;
  - `offerStats` int test on a real Postgres (orders in each state, a code by link and typed);
  - e2e 47 sees the strip.

## O·4 · Admin controls
- **Kill switch.**
  - `discounts.stopped_at`, `stopped_by` and `stop_reason` (a CHECK keeps the three together). Migration `0041` and custom
    `0042` (next free numbers on main at the time).
  - `frata_admin` gets column-level SELECT on (`id`, `tenant_id`, `title`, `style`, `started_at`, `ended_at`,
    `stopped_at`, `stop_reason`) and UPDATE on (`stopped_at`, `stopped_by`, `stop_reason`) only. It gets nothing on
    `order_discounts`, codes, targets or tiers.
  - `liveDiscountSql` and `offerStatus` treat stopped as not live: a new status, `stopped`, shown in the console with
    Frrata's reason.
  - The shop cannot clear it: `stopped_*` is not in `frata_app`'s update grant.
  - Admin actions `stopOffer` / `resumeOffer` go through `asStaff`, are audited (activity log), and refresh the shop
    (`refreshShop` via the admin's revalidate path).
- **Platform totals:** `app_admin_offer_totals(p_since)`.
  - A SECURITY DEFINER function, `search_path ''`, EXECUTE only to `frata_admin`. It returns per shop: live offers, orders
    with an offer since `p_since`, and ₹ given. Aggregates only, never a shopper.
  - The Admin › Offers page lists shops by ₹ given, with a link to each shop's offers (the kill switch).
- **Limits:** at most 20 live (started, unended, unstopped) offers per shop, and at most 4 tiers (already) and 200 targets
  (already). The 20 is a platform constant in `offer-form.ts`, checked by `stepOffer('start')` under the shop's offers
  lock. The signal to make it a per-plan setting: a merchant needing more than 20.
- **Templates:** a static `OFFER_TEMPLATES` list per business type (spec table "Business types"), shown as "Popular for
  shops like yours" on New offer. Pre-fills the form, nothing stored. Admin-edited templates wait until a template needs
  changing without a deploy.
- **Guardrails:** already enforced by the engine and form (real ends only, one answer for codes, no games, prior price
  derived). Admin has no switch to loosen them, by design.
- **Tests:**
  - `verify:tenancy` check 36: `frata_admin` sees only the named columns, can only set `stopped_*`, can't read
    `order_discounts`, and the definer returns aggregates only;
  - int test: a stopped offer drops out of the quote and the bar;
  - e2e: Admin stops an offer with a reason, the shop's console shows it stopped, the bag no longer gets it.
