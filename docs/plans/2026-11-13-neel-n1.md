# Neel N1 — the shell, the page anatomy and the list screens
Status: built, awaiting ship (reviews folded in, 2026-11-13) · Date: 2026-11-13 ·
Spec: `docs/specs/2026-11-13-design-language-neel.md` · Mockup: `docs/mockups/2026-11-13-design-language-neel.html`
(§ Layout, § Screens: Home, Orders, Products, Settings, On a phone) · Approved: owner, 2026-11-13 ("continue with N1")

## Goal
Ranked problems 3 (catalogue operations) and 1 (COD/RTO: the queue is where risk is acted on). Every console page sits
in the drawn shell and opens the drawn way; Home, Orders, Products and Settings are built as drawn. Measure: time to the
first confirm from Home; parity on each screen at 1280 and 390.

## What moved out (the reviews, 2026-11-13)
**Finding (§ Finding) is its own phase, N1c**, planned on the speed review's measurements: search through a light route
with `history.replaceState` (no page render per key), capped totals ("50+"), last-4 phone digits as an indexed column,
AWB by an exact `(tenant_id, tracking_number)` index, filter counts within the tab in a savepoint with a timeout,
`saved_views` with its tenancy proof, the phone filter sheet and quick look (a lean peek query, no costs). Risk, Source
and order tags have no SQL form or column today: their scope is an owner call asked when N1c starts. ⌘K is N1b.
So in N1 the Orders and Products screens keep today's search and tabs; the drawn filter chips arrive in N1c (Deviations).

## Regression net first
`npm test`, typecheck and the full e2e suite before and after (it walks every page: 90-ux clicks `aside a[href]` and
Sign out, so rail links keep accessible names). Flows that read a title or click the sidebar move to the new markup
in the same commit, never weakened. `05-shop-look`: console baselines retaken and read, store pages 0 px. New flow
`82-neel-shell`: the sidebar at 1280, the rail at 1024 and by choice, the bottom bar at 390, PageHeader's order, each
screen's drawn values, axe.

## Order of work
**N1·1 — The shell** (§ Layout · Sidebar)
1. Sidebar 232 px from 1280 (`--side` g25, a 1 px line): the store's name and its address under it (from what the
   layout already reads: `mainHostFor`/`setupFor`, no new query), groups in 11/16 capitals, items 34 px high with 8 px
   corners in text 2, hover g50, the current one white with e1 and a line edge, its icon neel; counts as pills.
2. A 64 px icon rail from 768 to 1279 by CSS, and at any width by choice ("[" and the button at its foot): a cookie
   `rail=1` (only '1' or '0' read; path /, read by the layout and handed down as a plain value, check:tenant §7), set from
   the browser without a server round trip. On the rail each link keeps its accessible name (an sr-only label and a tooltip
   on the bar), a count becomes a dot with its words for a screen reader ("43 to confirm"). "[" joins `SHORTCUTS` and the
   KeySheet. Admin and the partner workspace take the same widths and rail (their nav, their ink frame kept).
3. The page area: padding 28 · 32 at 1280, 24 from 768, 16 on a phone; shapes as a prop of the page (`list` fluid to
   1440 · `editor` 1120 · `settings` 760); 24 between sections, 32 between groups.
4. The phone: body 15/22 and titles 20/26 in `[data-neel]` (console, Admin, partner, look editor alike); the bottom bar
   (Home, Orders, Products, More), the active tab in neel; More as the Dialog sheet.

**N1·2 — The page header** (§ Layout · Header and breadcrumbs)
5. `PageHeader` as drawn, one component for all 66 pages: a breadcrumb above (two levels at most; `back` maps onto it;
   a phone shows "‹ Products"), the title 22/28 (20/26 on a phone) wrapping to two lines then "…" with the full name on
   hover, its status chip, a meta line of facts, then the actions at the right: More, secondaries, the one primary last.
   `titleSlot` kept. The header scrolls away; a list's tabs stick under the shell's top edge.

**N1·3 — The screens** (§ Screens), each with its parity review
6. **Home**: "Today" with the day ("Friday, 13 November"), Store open and Share store at the right; three count tiles
   (To confirm, To pack, On hold, 30/36 figures, "Oldest 8 d" or "Nothing waiting"); "Next to confirm", five rows with
   Confirm and "All 43" (today.ts `.limit(5)`, risk from the same map: 0 new statements); "Last 7 days" as bars (count
   and ₹ written; today in `--chart-now`) from the existing week read. `todaySummary` and `shareFor` in one
   `Promise.all`. The pre-open checklist and a failed summary keep their states.
7. **Orders**: the tabs in the drawn order, the search, the columns Order · Customer · Payment · Status · Total ·
   Placed (48 px, the name over "Madurai · 3 items"), risk as a chip beside the status, the bulk bar in neel (Confirm N,
   Put on hold, Clear), the pager; on a phone, two-line rows as drawn. Tab words as drawn (Couldn't deliver, To confirm,
   To pack, To ship, On the way, Coming back, All). Speed: counts, list, shop in one `Promise.all` inside one
   transaction, risk after (≤ 11 statements, ≤ 4 serial trips).
8. **Products**: photo rows 56 px, status pills, "Low · 2" and "Made to order" as drawn, the bulk bar in neel (Set
   status, Add to collection, Archive, Delete); `manualCollections` loaded when "Add to collection" opens (≤ 9 statements,
   one transaction).
9. **Settings**: one list, each row its current value, no icon tiles, 760 wide; "Ready for live payments" a chip in the
   header.

## Design review
- **UI/UX:** every screen as drawn; states empty (Empty/NoMatch), loading (Skeleton first, the top line after), error
  (Panel), no permission (Locked), phone. Keyboard: "[" toggles the rail (not while typing); the queue's j/k keys stay.
  Rule 0. The supersedes list: the 224 px sidebar, Settings' icon per row, rule 13's "never cut" for titles (now two lines
  then "…" with the full name on hover) — recorded in frata-design-language with a version bump.
- **Tenancy:** no new table or query in N1; the shop's address comes from the layout's existing reads; the rail cookie
  holds no tenant data and the shell imports no data module.
- **Modularity:** the shell in `src/components/shell/`, `PageHeader` and `PageShape` in `src/components/layout/`, the
  week bars in `src/components/data/`.
- **SSOT/DRY:** counts and the week from today's reads; one PageHeader; tab words from `orders/_components/labels.ts`.
- **YAGNI/KISS:** no client state beyond the cookie; Finding waits for N1c.
- **Scalability / performance:** every console page adds 2 statements to the streamed nav counts (the store's address:
  `tenants.slug` and its main domain, in the same `withTenant`, after the first paint); Home's five rows add an items
  subquery per row (`orderItemsSql`, on `order_lines (order_id)`); Orders ≤ 11 statements in one transaction; Products ≤ 9;
  the console's first-load JS stays ≤ 150 kB (check-client-bundle); the rail is CSS plus one cookie.
- **Reliability:** no new outbound call; a failed read keeps its Panel.
- **Security:** no new action; the cookie is read as '1' or '0' only.
- **Motion:** the rail's width change is not animated (layout); the active-tab colour 120 ms.
- **Unit economics:** none.
- **Blast radius:** AppShell (console, Admin, partner), PageHeader (66 files, 26 Admin/partner, 33 with `back`), the bottom
  bar, every flow that reads an h1 or clicks the sidebar (10, 13, 40, 45–50, 53, 62, 64, 67, 70, 71, 73, 78–80, 90, 95).
