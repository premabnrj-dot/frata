# Neel N1 — the shell, the page anatomy and the list screens

## Goal
Ranked problems 3 (catalogue operations) and 1 (COD/RTO: the order queue is where risk is acted on). A merchant finds
the order or product they want in seconds and acts from the list. Measure: time to the first confirm on Home; searches
that end in an open record; saved views kept per store.

## Regression net first
`npm test`, typecheck and the full e2e suite before and after. Flows that walk these screens and must stay green (their
selectors move to the new markup in the same commit, never weakened): `10-checkout`, `20-orders*`/`40-queue`,
`41-ship`, `42-order-page`, `78-products-pr0`, `79-products-list`, `62-settings`/`domains`, `90-ux` (every page × width,
targets, keyboard, text spacing, reduced motion), `30-primitives`, `81-neel`, `05-shop-look` (console baselines retaken
and read; store pages 0 px). New flow `82-neel-screens`: each screen at 1280 and 390 against its drawn values, axe.

## Order of work
**N1·1 — The shell and the page anatomy** (§ Layout)
1. Sidebar 232 px from 1280; a 64 px icon rail from 768 to 1279, and by choice at any width ("[" or the button at its
   foot), remembered per person in a cookie the layout reads (no flash). On the rail: each icon has a tooltip
   (InfoTip-style on the bar), a count becomes a dot. The store's name and its address under it ("kesarstudio.in").
   Groups as today (Today, Catalogue, Store; the account at the foot). Admin and partner keep their nav; they take the
   same widths.
2. `PageHeader` as drawn: breadcrumb above (two levels at most; a phone shows "‹ Products"), the title 22/28 (20/26 on a
   phone, two lines then "…" with the full name on hover), its status chip, a meta line of facts, then actions right:
   More, secondaries, the one primary last. The header scrolls away; a list's tabs or filter bar stick under the shell.
3. Page shapes (`PageShape`: list fluid to 1440, editor 1120, settings 760; padding 32 · 24 · 16) and the spacing scale
   (24 between sections, 32 between groups). Body 15/22 and titles 20/26 on a phone, in `[data-neel]` only.
4. The bottom bar and its More sheet as drawn (Home, Orders, Products, More; the active tab in neel).

**N1·2 — The screens as drawn** (§ Screens)
5. **Home**: "Today" with the day ("Friday, 13 November"), Store open and Share store at the right; three count tiles
   (To confirm, To pack, On hold) with "Oldest 8 d" or "Nothing waiting"; "Next to confirm" (five rows, Confirm on each,
   "All 43"); "Last 7 days" as bars with the count and ₹ written (`--chart`, today `--chart-now`).
6. **Orders**: the tabs in the drawn order and words (Couldn't deliver, To confirm, To pack, To ship, On the way, Coming
   back, All), the search, Payment and Risk filters, columns Order · Customer · Payment · Status · Total · Placed (48 px
   rows, the name and "Madurai · 3 items" two-line), the bulk bar in neel when rows are picked (Confirm N, Put on hold,
   Clear), the pager. Phone: two-line rows as drawn.
7. **Products**: as drawn (photo-forward 56 px rows, status pills, "Low · 2", "Made to order" as a Tag, the bulk bar in
   neel: Set status, Add to collection, Archive, Delete). Most is PR·1's; the differences only.
8. **Settings**: one list, each row its current value, no icon tiles, 760 wide; "Ready for live payments" as a chip in
   the header.

**N1·3 — Finding things** (§ Finding)
9. **Instant search** in Orders and Products: the URL follows each key after 150 ms (router.replace, no history spam),
   the match marked in the row, "No order matches 9845" with what it searched. Finds an order by number, name, any 4
   phone digits, AWB; a product by name, SKU, tag (today's queries, extended for AWB and phone digits where missing).
10. **Filter bar**: chips that read "Payment is COD ×"; "+ Filter" opens the field list, then values with their counts;
    ranges for ₹ and dates; Clear all; "9 of 300 orders". All in the address (Back works, a link can be shared).
    Orders fields: Payment, Risk, State, Placed, Total, Source, Tag. Products: Status, Category, Collection, Stock.
11. **Saved views**: "+ Save view" keeps the filters as a tab for the whole team; its menu renames or removes it. New table
    `saved_views` (tenant_id, list, name, query, position, created_by, created_at) with its grant, RLS and policy in one
    migration; written by Owner and Manager, read by every member. A query is re-validated on read (unknown keys dropped).
12. **Filters on a phone**: a sheet from the bottom; its button counts what you will see ("Show 9 orders").

**N1·4 — Quick look** (§ Layout · Right-side panels)
13. Orders: Space or a row's peek button opens a 400 px panel from the right over the list (the list keeps its scroll);
    ↑↓ moves between rows, Esc closes, "Open order" goes to the page; a sheet on a phone. Reads one order through the
    existing order query; nothing is edited there (Confirm is the queue's own action, as on the row).

## Design review
- **UI/UX:** every screen as drawn; states: empty (Empty / NoMatch), loading (Skeleton on first load, the top line after),
  error (Panel), no permission (Locked), phone. Keyboard: "[" toggles the rail; the queue's keys stay; ↑↓/Esc in quick
  look; the filter popover and sheet are dialogs with focus kept. Rule 0: no helper text.
- **Tenancy:** one new table, `saved_views`, with tenant_id, grant, RLS and policy in its migration; read through
  `src/lib/queries/saved-views.ts` in `withTenant`; written by an action that checks the role. Filter counts read through
  the existing list queries. The rail cookie holds no tenant data.
- **Modularity:** filter parsing and the chip sentence in a pure engine (`src/domains/finding/filters.ts`, per list's
  fields); queries in `src/lib/queries/`; the bar, sheet, search and quick look as primitives in `src/components/data/`.
- **SSOT/DRY:** one filter definition per list feeds the URL parser, the chips, the counts and the query.
- **YAGNI:** no per-person views (team-wide only, as drawn); no filter on Admin lists in N1 (N4).
- **KISS:** filters are URL state; views are saved URL queries; no client store.
- **Performance:** counts per filter value in one grouped query per open popover, not per page load; instant search
  debounced 150 ms and cancelled when a newer one starts; indexes for any new filter column (EXPLAIN at 10×).
- **Security:** the saved-view action validates the list and the query (Zod, length caps), role-checked; quick look
  reads by the order's id within the tenant.
- **Blast radius:** AppShell (console, Admin, partner), PageHeader (every page), the bottom bar, the queue's keys, every
  flow that clicks the sidebar or reads a page title.
