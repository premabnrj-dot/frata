## Practices recorded in skills (rule 6)
- **Architecture:** themes are data over one section library; `resolveTheme` is the only merge; sources are pure and
  never rank by cost; the live look is `liveOf` (the newest publish not on a withdrawn version); preview is the only framed route.
- **Design language:** Look and the customizer (one primary action, autosave states, Undo/Redo, picture cards,
  counters for limits, phone bottom sheet); storefront tokens come from the theme; promises come from facts.
- **Testing:** the visual gate (`05-shop-look` + `themes:shots`) for any storefront change; Lighthouse per theme.
- **Security:** framing exception documented next to `security-headers.ts`; links only to shop paths.

### unit-economics-reviewer: HELPS merchants, NEUTRAL for Frrata now (hurts below ~100 shops)
- No change to what a merchant pays. New Frrata cost is image transfer from Blob (≈ ₹6–211 per shop per month by
  shape), everything else ≈ ₹0. CI minutes are free while the repo is public.
