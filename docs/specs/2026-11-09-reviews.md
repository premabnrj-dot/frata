# Reviews — the app a shop turns on, and every review works for it
Status: draft · Date: 2026-11-09 · Mockup: https://claude.ai/artifact/3H3e4gpaXpQKcDPz7gi9By · Approved: pending
Research: `docs/research/2026-11-09-reviews.md` (apps, Baymard, Google, IS 19000, CCPA, ASCI, DPDP — dated).

## Goal
North star (1) COD/RTO and (2) size and fit returns, plus (4) conversion: real reviews from delivered buyers, with the
fit they found, raise first-time buyer trust (most of an Instagram-driven brand's traffic) and tell the next shopper
which size to pick. **Measures**: review rate (reviews ÷ delivered orders asked) ≥ 8% by month 2; share of fashion
products with a fit bar; returns with reason "size" on products with ≥ 10 fit answers vs without; product-page conversion
with vs without reviews (the shop's own analytics).

## Job
A brand turns on Reviews in Apps and, with no further setup, every delivered order is asked once on WhatsApp; the
answers appear on the product page in the shop's own look, with verified badges and a fit bar; the owner approves or
replies in one queue and sees what shoppers say.

## Research — taken
- **Judge.me / Loox / Okendo / Yotpo**: delivery-timed requests, photo reviews, star badge on cards, carousel, all-reviews
  page, Judge.me CSV import. *Taken.* Their star-threshold auto-publish and asterisk edits: *not taken* (IS 19000).
- **Okendo fashion attributes**: fit as Runs small / True to size / Runs large. *Taken.*
- **Baymard**: clickable histogram that filters; one combined fit bar; photos filter; reply to negative reviews; newest
  first; no collapsed filters. *Taken.*
- **Nector / Indian stacks**: WhatsApp requests, phone-only (COD) customers. *Taken* (WhatsApp first; email fallback).
- **IS 19000, CCPA dark patterns (binding 1 Jan 2027), Google 2026-07-24, ASCI, DPDP**: the rules in § Compliance are
  in the engine, not in settings.

## What the merchant gets (phases; each its own plan)
### R0 Regression net
e2e `e2e/flows/66-reviews.mjs` (request → submit → moderate → shown → JSON-LD) and unit tests for the engine, before the
first change to the product page.

### R1 Collect, show, moderate (the MVP — on as soon as the app is added)
1. **Turn it on**: Apps › Reviews › Add. The module activates with defaults (below): requests on, product-page widget on,
   card stars on. It depends on Communication (WhatsApp) — Add asks to connect it, or runs on email only.
2. **Requests**: N days after delivery (from the order's delivered time; default 5), one reminder after 3 days; never
   to RTO, cancelled or returned orders; every delivered order asked the same way (no gating). WhatsApp **Utility**
   template ("How was your {product}? {link}") — no offer in the message — then email if no WhatsApp. Through the outbox.
3. **The review form** (a shop page in the shop's look, opened from the link; no sign-in — a signed token per order
   line): stars (or the shop's rating shape), title optional, text, photos (per settings), **fit** for clothing and
   footwear (Runs small / True to size / Runs large), size bought filled from the order, name shown as "First L." or
   Anonymous, consent line. One review per order line; the author may edit (marked "edited") or delete.
4. **Verified buyer**: every request-born review carries "Verified buyer" (linked to a delivered order line). Imported
   reviews say "Imported"; a free-product review says "Received free product".
5. **Product page** (a Slot: works in every theme, drawn only from store tokens): average + count; **histogram whose bars
   filter**; fit bar (apparel); chips: With photos, 5★…1★, size; sort Newest (default) / Highest / Lowest / With photos;
   photo strip; each review: rating, verified tag, size and fit, date, text, photos, the shop's reply, Report.
   **Card stars** on product cards (on/off).
6. **Moderation**: *Publish automatically* (default) or *Approve each* (held reviews publish themselves after 72 h
   unless the owner acts with a reason). Auto-hold for **everyone alike**: profanity, links, phone numbers, spam signals.
   Hide only with a listed reason (IS 19000 grounds); the owner never edits text; removed reviews and reasons kept 180
   days. One public reply per review (moderated alike).
7. **Google**: `aggregateRating` + up to 5 `review` items in the product page's JSON-LD, only what the page shows; no
   shop-wide stars.
8. **Import / export**: Judge.me-format CSV (from Judge.me, Loox, Yotpo, Okendo exports); imports labelled.

### R2 Style, layouts, the reviews page, analytics, incentives
1. **Rating style**: shape Stars / Hearts / Circles (segmented); colour = the theme accent or a contrast-passing swatch;
   size S / M / L; show count on/off.
2. **Layouts** (pictures, as the Look editor's): product page **List · Grid · Compact**; home section **Carousel ·
   Photo wall · Featured quote**; all in every theme (store tokens, the theme's radius and type).
3. **Reviews page** `/reviews` (on/off; off = 404 and out of the sitemap): the shop's average and count, a photo wall,
   filters (rating, with photos, product, collection), newest first, each review linked to its product; in the menu by
   choice. Indexable; no shop-wide stars markup.
4. **Review types**: Text and photo (default) / Text only / Photo required; max photos 1–5. Video later.
5. **Incentive** (off by default): a coupon for a photo review, shown on the thank-you page (never in the WhatsApp
   message: it would turn it into Marketing), never depends on the rating, badge "Incentivised", **excluded from the
   average**, flagged in markup and feeds.
6. **Analytics** (all derived): rating trend, review rate, photo share, histogram, reply rate and time on ≤ 3★, fit by
   size (with a link to the size chart), most-mentioned words; per product.

### R3 Later
Q&A; AI summary (verified text only, ≥ 10 reviews, labelled "AI summary of verified reviews"); attributes beyond apparel
(Quality, Comfort, Value; jewellery, beauty); Merchant Center product ratings (≥ 50 reviews, via Merchant API — S·4);
Meta catalogue ratings.

## Console surfaces (mockup)
- **Apps › Reviews** with tabs: **Overview** (numbers, fit by size, attention list), **Reviews** (queue: chips All ·
  Held · Published · Hidden · Reported · ≤ 3★ unanswered; each row: stars, verified, product, size/fit, text, photos;
  Publish / Hide with reason / Reply), **Requests** (sent, opened, reviewed, by day), **Display** (layout pictures,
  rating style, card stars, reviews page — a live preview in the shop's theme), **Settings** (the table above).
- On the product editor: the product's average and a link to its reviews.

## Design review
- **UI/UX** — the one action per tab: approve/reply (Reviews), pick a look (Display). States: no reviews yet (the
  widget is hidden until the first review; the console shows 0 beside the requests sent), one, many, held, hidden, reported, imported, photo failed, phone width (filters as a horizontal chip
  row; the queue as cards). Controls as the Look editor: segmented for ≤ 4 choices, layouts as pictures, swatches with a
  check, chips for filters and day counts, radios for publishing. Keyboard: chips are radio groups; the histogram's bars
  are buttons. Norman: the live preview is the signifier; defaults do the work; Undo on Hide.
- **Tenancy** — `reviews`, `review_photos`, `review_events` (moderation log), `review_requests` with tenant_id, grant,
  RLS, policy and proof rows in one migration; the form's token is HMAC over (tenant, order line) and the tenant comes
  from the host, checked against the token; the storefront reads published reviews only, through `src/lib/queries/`.
- **Modularity** — `src/domains/reviews/` (pure: summary, histogram, fit bar, moderation rules, request schedule,
  markup); `src/lib/queries/reviews.ts`; the module's Slot on the product page and a theme section; `src/app/(console)/
  console/apps/reviews/`; the WhatsApp template via Communication's sender.
- **SSOT / DRY** — the average, count, histogram and fit bar are derived from published rows by one engine function
  (cached per product, invalidated by tag on publish); the incentive exclusion lives there once; verified = has an order
  line, never a stored flag.
- **YAGNI** — no Q&A, AI summary, video, syndication, a review network, or SMS in R1; each has its signal in § R3.
- **KISS** — R1 is four tables, one engine, one form page, one Slot, one console page.
- **Scalability** — reviews per product indexed (tenant, product, status, created); the storefront reads a cached
  summary + first page (≤ 10) per product; photos through the existing WebP pipeline; the request run batches per tenant.
- **Performance** — the product page gets the summary in its static render (ISR, tag `shop:<t>:reviews`); the filter and
  "more" load a JSON page on interaction; no new script on cards (stars are markup); the form page is its own route.
- **Reliability** — requests through the outbox with retries; a WhatsApp failure falls to email; a held review publishes
  itself at 72 h (the daily run).
- **Security** — the form: signed token per order line, one-time per line, rate-limited per IP and token; photos only as
  WebP into `tenants/<t>/reviews/`; text stored as text, rendered as text (no HTML, links auto-held); Report is
  rate-limited; reviewer phone/email never reach the page. `security-reviewer` on the plan.
- **Unit economics** — WhatsApp Utility ₹0.115 + GST per request (+ one reminder) on the merchant's own WhatsApp
  account: ~₹0.27 per delivered order; email via SES ~₹0.01; photo storage negligible. Priced as an add-on (registry:
  `billing: 'add_on'`); a free tier to be set with `unit-economics-reviewer`.
- **Blast radius** — the product page (a Slot), product cards (stars), the sitemap (reviews page), JSON-LD, the outbox,
  the daily run, Communication's templates, Apps. Footgun: card stars must not add a per-card query — they come from the
  catalogue's cached summary.

## Compliance (in the engine)
Everyone delivered is asked alike; no rating-based publishing or asking; never edit; 72 h; newest first; histogram
filters; negatives never collapsed; verified only from an order; imports labelled; incentives independent, badged,
excluded from the average, flagged; free-product reviews labelled; "How reviews work" note linked from the widget;
reply free and moderated; Report with a reasoned answer; author deletion; 180-day log; DPDP notice at submit, "First L."
default, contact details never shown.

## Out of scope (and what brings it back)
Q&A — shoppers asking in DMs about the same product; AI summary — products with ≥ 10 reviews common; video — merchants
asking; Merchant Center ratings — S·4 restarts and ≥ 50 reviews; SMS requests — shops without WhatsApp > 20%.
