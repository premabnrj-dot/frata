# Orders (1D): the queue, the order page, risk, cancel, packing slip, invoice — and cleaner screens
Status: active (approved 2026-11-07, owner calls 1–5 as recommended; reviews folded in) · Date: 2026-11-07 · Mockup: https://claude.ai/artifact/E9DkUXWME9QXbgewuct3Lo · Approved: 2026-11-07

## Goal
Ranked problem 1 (COD/RTO losses) and the Phase 1 promise "take orders and
**confirm them**". Confirming a COD order before it ships is the manual version
of Phase 2's WhatsApp confirmation; the risk reasons tell the merchant which to
call first. Problem 6 (true profit) gets the invoice and the cost snapshot read
back. Measures: a merchant clears 30 COD orders in under 10 minutes with the
keyboard; every cancelled order says why; no order is confirmed twice; the
invoice series has no gaps.

## Job
A merchant opens Orders in the morning, sees what to confirm and what to pack,
calls the risky ones, confirms or cancels each in a keystroke, prints packing
slips and invoices, and never wonders what an order's state is.

## What the cross-check found (plan vs built, 2026-11-07)

taken into **1D·0** (what orders need) and **1D·5** (the rest):
~~variants with orders are still deleted~~ (archived, 1D·4a); ~~the storefront is not

(marked "Next" until they do); a stale "Razorpay arrives with checkout" line.
**Left for later, listed so nothing is forgotten:** logo upload, team members,
Admin address change, pincode directory loader + attribution, demo catalogue,
would-be fee and free-shop count in Admin, LCP/INP/CLS gates, "find my size"
(Phase 4).

## Research (sources and dates in § Facts used)
- **Shopify Orders** — payment and fulfilment are separate axes, never one
  merged label; risk is a level + separate indicators + one recommendation
  sentence with its action; cancel sits under More with a required reason and
  "restock" ticked; one bar for views, search and filter chips; line items one
  uniform row. **Taken:** two badges per row, the risk shape, the cancel dialog,
  view tabs.
- **Razorpay RTO intelligence / review COD** — reasons in plain words (address
  has no building name, many orders in an hour, first-time customer…);
  approve / hold / cancel; "an RTO doesn't by itself prove fraud".
  **Taken:** Confirm · Hold · Cancel, risk advises and never cancels.
- **Shiprocket (Aug 2026)** — a Duplicate Orders tab (phone + address + SKU +
  qty), On Hold, repeat-buyer chip. **Taken:** possible-duplicate reason,
  On hold, buyer history.
- **Linear triage, Gmail, Stripe** — single-key decisions, j/k/x/z, `?` for the
  list, nothing fires in an input. **Taken:** the keys below.
- **NN/g confirmation dialogs (reviewed 2026-08-07)** — no dialog for routine
  actions, undo instead; dialogs for serious ones, buttons that name the
  outcome. **Taken:** Confirm/Hold undo; Cancel asks.
- **GST invoices** — Rule 46 particulars (≤ 16-char serial unique per
  financial year, place of supply, HSN, taxable value, CGST+SGST or IGST);
  issue at removal of goods (= packing); apparel 5%/18% per piece.
  **Taken:** the invoice number is issued at "Mark packed", so a cancel before
  packing leaves no gap. **A CA confirms the template before first use.**
- **Info tips (NN/g 2026-01-23, Carbon 2026-09-30, WCAG 1.4.13, Polaris,
  Atlassian, DWP)** — tap-to-open (i), optional detail only; never errors,
  limits, money or legal text; many tips = unclear labels. **Taken:**
  `frata-design-language` § Labels, info tips and help text (owner rule).

## Design (see the mockup)

**1D·0 What 1D stands on** (gap fixes 1D depends on, and the primitives)
- Variant delete → archive when it has orders (`src/app/(console)/console/products/actions.ts`);
  storefront refreshed when checkout or cancel moves stock across 0; ship-by on
  the order page; the 90-day erasure run by the cron (looping tenants through
  `withTenant`, never a no-tenant policy); "To confirm" count in the nav;
  Collections in the nav.
- Primitives in `src/components/ui/`: `InfoTip`, `Dialog`, `Menu` (More),
  `Tabs`, `Toast` with Undo, the `?` key sheet. The barcode is drawn locally
  (Code 128 as SVG, a small pure encoder in `src/domains/orders/barcode.ts`) —
  never a third-party image API.

**1D·5 Cleaner screens** (after 1D·1–4, its own commit)
- Every existing help line (~70: product editor, settings, online payments,
  admin, checkout) is removed (label made clear), turned into an (i), or kept as
  the one visible line the action needs (errors, limits, money, legal). The
  checkout keeps its 120 kB budget (`check:storefront-build`); if `InfoTip`
  does not fit there, the checkout keeps its lines. The mockup shows
  before/after for the product editor, Settings → Pay on delivery and the
  checkout.
- The homepage claims marked "Next"; the stale Razorpay line removed;
  grievance officer refused when emptied on an open shop.

**1D·1 The queue** (`/console/orders`)
- Tabs (derived, with counts): **To confirm** (COD placed, not confirmed or
  held) · **To pack** (confirmed, or paid online) · **On hold** · **All** ·
  Not paid (online, abandoned — off to the side).
- Row: order number · customer + city · items · **two badges** (payment:
  COD / Paid online; state: To confirm, On hold, Confirmed, Packed, Cancelled) ·
  risk (Low / Medium / High as word + mark, only Medium/High shown) · total ·
  placed. 50 a page; search by number, name or phone.
- Keys: `j`/`k` move, `x` select, `Enter` open, `c` confirm, `h` hold, `p`
  print slip, `z` undo, `/` search, `?` the list. Off in inputs. `c` on a
  High-risk row opens the order instead (same guard as bulk). A switch in the
  `?` sheet turns single-key shortcuts off, per person (WCAG 2.1.4).
- Bulk: N selected → Confirm · Hold · Print slips. Bulk confirm skips High-risk
  orders and says "Confirmed 12. Skipped 2 high-risk orders — open them to
  decide."
- Confirm and Hold are instant (optimistic) with Undo for 8 s (the phase spec's
  5 s is updated to 8) and an Undo link kept on the order; conditional
  (`confirmed_at is null`): the second person sees "Already confirmed by Riya
  at 10:42" (read from `order_events`). Undo is a compare-and-set: `set
  confirmed_at = null where confirmed_at = <the value this action set> and
  packed_at is null`; if packed in between: "Already packed — cancel instead".
- A paid order whose stock ran out at payment is not To pack: it shows in To
  confirm with the state **Refund due** and the one action Cancel (reason Out of
  stock, "Refund ₹X in Razorpay").

**1D·2 The order page** (`/console/orders/KS-1001`)
- Header: number, two badges, ONE primary action — To confirm: **Confirm**;
  On hold: **Confirm**; Confirmed or paid: **Mark packed**; Packed: **Print
  packing slip**; Cancelled: none. More: Hold, Cancel, Print invoice, Copy link.
- Risk card (Medium/High only): level, up to three reasons, one next step
  ("Call before confirming") with **Call** (`tel:`) and **WhatsApp** (prefilled).
- Items from the snapshot (image, name, size · colour, qty × price), delivery,
  COD fee, GST, total; "Paid online — do not collect cash"; "Paid after it sold
  out — refund in Razorpay".
- Customer: name, phone with Call/WhatsApp, address — **Edit** until packed
  (the phase spec's "until shipped" is updated), each edit a timeline entry
  ("Address edited by Riya" — field names only, never old or new values, so
  no shopper data outlives an erasure), risk recomputed.
- Notes (team only) and Activity (placed, paid, confirmed by…, held, edited,
  packed, invoice issued, cancelled with reason; support visits badged).
- Phone: one column in the same order, the primary action fixed at the bottom.

**1D·3 Actions** — confirm, hold, unhold, cancel (reason required: Fake order,
Customer cancelled, Couldn't reach, Out of stock, Can't deliver there, Other +
note; "Put stock back" ticked — stock goes back only for lines whose stock was
taken; a paid order says "Refund ₹2,490 in Razorpay" with a link), mark packed
(issues the invoice number), edit address/phone, add note. All through
`asMember`, each writing one `order_events` row in the same transaction; the
storefront refreshed when stock crosses 0. In support mode the `audit_events`
row holds the order id as `target` and only the NAMES of changed fields in
`detail` — never an address, phone or note (`frata_admin` reads that table
across shops). Bulk actions write one row per order.
- **Restock is per line:** `takeStock` stamps `order_lines.stock_taken_at`;
  cancel puts back only lines with it set and clears it. The `checkout.stockTaken`
  jsonb flag is retired (existing COD orders backfilled in the migration).
- **Pre-order lines** take stock at Mark packed (refused with "Only 2 in stock
  — 3 needed" if short), so stock falls for pre-orders too.
- **Cancel after packing** keeps the invoice and marks it cancelled (shown
  struck on reprint, number never reused). Whether a credit note is also
  needed is a CA question, asked before first use.

**Who may do what** (each list passed to `asMember(...)`; hiding a button is not
the guard)

| Action | Staff | Admin | Owner | Support session |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Confirm, hold, unhold, undo, add note | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Mark packed (issues the invoice number), print slip, print invoice | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ (open audited) |
| Edit address / phone | — | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Cancel | — | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |

Staff never see cost or profit on the order (profit per order is Phase 3).

**State** (`src/domains/orders/order-state.ts`, one precedence, tested):
returned (rto_received > rto) > delivered > cancelled > delivery failed >
shipped (Phase 2, unchanged) > packed > refund due (paid, and an in-stock line
whose stock was never taken) > held > confirmed / paid > to confirm (COD) / awaiting payment /
abandoned. A paid order can be held. Confirm and unhold both clear `held_at`.
The new `OrderMoments` fields are required, so a query that forgets one fails
to compile. The shopper sees `shopperState()` only: held and to confirm read
"Confirming your order", packed "Packed"; never a cancel reason, note, risk or
invoice detail (names added to the shopper sentinel list in `check-tenant`).
The tab and nav-count SQL comes from one module of predicates
(`src/lib/db/order-predicates.ts`, also used by checkout's `real` and
`holding`), with a test that checks it against `orderState()` on fixtures.

**1D·4 Documents** — printable pages (browser print, A4):
- **Packing slip**: shop name, order number large (+ barcode), items with size,
  colour, qty, SKU, thumbnail; delivery name, address, phone; return address;
  a large **"Collect ₹2,539"** box or **"Prepaid — collect nothing"**; no prices.
- **Tax invoice** (the order's stored seller GSTIN) or **Bill of supply** (none;
  its own series, Rule 49): number `KESR/26-27/00001` from the shop's invoice series (up to 4 characters, or 5 ending in / or -, so a whole year fits: `seriesFits` in `src/domains/orders/work.ts`, as built 1D·7; bill of supply `KESR/26-27/B0001`, 9,999 a year) (asserted ≤ 16 chars, per
  financial year in IST, from `tenant_counters`), date, seller name/address/
  GSTIN, buyer name and address, place of supply (state + code), lines with
  HSN, qty, taxable value, rate, CGST+SGST (same state) or IGST (other state),
  delivery and COD fee as part of the supply apportioned per rate, totals,
  "Prices include GST" only when the order's `prices_include_gst` is true.
  Issued once; reprints show the same number.
- **Amounts are read, never recomputed:** the order stores, at placement, the
  seller GSTIN (registered = GSTIN present; no second flag), and each line's share of the charges
  (`order_lines.charge_taxable`, `charge_gst`), with a test that lines + charges
  equal `gst_total`. Orders placed before 1D (none in production on
  2026-11-07) get their shares from `taxOrder` on the stored rates, by the same
  `amounts.ts`, asserted equal to `gst_total`. A capture after a switch to COD
  removes the COD charge and recomputes the stored totals through `amounts.ts` — before packing only; once packed the issued invoice stands and the order page flags it (`paidAfterPacking`, 1D·7). Same-state is decided by **state code**: one name↔code map in
  `src/domains/checkout/states.ts` (`STATE_CODE`, as built; `supply_state` is a name, the seller's state comes from `seller_gstin`), tested.
- **One amounts function** (`src/domains/orders/amounts.ts`): what to collect,
  what was paid, what to refund, what the invoice totals — so a paid-after-
  switch order's COD charge never reaches the slip, the invoice or the refund.
- Documents are `page.tsx` under `(console)` (so `requireMember` is forced),
  `Cache-Control: private, no-store`, `Referrer-Policy: no-referrer`, no static
  params. Bulk slips: up to 50 ids, resolved within the shop, unknown ids
  skipped.

**Risk rules** (`src/domains/orders/risk.ts`, pure; reasons, never a score;
never stored — bulk confirm recomputes on the server, never trusts the client;
windows counted from the order's own `placed_at`, in IST; erased orders
excluded; inputs from one query in `src/lib/queries/orders.ts` with
`tenant_id =` in every subquery):
- *High:* possible duplicate (same phone and same items in an open order in 24 h);
  3+ COD orders from this phone today; a previous order from this phone
  cancelled as "Fake order".
- *Medium (two or more of):* first order from this phone; address line
  (before the area is joined on — stored separately from now) without a
  house/building number or under 15 characters; city typed does not match the
  pincode's city (**off until the pincode directory is loaded**); order value
  over 2× this shop's median COD order over 90 days; name looks like a test
  ("test", "abc", one letter).
- The Fake-order rule reads `orders.cancel_reason` (new column).
- Next step: High → "Call before confirming. Cancel if unreachable." Medium →
  "Call or WhatsApp to confirm." Low → nothing shown.

## Design review
- **UI/UX** — states: empty queue per tab ("Nothing to confirm. New COD orders
  land here."), loading, one, 300, phone width; every action has feedback and
  undo or a dialog; labels need no tip; (i) used only for definitions (RTO,
  bill of supply).
- **Tenancy** (tenancy-auditor, 2026-11-07, folded in) — new tables
  `order_notes` and `order_events`: tenant_id not null, composite FK
  `(tenant_id, order_id)` → orders on delete cascade, index `(tenant_id,
  order_id)`, author from auth never a form, any support-session FK composite;
  `select, insert` to `frata_app` only (append-only), revoked from anon and
  authenticated, no `frata_admin` grant, RLS with `using` and `with check`.
  Names: `app_member_names()` — no arguments, returns names of
  `app_current_tenant()`'s members and support staff only, `search_path ''`,
  execute revoked from public/anon/authenticated, granted to `frata_app`.
  `invoice_number`: `unique (tenant_id, invoice_number)`, CHECK set iff
  `packed_at` set, immutable once set (trigger). The order URL resolves by
  number within `requireMember`'s tenant (a foreign prefix is the same 404);
  actions take the order id under the same tenant. `check-tenant` gate 5
  tightened: an `app_current_tenant() is null or …` policy fails except on
  the `OPEN_FOR_APP` tables. Proof cases: no tenant → 0 rows; A cannot read or
  insert B's notes/events; no update/delete on notes/events; anon,
  authenticated, frata_admin hold nothing; A cannot set B's packed/invoice
  columns; duplicate invoice in one shop refused, same number in two shops
  allowed; invoice number cannot change; names function scoped; an order
  action's audit row holds no address or phone.
- **Modularity** — `orders/risk.ts`, `orders/documents.ts` (invoice/slip
  view-models, pure), `counters/fiscal-year.ts` (exists), actions in
  `console/orders/actions.ts`, reads in `queries/orders.ts`.
- **SSOT/DRY** — state from `orderState()` only; money from the stored order;
  GST from the stored lines (never recomputed); the invoice number stored once.
- **YAGNI** — no tags, no saved custom views, no export, no bulk cancel (one at
  a time with a reason), no courier labels (Phase 2), no refunds from Frata.
- **KISS** — documents are print pages, not PDFs.
- **Scalability/performance** — list 50 a page on `orders_tenant_placed`; no
  partial indexes (they cannot use `now()`, and 300 orders a day does not need
  them); risk inputs in one query.
- **Reliability** — conditional updates; undo is a compare-and-set on the
  value the action wrote.
- **Security** — the role table above; support opens of slips and invoices are
  audited (they hold full PII); no shopper data beyond what the order holds.
- **Unit economics** — none new (no new Frata cost); invoice correctness is a
  merchant compliance risk → CA sign-off.
- **Blast radius** — `orderState` gains `held` and `packed`; the shopper's
  order page reads it; the product editor's variant delete becomes archive.
