# Billing: how Frrata bills its merchants, and how Frrata's team runs it
Status: active · Date: 2026-11-08 · Mockup: https://claude.ai/artifact/QUgWTNC7Fq1K639q5YURhm (`docs/mockups/2026-11-08-billing.html`) · Approved: owner, 2026-11-08 ("take up both plans and execute"; calls: calendar month, plan in advance + fee in arrears, Razorpay Recurring, acknowledge-only notice after day 14, partner payouts from paid invoices; built to test mode until Frrata's GSTIN, SAC and Razorpay account exist)
Research: `docs/research/2026-11-08-documents-and-billing.md` § Billing (integration-researcher, 2026-11-08).

## Goal
Frrata earns only when a merchant keeps more profit per delivered order, so it bills **a plan fee plus a fee on
delivered orders** (North star (6) true profit per order; the business model in `frata-unit-economics`). Billing must
be **predictable for the merchant** (the bill so far is visible every day; nothing surprises), **correct for GST**,
and **cheap to run** (Frrata's team handles exceptions, not every bill).
Measures: share of invoices paid within 3 days of issue (target ≥ 95%); billing tickets per 100 merchants per month
(target < 2); share of merchants on autopay (target ≥ 80% by month 3); invoices a CA had to correct (target 0).

## Job
A brand owner sees what they will pay before they pay it, pays without doing anything each month, downloads a
GST invoice their CA accepts, and understands every line; Frrata's team runs the month, fixes the few that fail,
and never touches a merchant's shop to collect.

## Decided (owner, 2026-11-08)
1. An order packed and never shipped is **never billed**.
2. **Lost** marked by hand needs no proof; a Lost order is not billed.
3. A late **Came back** or **Lost** after its month was billed: **the invoice is a snapshot; the change is credited on
   the next invoice**.
4. Vercel Pro later; scheduled billing runs need it (docs/setup.md § Later, point 5).
**Proposed (this spec):** the fee for an order is the plan version in force **when the order becomes billable**
(`billableDelivered`: delivered, or shipped ≥ 30 days with no outcome), and it is billed in that IST month
(`feeForOrder(planHistory, billableAt)`, `src/domains/billing/fee.ts`, read by Analytics A·1 too).

## Research (summary; sources and dates in the research note)
- **RBI E-mandate Framework 2026** (21 Apr 2026): cards, PPI, UPI; no extra approval up to **₹15,000** a debit (SaaS
  is not in the ₹1 lakh list); first debit always approved; fixed or **variable** amount with a maximum; **notice ≥
  24 h before every debit**. eNACH is outside it. *Taken:* a variable mandate with a maximum; our own notice.
- **Razorpay Subscriptions**: fixed amount per cycle; **add-ons deprecated**; a live UPI/eMandate subscription cannot
  change amount; retries T+1…T+3 then halted. *Not taken* (our bill changes every month). **Razorpay Recurring
  Payments** (a token; we start each charge; UPI Autopay, card, eMandate, NACH). *Taken.* **Razorpay Invoices** cannot
  make GST invoices → *Frrata issues its own.* **Smart Collect / Payment Links** → *taken as the fallback.*
- **Cashfree On-Demand** (charge up to an authorised maximum; eNACH to ₹1 crore). *Kept as the alternative.*
- **Shopify (India)**: 30-day cycles, apps on their own cycle; INR by UPI, cards through Razorpay; above ₹15,000 the
  merchant approves each bill; unpaid → store frozen, no grace. *Taken:* INR, UPI first, approval above ₹15,000.
  *Not taken:* freezing a shop (a frozen shop cannot deliver the orders Frrata is paid on).
- **Stripe** (prorations; customer portal: payment method, invoices, download), **Linear** (credits not refunded;
  added mid-month billed next month). *Taken:* upgrade now prorated, downgrade next cycle, usage never prorated,
  credits carried forward, a self-serve billing page.

## Design
**Console › Settings › Plan and billing** (owner and admin; staff and partners never see money):
- **This month so far** — a meter: *118 of 150 orders included*; *Fee on delivered orders so far ₹2,140*; *Estimated
  bill on 1 Dec ₹4,294.02 incl. GST*; each line opens its working (which orders, the rate).
- **Your plan** — Launch · ₹1,499/mo · 150 orders · 2% of delivered value; modules on it; **Change plan** (B4).
- **Paying** — UPI Autopay **Active** · up to ₹15,000 · next debit 1 Dec, notice on 29 Nov; **Change** · or, without a
  mandate, **Set up autopay** with UPI / card / bank (eNACH) and why each.
- **Invoices** — month, number, amount, status (Paid · Due 5 Dec · Failed · Credited), **Download PDF**; credits listed.
- **Billing details** — legal name, GSTIN (validated), billing address and **State** (decides CGST+SGST or IGST),
  billing email. Prefilled from Store details; editable here.
- **A failed debit** — a banner on every console page for owners and admins: "Your 1 Dec payment of ₹4,294.02 didn't
  go through. Pay now" (Pay link, UPI or card); never shown to staff; the shop and its orders keep working.

**Admin › Billing** (Frrata staff):
- **This month's run** — status strip: Draft 412 · Ready 398 · Issued 0 · Paid 0 · Failed 0 · Total ₹23.4 lakh ex
  GST. **Review drafts** lists outliers first (bill up > 50% on last month; custom prices; credits; first bills;
  merchants without billing details). **Issue all ready** (admin only, typed confirmation), then collection starts.
- **Collections** — a queue: Failed (reason, attempts), Awaiting approval (above ₹15,000), No mandate (pay link sent),
  Overdue 7 / 14 days. Row actions: Retry · Send pay link · Record bank transfer · Extend (date, reason) · Waive (reason).
- **A merchant › Billing tab** — plan history, mandate, invoices, payments, credits, adjustments; **Issue credit
  note** (reason, amount by line); every action audited.
- **Exports** — GSTR-1 ready CSV for the month (B2B with GSTIN, B2C by State), invoice register, payments register.
- **Partners** (B5) — share statements from paid invoices only.

**Frrata's tax invoice (PDF, Frrata-branded)** — Frrata's legal name, address, GSTIN; series `FRR/26-27/000001`
(≤ 16); the merchant's legal name, GSTIN, address and State; place of supply; SAC (CA to confirm; 998315 most cited);
lines: plan (period, in advance), modules, orders over the plan, fee on delivered orders (count, value, rate; period,
in arrears), credits; taxable value; CGST+SGST or IGST at 18%; total; reverse charge: No; signature; "Paid ₹… on …
by UPI Autopay" once paid. Rendered by the documents renderer (Documents D0), stored at issue.

## The model
- **One calendar month, IST.** The invoice on the 1st carries **this month's plan and modules in advance** and **last
  month's delivered-order fee and orders over the plan in arrears**, plus credits. One invoice a month per shop.
- **The bill is computed by Frrata, collected by the gateway.** `computeBill(inputs)` is pure
  (`src/domains/billing/`), in paise, from: plan history, add-ons, `billableAt` orders (via `billableAtSql`, the SQL
  twin of `billableDelivered`, with a parity test), adjustments. Its output is frozen into the invoice at issue.
- **Fee per order** = `feeForOrder(planHistory, billableAt)`; orders over the plan are counted against the included
  orders of the month they became billable.
- **Snapshot and credit.** An issued invoice never changes (no UPDATE grant on its money columns). A later Came back /
  Lost, a waiver or a downgrade becomes an **adjustment** credited on the next invoice; a refund of money paid is a
  **credit note** (own series) — CA to confirm the practice.
- **Proration:** upgrade takes effect at once; this month's plan difference is charged pro rata by days on the next
  invoice; downgrade takes effect on the 1st; the usage fee is never prorated (it follows the version at billableAt).
- **Trials and ₹0 months:** a trial bills ₹0 plan, fee as usual unless the trial says otherwise; a total under ₹100
  rolls to the next month (no ₹0 invoices).
- **Collection** (ADR-0006: each debit, notice and email is an outbox row):
  issue (1st) → notice to the merchant ≥ 24 h before (we send it; the bank also does) → debit on the 3rd → retries T+1…T+3
  → banner + email + pay link → day 7 reminder → day 14 owner call (see below). Above ₹15,000: the debit waits for the
  merchant's approval (UPI/card) or uses eNACH without approval.
- **Never block the shop.** The storefront, checkout, orders and shipping never stop for an unpaid bill.
- **Stored:** gateway customer, token and mandate ids and status only; never card or bank numbers.

## Data (B0–B3; every table tenant-scoped with RLS; merchants read their own, never write)
`billing_accounts` (tenant, legal name, GSTIN, address, state code, email, gateway customer id, mandate id, method,
max amount, status) · `bill_runs` (month, status, created/issued by) · `bills` (tenant, run, number, period, status
draft/issued/paid/void, frozen totals, pdf path, issued at) · `bill_lines` (bill, kind plan/module/over/fee/credit/
proration, quantity, unit, rate, taxable, cgst/sgst/igst) · `bill_payments` (bill, attempt, method, gateway id,
amount, status, at) · `billing_adjustments` (tenant, kind late_outcome/waiver/proration/goodwill, amount, reason,
by, applied_to_bill) · `billing_credit_notes` (own series). Frrata's GSTIN, address and invoice series are platform
config. Writes only by `frata_admin` definers and the billing cron; `frata_app` reads its own shop's rows for the
Plan and billing page (owner/admin only, enforced in the page and the query).

## Design review (frata-planning § 3)
- **UI/UX** — merchant states: trial, first bill, paid, due, failed, awaiting approval (> ₹15,000), no mandate,
  credited, phone; staff/partner see nothing about money. Admin states: run not started, drafts with outliers,
  issued, collecting, closed. Norman: the estimate shows before the debit; every line opens its working; a failed
  payment has one action; nothing irreversible without a typed confirmation (issue all, waive).
- **Tenancy** — 7 new tables, each with tenant_id, RLS, select-only policy for frata_app, admin definers for writes;
  proof checks (a shop reads only its bills; cannot write any; admin reads all). Run `tenancy-auditor`.
- **Modularity** — `src/domains/billing` (pure: computeBill, feeForOrder, proration, GST heads) · `src/lib/billing`
  (gateway client through fetchWithTimeout, the run, collection senders in `drain.ts`) · `src/lib/admin/billing.ts` ·
  console page + admin pages.
- **SSOT / DRY** — billable orders: `billableDelivered` / `billableAtSql` (one rule, parity-tested); the fee:
  `feeForOrder`; GST heads: `src/domains/tax/gst.ts` (reused); the invoice: frozen lines, never recomputed.
- **YAGNI** — no annual plans, no coupons on Frrata's bill, no multi-currency, no per-seat pricing, no self-serve
  refunds, no Razorpay Subscriptions.
- **KISS** — one invoice a month; the gateway only collects.
- **Scalability** — 500 shops: one run computes 500 bills in SQL batches; collection drains through the outbox at 5
  minutes (Pro); invoices rendered once and stored.
- **Reliability** — a debit that times out is reconciled by the gateway webhook before any retry (idempotency key per
  bill and attempt); a run is restartable (draft rows keyed by tenant and month).
- **Security** — Admin actions `ADMIN_ONLY` for issue, waive, credit; `ANY_STAFF` read; merchant page `CAN_MANAGE`;
  webhook HMAC (Frrata's own Razorpay account, a platform secret — one owner module); `security-reviewer` on the plan.
- **Unit economics** — this IS the revenue line: run `unit-economics-reviewer` on computeBill against the decided
  tiers; gateway cost per collection (UPI Autopay vs card vs eNACH) goes into fixed costs.
- **Blast radius** — Analytics A·1 reads `feeForOrder`; Ship's `billableDelivered` gains its SQL twin; plans and
  add-ons start to bill; Admin › Merchants plan panel links to the billing tab.

## Skills, agents and gates (with this plan)
- **New skill `frata-billing`**: the model (month, advance + arrears, snapshot + credit, never block the shop), GST
  invoice rules, mandate rules (dated), the run, dunning, what a merchant sees by role.
- **`frata-integrations` 1.5.0**: § Merchant billing (the dated facts table).
- **`frata-unit-economics`**: computeBill is the revenue formula; gateway costs per method.
- **`frata-architecture`**: § Billing tables and who writes them; ADR-0008 "How Frrata bills merchants".
- **New agent `billing-reviewer`**: any diff touching plans, fees, bills or GST — snapshot immutability, parity of
  billable rules, proration, GST heads and series, rounding in paise, the merchant's view by role.
- **Gates**: `check:billing-immutable` (no UPDATE of `bills`/`bill_lines` money columns outside the issue path); vitest
  parity `billableDelivered` ↔ `billableAtSql`; a golden-month fixture (`computeBill` over 30 crafted orders).

## Owner calls
1. Calendar month, plan in advance + usage in arrears, one invoice on the 1st — recommended.
2. Collection: Razorpay Recurring (UPI Autopay first, card, eNACH for > ₹15,000), Payment Link fallback — recommended;
   Cashfree On-Demand the alternative.
3. After day 14 unpaid: (a) owner and admin see a blocking notice in the console but can still work, (b) new paid
   modules pause, (c) nothing more — recommended (a); the shop never stops.
4. Frrata's GSTIN, registered address and SAC: from the owner and the CA before B2.
5. Partner share paid from **paid** invoices only, monthly — recommended (B5).

## Out of scope (and the signal that brings it back)
