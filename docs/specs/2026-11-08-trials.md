# Free trials on plans — the onboarding strategy
Status: active · Date: 2026-11-08 · Mockup: https://claude.ai/artifact/VUAw61mqZzutVNztj5SiuC (docs/mockups/2026-11-08-trials.html) · Approved: owner, 2026-11-08 ("go with your recommendations"; trial length configurable, up to 3 months)

> Owner, 2026-11-08: "We want to set up trial plans. That would be our onboarding strategy. Please plan it
> thoroughly." Already decided the same day, binding on this spec: **an upgrade during a trial waits until the trial is
> over**, and **each order's fee is charged at the rate of the plan it shipped under**.

## Owner decisions (2026-11-08) — binding
- Q1 (revised by the owner, 2026-11-08): **only the plan's monthly price is free during a trial.** The per-order fee,
  add-ons and every other charge apply as usual, from the first order. No waiver, no cap, no counting.
- Q2 at the end without autopay: **7 days of grace, then paused** (no new orders; orders on their way finish; data kept
  180 days).
- Q3 **the length is a setting, not a constant**: Admin › Settings › Trials holds the length for new sign-ups
  (**7–90 days**, default 30, from the day the shop opens) and the days a brand has to open before the clock starts
  anyway (0–30, default 15). A trial keeps the terms it started with; a later change of the setting never moves a
  running trial. Admin may give **one shop** a different length (7–90) until it opens. The owner extension (7 days,
  once) stays.
- Q4 **a one-time code to the owner's phone at sign-up, by WhatsApp only** (one trial per business). No SMS.
- Q5 (owner, 2026-11-08): **WhatsApp only** for the code and every reminder — **no email and no SMS**. (So no email
  provider, and ADR-0005's "No AWS" stands.) Invoices and notices stay in the console.
- Default length stays **30 days** (owner, 2026-11-08). Partner-built shops: the trial starts after handover (spec rule 9).

## Goal
North star: a small Indian fashion brand keeps more profit per delivered order without a developer. A trial moves no
ranked problem by itself; it is how a brand **gets to see** problems 1 (COD/RTO) and 6 (true profit per order) solved
in its own numbers before it pays. The trial's job is to reach that moment, not to give a free store.

**The moment a trial is built around ("first proof"):** the shop's first *delivered* order, with its ₹ kept shown
on the order (profit per order) — COD delivery takes 3–7 days in India, so a trial too short to see one proves nothing.

**How we will know** (Admin › Trials, phase TR3; measured per weekly cohort):
| Measure | Target to test (hypothesis, frata-unit-economics) |
|---|---|
| Trials that open their shop (first publish) | ≥ 60% |
| Trials that reach first proof (a delivered order) | ≥ 35% |
| Trial → paid (autopay set up or first invoice paid) | ≥ 25% of trials, ≥ 50% of those reaching first proof |
| Days from sign-up to first proof (median) | ≤ 14 |
| Paid shops still paying at month 3 | ≥ 80% |
Benchmarks behind these: no-card trials convert 4–6% (good) to 10–15% (great); card-required 25–35% / 50–60%
(ChartMogul, 4 Feb 2026). Ours is no-mandate (see Design § 2), so 25% overall is ambitious and is the hypothesis to test.

## Job
A brand owner who has just found Frrata (Instagram ad, a partner, a referral) wants to put their real catalogue up,
take real orders and see what each delivered order left them — before they commit money every month.

## Research (sources dated; full brief in the session log, 2026-11-08)
| Product | How it runs a trial | Taken |
|---|---|---|
| **Shopify India** (pricing page dated 5 Nov 2026; help page undated) | 3 days free, no card; then ₹20/mo for 3 months; store private / checkout off until a plan is picked; at the end the store freezes, data kept; never restarts a trial | Freeze, never delete; no restarts; a tiny paid bridge turns trialists into mandate holders |
| **Squarespace** (15 Oct 2026; 14 May 2026) | 14 days, no card; commerce off; site goes offline at the end; **one 7-day extension, offered in the "trial expired" email** | One extension, offered *after* expiry, is the win-back moment |
| **Stripe Billing** (API 2026-09-30) | Free trials without a payment method end by **pause** (indefinite) or cancel; `trial_will_end` 3 days before; hosted reminder 7 days before; **usage during a trial is not billed** | Pause as the end state; reminders at 7 and 3 days; decide the per-order fee in a trial on purpose |
| **Dukaan** (© 2026) | No trial: a free plan at 4.99% per order (cap ₹200) | The Indian low end sells "free + commission": our trial must show profit, not just a store |
| **Instamojo**, **Wix** (© 2024; undated) | No trial; 14-day money-back | A refund guarantee is the cheap alternative — kept as Out of scope |

**Indian rules that shape it:** RBI e-mandate framework 2026 (21 Apr 2026): ₹15,000 per debit without extra
authentication, issuer's pre-debit notice 24 h ahead, mandate withdrawable any time; eNACH is NPCI's, unverified here.
Razorpay (12 Mar 2026, vendor claim): ~30% abandon mandate registration. CCPA dark-pattern guidelines list a
"subscription trap" — reportedly including *requiring auto-debit authorisation to get a free subscription*
(primary text unverified; CCPA is fining in 2026, e.g. McAfee, Bar & Bench 1 Sep 2026). **A lawyer reads the
guidelines before we ever require a mandate to start a trial.**

**In the codebase already:** `tenant_plans.trial_ends_at` (a trial makes the plan ₹0 by days: `monthlyPrice`,
`computeBill`); Admin sets a trial when it creates a merchant; module trials (`tenant_addons.trial_ends_at`);
self-serve `/start` creates a shop on **Launch, paid, no trial** (`app_create_shop`, 0008); partner-built stores
(`app_create_partner_shop`, 0023) open with no owner and are handed over; autopay and the billing banner (B3); plan
changes with "an upgrade waits for the trial" (B4).

## Design (phases; each opens with its mockup — TR1's is the one to approve now)

### The rules (all phases)
1. **Length: N days from the day the shop OPENS** (first publish; N = Admin's setting, 7–90, default 30), never more
   than **N + W days from sign-up** (W = days to open, setting 0–30, default 15; so 45 by default). Building
   the catalogue is not spent trial: a brand that takes 10 days to load 80 products still gets 30 days of selling. The
   45-day cap stops "never open, keep a free console". Before opening, the console says "Your 30 days start when you
   open your shop."
2. **No mandate to start.** Autopay is asked for from day 1 but required only at the end. Reasons: CCPA risk above;
   ~30% mandate drop-off; no-mandate trials bring 3–4× the starts (First Page Sage, Sep 2025, older than 12 months,
   unverified). Revisit after 3 cohorts if trial → paid is under 15%.
3. **Which plan:** the brand picks Launch, Growth or Scale at sign-up; the trial is on that plan, with all its
   features. Default selection: **Growth** (shows inventory sync, Instagram DMs, the analytics that prove problem 6).
   Downgrade during a trial: keeps the trial (B4). Upgrade during a trial: waits until it is over (owner, 2026-11-08).
4. **The per-order fee during the trial — OWNER CALL (Q1).** Recommended: **waived on orders SHIPPED during the
   trial, up to the plan's included orders** (150 / 1,500 / 5,000); charged as usual beyond. The fee rate is the plan
   the order shipped under (decided), so "during the trial" = shipped while the trial ran. Cost to Frrata at the
   hypothesis (Launch, 40 delivered orders × ₹1,000 net × 2%) ≈ ₹800 per converted trial; `unit-economics-reviewer`
   runs on it. The alternative (fee charged from settlements while the plan is free) is simpler and is what Dukaan's
   model implies.
5. **The end — OWNER CALL (Q2).** Recommended:
   - **Autopay set up** → the trial ends into the paid plan; the first invoice charges the plan by days from the end.
   - **No autopay** → **7 days of grace**: everything works, a banner counts down, Pay now works. Then **paused**: the
     shop shows "Taking a short break — back soon" with WhatsApp contact, and accepts **no new orders**; orders already
     placed are shipped, delivered, returned and paid out as normal (never strand a shopper's order); the console is
     open for those orders, products are read-only, and Plan and billing is open to resume. Data is kept **180 days**,
     then deleted after two notices. Resume = set up autopay or Pay now → open again at once.
   - Shopify freezes at the end; Squarespace takes the site offline. A COD brand's shop going dark mid-campaign costs
     real sales, hence the grace week.
6. **One extension, once, 7 days.** Offered (a) by the owner from the expiry banner when the shop has fewer than 3
   delivered orders ("You have not seen a delivered order yet — take 7 more days"), or (b) by Frrata staff in Admin
   with a reason. Never restarted, never stacked.
7. **One trial per business.** A trial is refused (the shop starts paid, and the page says so plainly) when any of these
   has had one before: the owner's verified email, the owner's phone (OTP-verified at sign-up — new), the GSTIN, the
   Razorpay settlement account (sub-merchant KYC — the strongest key, known once payments are connected; a later match
   ends the trial at once with a notice). Stored as **salted hashes only** (`trial_claims`), never the values.
8. **Reminders** (each says the date, the plan, the price with GST, and two equal buttons — "Set up autopay" and
   "Not now"; CCPA: decline as visible as continue): console banner always during the last 10 days; day −7, −3, −1 and
   end-of-grace by **email** (needs the email provider — the open owner call from billing B3) and a **WhatsApp utility
   template** (TR2; Meta pricing per frata-integrations, dated). The RBI pre-debit notice is the bank's, on top.
9. **Partner-built stores:** the trial clock starts at **handover** (the owner's first sign-in), not at build — the
   partner's build time is not the brand's trial. A partner may not start a second trial for the same GSTIN.
10. **The open moment** is `shop_trials.started_at`, set once (closing a shop nulls `published_at`; the clock never
    restarts). Every trial end is the end of a day in IST. A provisional end (sign-up + window + length) is written at
    sign-up and recomputed by ONE definer whenever any input changes (plan § A1). The month a trial ends in is billed in
    arrears on the next invoice; nothing is debited during a trial (plan § A2).
11. **Trial state is derived, never stored** (frata-architecture § Derive): `trialState(now, signedUpAt, openedAt,
    plan row, extension, mandate, payments)` → `building | trial | ending | grace | paused | paid` in
    `src/domains/plans/trial.ts`, pure, unit-tested on a calendar of cases. `tenant_plans.trial_ends_at` stays the one
    stored fact; it is set when the shop opens (30 days, capped at sign-up + 45) by a definer, and extended once by
    another.

### Screens (TR1 mockup)
1. **/start** — step "Choose your plan": three cards, Growth preselected, each "30 days free from the day your shop
   opens · then ₹x/month + GST · y% per delivered order". Under them: "No payment needed to start." Phone with OTP.
2. **Console, building** — Home card: "Your 30 days start when you open your shop" with a 3-step checklist (products,
   payments, open) — the existing open-shop checklist, with the trial line added.
3. **Console, trial** — a quiet line in the shell header: "Growth trial · 23 days left" → Plan and billing. Home card
   "First proof": your first delivered order and what it kept (appears once one is delivered).
4. **Console, ending (≤ 10 days)** — the banner (B3's billing banner, a trial variant): "Your Growth trial ends on
   18 December. Then ₹5,899/month incl. GST. [Set up autopay] [Not now]".
5. **Plan and billing in a trial** — the trial's timeline (signed up → opened → ends → first bill), the plan, change
   plan (upgrade waits for the end, B4), autopay, "Take 7 more days" when eligible.
6. **Grace and paused** — the banner in its danger tone with the day count; the shop's paused page; "Resume" on
   Plan and billing.
7. **Admin › Trials** — the list: shop, plan, opened, days left, delivered orders, autopay yes/no, state; extend once
   with a reason; end now; a cohort funnel (TR3).

## Design review (frata-planning § 3)
- **UI/UX** — one primary action per state (open the shop → see first proof → set up autopay). States: building, trial,
  ending, grace, paused, paid, refused (second trial), staff, support visit (no banners, as B3), phone width. After
  every action the banner and Plan and billing say the new state. Decline equal to continue (CCPA). Keyboard and
  labels: the banner's two buttons are real buttons. Built from the B3 banner and the open-shop checklist.
- **Tenancy** — `trial_claims` is platform data (no tenant_id; hashes only; written by definers; no app grant);
  every trial read is through `src/lib/queries/` under `withTenant`; the open-sets-the-clock and extend definers are
  bound to `app_current_tenant()` and an owner/admin member; Admin's extend and end through `frata_admin`. Proof rows in
  `scripts/verify/tenancy.sql`. `tenancy-auditor` on the plan.
- **Modularity** — engine `src/domains/plans/trial.ts`; reads `src/lib/queries/billing.ts`; the daily run beside the
  collection run (`src/lib/billing/`); banner in `_components/billing-banner.tsx`; the paused shop in the storefront's
  shop-state gate (the existing closed/draft state).
- **SSOT / DRY** — stored: `trial_ends_at` (one row), `opened_at` (exists: first publish), the extension (a definer
  writes a new `trial_ends_at` and an audit row), claim hashes. Derived: the state, days left, eligibility to extend,
  grace end (= trial end + 7 days), pause. Prices with GST from `src/domains/billing` (one GST rule).
- **YAGNI** — no paid bridge (₹20 months), no money-back guarantee, no trial for add-on modules beyond what exists, no
  per-trial custom lengths except Admin's one extension. Signal for the ₹20 bridge: trial → paid under 15% after 3
  cohorts.
- **KISS** — the simplest version: a 30-day `trial_ends_at` set at opening, a derived state, a banner, a daily run
  that pauses unpaid shops after 7 days of grace.
- **Scalability** — 500 trials at once: the daily run reads one row per shop (indexed on `trial_ends_at`); claims
  lookups by unique hash.
- **Performance** — the banner and trial line read through one `cache()`d trial reader per request (B3's banner reads `billing_notices`; one reader orders the two); the paused-shop check
  rides the storefront's existing shop-state read (cached, tag `shop:<id>`), invalidated when the state changes.
- **Reliability** — reminders are outbox rows (ADR-0006), once per (shop, kind) by dedupe key; the daily run is
  idempotent; a pause is a derived state, so a late run never double-pauses; resuming is immediate on payment.
- **Security** — OTP for the phone (rate-limited, failing closed); hashes salted with a server secret (one owner
  module, `check:config`); "end now" and "extend" are ADMIN_ONLY and audited; a support visit cannot extend.
- **Unit economics** — Q1 changes Frrata's revenue during a trial; the trial itself costs Vercel/Supabase pennies and
  WhatsApp utility messages (≈ 3 per trial). `unit-economics-reviewer` runs on Q1 and the targets above.
- **Motion and visuals** — no new motion: the banner and the trial line use the existing banner and chip transitions
  (frata-design-language § Motion); the paused shop page is static.
- **Blast radius** — `/start` (sign-up), `app_create_shop` (no longer always paid Launch), the storefront gate (paused),
  the billing run (fees waived in a trial if Q1 says so: `feeForOrder` takes the trial into account — the one rule),
  B3's banner, partner handover. Footgun: never pause a shop with an order on its way — pausing stops *new* orders only.

## Out of scope (and what brings each back)
- A paid bridge (₹20/month for 3 months, Shopify-style) — trial → paid under 15% after three cohorts.
- A money-back guarantee instead of a trial — if trial abuse costs more than it converts.
- Requiring a mandate to start — a lawyer's clearance under the CCPA guidelines and trial → paid under 15%.
- Trials for Scale's custom price (agreed with Frrata) — sales-led, by hand in Admin.

## Owner calls (all answered 2026-11-08 — see § Owner decisions)
- **Q1** Fee on orders during the trial: waived up to the plan's included orders (recommended), or charged as usual?
- **Q2** At the end without autopay: 7 days' grace then paused (no new orders, data kept 180 days) — recommended — or
  pause at once?
- **Q3** Length: 30 days from opening, capped at 45 from sign-up (recommended) — or a flat 14 days from sign-up
  (the most common SaaS length, ChartMogul)?
- **Q4** Phone OTP at sign-up (needed for one-trial-per-business; costs an SMS or WhatsApp authentication message)?
