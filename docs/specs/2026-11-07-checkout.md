# Checkout and payments (1C): bag, one-page checkout, Pay on delivery and Razorpay, the shopper's order page
Status: built · Date: 2026-11-07 · Mockup: https://claude.ai/artifact/Cd4FETBGGfKv2JovVkZzTN · Approved: owner, 2026-11-07 ("approved, go with your recommendations": owner calls 1–5 as recommended)

Pincode check deferred (owner, 2026-11-06 and 2026-11-07). No email (owner,
2026-11-06). No shopper OTP until DLT registration (Phase 2).

## Goal
Ranked problem 4 (mobile checkout conversion) and the Phase 1 measure "first
real order". It also lays the rows problem 1 (COD/RTO) and problem 6 (true
profit) read: every order stores what it was sold for and what it cost.
Measures: a shopper on a phone gets from bag to "Order placed" in under 60
seconds with 7 visible fields; 0 orders lost or charged twice in the stress
tests; no order whose total differs from what the shopper last saw.

## Job
A shopper who picked a size buys it on their phone, paying online or on
delivery, without an account, and keeps a link that tells them where their
order is. The merchant connects their own Razorpay account once and opens
their shop.

## Research (sources and dates under § Facts used)
- **Baymard, 2026** — abandonment is led by extra costs (40%) and a total not
  shown up front (12%); 7–8 fields; guest only; labels above, one column; say
  why the phone is asked; hide address line 2; delivery as a date range; − / +
  quantity; the total visible before payment details; payment methods each
  with their conditions shown before the final step; collapsed review of what
  was entered. **Taken:** all of it.
- **Shopify one-page checkout (2026-10-17)** — contact → delivery → payment on
  one page, the total updated live; stock held only from payment, re-checked at
  every step; order status page at a tokenised link with address and payment
  redacted on a bare link. **Taken:** the order, live totals, no hold in the bag,
  the two-level order page (masked details).
- **Shiprocket Fastrr, Shopflo, GoKwik, Razorpay Magic (2026; undated where
  noted)** — phone first; COD kept and steered with price (COD fee and/or a
  prepaid saving) rather than hidden; partial COD; a "Pay now and save" nudge
  after a COD order; COD blocked for high-risk shoppers by a server decision.
  **Taken:** phone first, the COD fee shown on its card. **Left for later:**
  prepaid saving (owner call 2), partial COD, the after-order nudge (Phase 2,
  with WhatsApp), risk-based COD blocking (1D's risk rules first).
- **CCPA dark-pattern orders (Bar & Bench 2026-09-01; Legalmantra 2026-08-08)**
  — drip pricing (fees or GST appearing at checkout), basket sneaking,
  pre-ticked consent are being fined (Zepto ₹7 lakh, Dec 2025). **Taken:** every
  charge visible in the bag before checkout; prices shown GST-inclusive stay
  inclusive; nothing pre-ticked; COD and Online cards of equal weight.
- **DPDP Rules 2025 (notified 2025-11-13; notice and consent from 2027-05-13)**
  — a notice beside collection: the data, its purpose, how to withdraw and
  complain. **Taken:** built now, one short notice under the address, written
  from the shop's identity; marketing would be a separate unticked opt-in (none
  in 1C).
- **Razorpay (docs, lastmod 2026-10-08 … 2026-11-04)** — create the order on the
  server; verify `hmac(OUR order id | payment id, key secret)`; fulfil on the
  webhook too (`payment.captured`, `payment.failed`, `order.paid`); webhooks by
  API only for partners, so a merchant with pasted keys adds the webhook in
  their own dashboard; no documented order expiry and the Checkout `timeout`
  is not reliable, so the server owns expiry. **Taken:** all of it (see Design).
- **Code** — `orders` exists as a stub (number, method, net revenue, cogs,
  timestamps; `orderState` derives state; `profit.ts` reads it);
  `webhook_events` and the env-secret Razorpay route exist (to be replaced by a
  per-shop route); `tenant_counters` makes order numbers; `rate_limits`;
  `src/domains/checkout/delivery.ts` (shipping, COD offered and why);
  `availability()`; `shopProduct`/`shopCatalogue` loaders; the product page's
  Add to bag stub. Settings already has Pay on delivery rules and a
  "Ready for live payments" list.

## Design (mockup: see link at top)

**Shopper (storefront look, phone first)**
1. *Add to bag* → a bag drawer slides in: the line, a − / + stepper, Remove,
   the bag total with delivery and (if COD is on) the COD fee in words,
   "Checkout" and "Keep shopping". `/bag` is the same component as a page.
   The bag lives in the browser (variant ids and quantities); prices and
   stock are always the server's. Nothing is reserved in the bag.
2. *Checkout* `/checkout`, one page, one column, labels above:
   - **Mobile number** (10 digits, `+91` fixed) — "The courier calls this
     number to deliver." No email asked: Phase 1 sends none (owner).
   - **Delivery address**: full name, pincode, house and street, area (one
     line, "Add landmark" link), city, state (a list). City and state fill from
     the pincode where our pincode directory knows it (no serviceability claim).
   - **Payment**: two equal cards, each with its own total —
     "Pay online · UPI, cards, netbanking · ₹2,490" and "Pay on delivery ·
     ₹2,539 (includes ₹49 Pay on delivery fee)". A card that cannot be used says
     why on itself ("Pay on delivery is available up to ₹3,000"). Default:
     owner call 1.
   - **Review**: items (photo, size, colour, qty, price), delivery charge, COD
     fee, total, "Arrives 11–15 Nov" (dispatch + metro…rest days from today,
     IST), the return window in words; the DPDP notice; **Place order · ₹2,539**
     in a sticky bar that always shows the total.
   - Recomputed on the server on every change of payment method and before
     placing; if anything changed: the line says "Price changed from ₹2,290 to
     ₹2,490" / "Only 1 left — quantity set to 1", the button reads the new total
     and needs a fresh tap.
3. *Place order*:
   - **COD** → order placed → order page.
   - **Online** → order placed as *awaiting payment* (stock held 20 minutes) →
     Razorpay Checkout opens (prefilled name and phone, UPI Intent on phones) →
     paid → order page. Closed or failed → back on the filled checkout with
     "Payment did not go through. Nothing was charged." and both cards again
     (choosing COD switches the SAME order to COD; no duplicate).
4. *Order page* `/orders/<token>` (128-bit token, unguessable, noindex): order
   number (`KS-1001`), state in words ("Placed — the shop confirms it soon" /
   "Paid" / "Confirmed" / "Cancelled"), items, total, how it is paid and, for
   COD, "Keep ₹2,539 ready", the arrival range, the return window,
   "Message Kesar Studio on WhatsApp" (prefilled "Order KS-1001"), and "Save
   this page: it is your receipt" with Copy link. Address and phone are shown
   masked ("…3210", "Jaipur 302001") — the page is a link anyone may be sent.

**Merchant (console look)**
5. *Settings → Online payments* (new section): status in words (Not connected ·
   Test mode · Live · Needs attention). Connect: paste Key ID and Key secret →
   **Test and connect** (a read-only call; wrong keys refused before saving;
   test or live read from the prefix). Then the webhook step, done once in
   Razorpay's dashboard, written out: the URL to paste (ours, per shop), the
   secret to paste (we generate it and show it once), the three events to
   tick, and "Waiting for Razorpay's first event…" → "Working · last event
   2 min ago". Disconnect keeps past orders.
6. *Home → Open your shop*: the checklist's last step becomes a button when a
   product is Active, a way to pay works, and the business details are filled:
   **Open my shop** (confirm: "Shoppers can order from kesar-studio.frata.store
   now"). Close again from Settings → Store (orders kept).
7. *Orders*: the existing list shows new orders (number, name, items, total,
   payment and state); awaiting-payment and abandoned orders are hidden from
   "To confirm" (shown under a filter). The queue and order page are 1D.

**Owner calls (recommendation first)**
1. **Default payment card**: *none selected* (recommended — no pre-selection
   reads as neutral under the CCPA orders; one extra tap) · Pay online · Pay on
   delivery.
2. **Prepaid saving** ("save ₹50 paying online"): *not in 1C* (recommended —
   it is a merchant setting with a margin cost; add it with the after-order
   nudge in Phase 2 when WhatsApp can send the link) · build now as a setting.
3. **Unpaid online order hold**: *20 minutes* (recommended — Razorpay's shortest
   capture window is 12 min; a UPI app switch and back fits) · 15 · 30.
4. **Order page shows**: *masked address and phone* (recommended) · full
   details for 1 hour in the browser that placed it, masked after.
5. **Who confirms a paid order**: *confirmation applies to COD only; a paid
   order is ready to pack* (recommended) · both wait in "To confirm".

## Data (one migration pair; every table RLS + policy + proof case)
- `orders` gains: `token_hash` (sha256 of the link token, unique), `idempotency_key`
  (unique per tenant), `customer_name`, `phone` (10 digits), `address_line`,
  `landmark`, `pincode`, `city`, `state`, `subtotal`, `shipping`, `cod_charge`,
  `gst_total`, `total` (all paise), `prices_include_gst`, `razorpay_order_id`
  (unique), `razorpay_payment_id`, `paid_at`, `pay_by` (prepaid hold expiry),
  `payment_failed_at`, `switched_to_cod_at`. `net_revenue` = total − GST (what
  `profit.ts` reads: what the customer pays, excluding GST; the COD fee is
  revenue that offsets the courier's COD cost), set by `checkout/quote.ts`.
  State gains `awaiting_payment` (prepaid, `paid_at` null, now < `pay_by`) and
  `payment_abandoned` (prepaid, unpaid, past `pay_by`) in `orderState`, both
  derived.
- `order_lines`: order, variant, product title, option values, SKU, image base
  (snapshots), unit price, compare-at, qty, **unit cost** (server only, for
  profit), GST rate in basis points, HSN.
- `payment_accounts` (one per shop): key id, key secret and webhook secret
  encrypted (AES-256-GCM, key from `PAYMENT_KEYS_SECRET` via HKDF; never sent to
  a browser, never logged), mode test/live, webhook key (random, in the URL),
  connected_at, last_event_at, needs_attention reason.
- Definer function `app_payment_webhook_for(key)` → tenant id and the encrypted
  webhook secret (the webhook arrives with no tenant; ADR-0006).
- No `customers` table: repeat shoppers are found by `(tenant_id, phone)` (index).

## Engines (pure, tested)
- `checkout/quote.ts`: lines (from server rows) → subtotal, shipping
  (`delivery.ts`), COD offered and fee, GST per line, total per payment method,
  what changed against the shopper's last-seen total. One function for the
  bag, the checkout, place-order and the order record.
- `tax/gst.ts`: apparel and footwear (HSN chapters 61, 62, 64): 5% if the sale
  value per piece is ≤ ₹2,500, else 18% (Notification 9/2025-CT(R), w.e.f.
  22 Sep 2025); GST-inclusive prices back-computed; shipping and the COD fee
  follow the line's rate as one composite supply (unverified — see Facts; the
  owner's CA confirms before the first invoice in 1D).
- `checkout/address.ts`: phone, pincode, name, line shapes (Zod), state list.
- `orders/order-state.ts`: the two new derived states.
- `crypto/secret-box.ts` (lib, not domain: it reads the env key).

## Routes (all on the shop host, tenant from `requireShop()` = Host header)
- `POST /api/shop/quote` — bag lines → quote (no writes). Rate-limited per IP.
- `POST /api/shop/orders` — place: Zod body (variant ids, quantities,
  address, method, expected total, idempotency key); in ONE transaction:
  release this shop's expired holds, lock variants in id order, re-quote,
  refuse on a changed total (returns the new quote), decrement stock where
  `stock >= qty` (or pre-order cap), take the order number, insert order +
  lines. Prepaid: after commit, create the Razorpay order (`fetchWithTimeout`,
  merchant's keys) and return its id; failure → "Online payment is not working
  right now" and the order stays switchable to COD. Rate limits per IP and per
  phone per shop.
- `POST /api/shop/orders/pay` — verify the Checkout signature with OUR stored
  razorpay order id; mark paid (conditional on `paid_at is null`).
- `POST /api/shop/orders/cod` — switch an unpaid online order to COD.
- `GET /api/shop/orders/<token>` — the order page's data (masked), `no-store`.
- `POST /api/webhooks/razorpay/<key>` — tenant and secret from the key;
  signature on the raw body; stored under `razorpay:<tenant>:<event>:<id>`;
  answered at once; processed in `withTenant` (amount and our order id must
  match). The env-secret route is removed.
- Console actions: connect/test/disconnect Razorpay; open/close the shop.

## Design review
- **UI/UX** — shopper: one primary action per screen (Checkout · Place order),
  labels above, one column, `autocomplete` on every field, inline validation,
  44 px targets, sticky total, the keyboard never hides the button (the bar
  sits above it); states: empty bag, item gone, price changed, sold out while in
  the bag, COD unavailable (with why), online payment down, payment failed,
  double tap, offline. Merchant: Online payments states (not connected, test,
  live, needs attention: wrong keys, no webhook yet, webhook failing), Open my
  shop with what is missing. Phone width 360 for every screen.
- **Tenancy** — shop routes take the tenant from the Host only
  (`requireShop`); the webhook from our key via a definer function; console
  actions through `asMember`. New tables with composite FKs `(tenant_id, …)`.
  The order page is found by `(tenant_id, token_hash)`: a token from shop A on
  shop B's host finds nothing. Proof cases added.
- **Modularity** — engines above; reads in `src/lib/queries/checkout.ts`
  (named columns; unit cost only in the order insert, never returned); route
  handlers thin.
- **SSOT/DRY** — the quote is computed once (`quote.ts`) and stored on the
  order; the order page, console and profit read the stored order, never
  re-price. Stock is the variant row; holds are the orders themselves
  (unpaid, `pay_by`), not a second table. State is derived.
- **YAGNI** — no customers table, no accounts, no coupons, no prepaid saving,
  no partial COD, no gift wrap, no saved addresses, no OTP, no pincode check,
  no email, no refunds UI (1D cancels; refunds in Razorpay by hand until Phase 2).
- **KISS** — the bag is browser storage plus one quote endpoint; one order row
  is both the hold and the record.
- **Scalability** — festive: place-order locks only the variants in the bag,
  in id order (no deadlocks); the expired-hold release is per shop and
  indexed (`tenant_id, pay_by` where unpaid); the quote is read-only, uncached
  (prices change) and cheap: one query for the bag's variants.
- **Performance** — checkout page static (shell) + one quote call; place-order
  one transaction plus, for online, one Razorpay call after commit.
- **Reliability** — Razorpay down → the order is kept, the shopper can switch
  to COD; webhook and browser race → one conditional update; a capture after
  the hold expired re-reserves if stock allows, else the order reads "Paid ·
  out of stock · refund" for the merchant; every outbound call timed out (8 s).
- **Security** — Zod on every body; prices never from the browser; signatures
  verified with timing-safe compare; secrets encrypted, never returned;
  `check:actions` lists each shop route with `requireShop` and the webhook with
  its key; per-IP and per-phone limits; the order token stored hashed.
- **Unit economics** — Frata's fee is on delivered orders (decided 2026-11-06);
  the gateway fee is the merchant's own Razorpay contract; no new Frata cost
  except function calls. Run `unit-economics-reviewer`.
- **Blast radius** — the product page's Add to bag, `orderState` (every order
  list), the webhook route (replaced), `asMember`, the Home checklist,
  `check:actions`, `check:tenant`. Shops are all closed until a merchant opens
  theirs, so a shopper meets this only after Open my shop.

## Review findings and decisions (2026-11-07) — these win over the text above

**Tenancy audit (no leak by design; seven HIGH gaps closed here).**
- *Order lookups:* `/orders/pay`, `/orders/cod` and the order page find the order
  ONLY by the link token: `(tenant_id, token_hash)`; switching to COD also
  needs `paid_at is null`. A wrong shop and a missing token answer the same 404.
- *Idempotency:* the browser's key is 128 random bits; stored with a hash of
  `(phone, lines, method)`. A replay returns the earlier order only when that
  hash matches, else 409. Unique on `(tenant_id, key)`; `razorpay_order_id`
  unique on `(tenant_id, …)` too.
- *requireShop:* `surfaceFor()` first — 404 unless the Host is a shop host of
  the right shape; then the cached host lookup; every `/api/shop/*` refuses a
  shop that is not open (`published_at` null), and the quote accepts only
  variants of Active, listed-or-unlisted products of that shop.
- *Who connects payments:* connect, test and disconnect Razorpay are
  `asMember(['owner'])` AND refused during a Frata support visit (support acts as
  owner; it must never be able to redirect a merchant's money). Open/close shop:
  `CAN_MANAGE`. The webhook secret is shown once, never cached, never in an
  audit row's detail.
- *Webhook processing:* the receiver fills `webhook_events.tenant_id` from
  `app_payment_webhook_for(key)`; the processor works in `withTenant(row.tenant_id)`
  only, never trusting `notes` or anything else in the payload; it matches our
  stored `razorpay_order_id`, `amount = orders.total` and INR, then one
  conditional update. Processing runs right after storing (in the same request,
  after the 200 is decided) and again from the daily cron for anything missed.
- *Costs and secrets in replies:* the order insert lives in one write module
  returning named columns; `check:tenant` rule 6/6b extends to
  `src/app/api/shop/**` and `src/lib/queries/checkout*` (sentinels, no bare
  `select()`/`returning()`), and `keySecret`/`webhookSecret` join the sentinels
  for those paths.
- *Encryption:* AES-256-GCM with AAD = `tenant_id ‖ field ‖ version` (a
  ciphertext moved to another row will not decrypt); `frata_admin` no grant;
  console reads name status columns only; `app_payment_webhook_for` has
  `search_path ''`, returns `(tenant_id, webhook_secret_enc)` only, revoked from
  public/anon/authenticated. Test-mode keys do not count as "a way to pay" for
  Open my shop.
- *Platform tables:* the `webhook_events` (and `outbox`) read policy becomes
  `app_current_tenant() is null or tenant_id = app_current_tenant()` before
  payloads carry shopper details.
- *Rate limits:* keys `shop:<tenant>:place:ip:<ip>` and
  `shop:<tenant>:place:phone:<sha256(phone)>` — never a plain phone.
- *Order page:* static shell + `no-store` fetch, never `shopCached`; no third-
  party scripts on `/orders/*`.
- *Also:* composite FK `order_lines(tenant_id, variant_id)`; the env-secret
  webhook route and its ENTRY_POINTS entry removed; Razorpay bodies and keys
  never logged.
- *Proof cases:* order_lines and payment_accounts isolation; a token hash from
  B finds nothing under A; idempotency key unique per tenant only; cross-tenant
  variant FK refused; `app_payment_webhook_for` shape and grants; `frata_admin`
  refused; B's webhook_events invisible under A.

**Architecture review (four BLOCKERs, all taken).**
1. *Webhooks are processed at once:* the receiver stores the event, answers
   200, and processes it in `after()` in the same invocation; `/pay` and the
   order page also process pending events for their order; the daily cron is
   only the safety net. A paid order can never sit unseen until tomorrow.
2. *Holds are derived, not released:* available to sell =
   `stock − Σ qty of unpaid online orders with pay_by > now`, computed under the
   variant locks (bag variants only, id order). Stock is decremented only when
   an order becomes real: at placement for COD, at capture for online. Nothing
   is "given back", so nothing can be given back twice, and an expired hold
   frees itself the moment its clock passes. The storefront's availability
   ignores holds (cached; at most 20 min optimistic); checkout is exact.
3. *Switch to COD vs a late capture:* the switch is conditional on
   `paid_at is null` AND first asks Razorpay for the order's payments, refusing
   if any is authorized or captured ("Your payment just went through" → the
   order page). If a capture still lands after a switch, paid wins in the
   derived state and the merchant reads **"Paid online — do not collect cash"**.
   Orders are created with per-order `payment.capture = automatic`, so a
   merchant's manual-capture setting cannot leave a payment authorized only.
4. *Idempotency:* under `pg_advisory_xact_lock(tenant, key)` the key is looked
   up FIRST; a hit with the same body hash returns that order (409 if the body
   differs); only a miss goes on to check stock and insert. The link token is
   recoverable: `HMAC(order id, key derived from PAYMENT_KEYS_SECRET for "order
   links")`, so a retry can hand back the same link; only its hash is indexed.

**HIGH, taken.**
- *Line kinds:* each line stores what it was sold as — `in_stock`, `preorder`
  (with ships-by) or `made_to_order` (with lead days) — and its arrival range.
  Pre-orders taken = Σ qty of live pre-order lines of that variant (the cap's
  count; replaces the storefront's "1C counts them"). A bag asking for more than
  can be sold is cut to what can, said on the line; a line never mixes kinds.
  Place-order refuses through `visibility()` and `availability()` (draft,
  archived, scheduled, sold out), never a second rule.
- *GST:* a shop with no GSTIN charges no GST and its documents are bills of
  supply (1D). With a GSTIN: the ₹2,500 test is on the taxable value per piece
  (an inclusive ₹2,600 is ₹2,476.19 at 5% → 5%); shipping and the COD fee are
  apportioned over the lines by taxable value and take each line's rate; the
  place of supply (the delivery state) and the shop's state (GSTIN's first two
  digits) are stored so 1D prints IGST or CGST + SGST without recomputing.
