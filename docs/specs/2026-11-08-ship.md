# M2·2 · Ship

## Goal
Ranked problems **(1) COD/RTO losses** and **(6) true profit per order**. Today nothing records that an order left,
arrived, failed or came back, or what shipping it cost: every delivered rate and ₹ kept on the platform is empty or
leaves freight out, failed deliveries are nobody's work, and Frrata's per-delivered-order fee has no base.
Measure: (a) the share of packed orders with an outcome 30 days later; (b) failed deliveries acted on the same day;
(c) RTO rate on COD orders, 60 days after a shop connects, against the 60 before; (d) orders whose freight is actual,
not estimated.

## Job
A founder packs an order and ships it from the same screen on a phone: sees the courier and price already chosen,
taps Ship, prints the label, and the pickup is booked. From then on Frrata knows where the parcel is, puts a failed
delivery in front of them while it can still be saved, tells the shopper, and records what the shipment really cost.
A founder who ships some other way marks Shipped, Delivered or Came back in one tap.

## Research (summary; sources dated in the research file)
- **Shiprocket:** per-seller API users, no platform programme; a 10-day token; rates with a recommended courier; AWB,
  pickup, label, manifest, cancel before pickup, tracking, NDR actions in the API; actual freight in the statement by
  AWB; COD remittance not in the API; a tracking webhook the merchant sets in the panel, unsigned, with an optional
  static token, retries and ordering undocumented. Taken: all of it as the integration's shape.
- **Shiprocket panel and app:** Taken: work grouped by next step; NDR rows with one-tap actions; weak addresses flagged
  before a re-attempt; the buyer's past outcomes before shipping. Avoid: proof uploads before a re-attempt.
- **Shopify:** Taken: fulfilment and payment status side by side; actions at the top; "not shipped by us" as its own
  state (our "shipped yourself").
- **Baymard (tracking):** Taken: the expected date first, a progress bar, the courier, the tracking number as a link,
  the scans, what is in the parcel, and the way back to the shop, on the shop's own page.
- **Sendcloud, ShipStation:** Taken: bulk as a background job where one bad order never blocks the rest; exceptions
  raised early (a pickup about to lapse, a parcel gone quiet).

## Phases (each usable on its own; one mockup for all four, signed off together)
- **S·0 · Outcomes by hand, and the regression net** (approved as analytics call A, billable as call E): an order's
  Shipped (courier name and tracking number optional), Delivered, Came back, each with Undo; the orders queue gains
  the shipping tabs; the shop's two shipping costs for orders it ships itself (call S3). No Shiprocket yet.
- **S·1 · Connect Shiprocket and ship an order:** Settings › Shipping › Shiprocket (API user, pickup location,
  webhook); on a packed order, Ship (rates, courier, AWB, label, pickup) and Cancel before pickup; tracking by webhook
  and a sweep, setting shipped, NDR, delivered and RTO dates.
- **S·2 · Failed deliveries and the shopper's tracking:** the Couldn't deliver tab (NDR) with Re-attempt (a date, a phone
  or an address) and Return; the shopper's order page shows the date, the courier and the scans; WhatsApp updates
  through Messages (dispatched, out for delivery, delivered, a failed attempt) where Messages is connected.
- **S·3 · Bulk and actual freight:** Ship up to 50 at once as a background job; the statement sweep writes actual
  forward and return freight and COD fees per order; weight disputes shown on the order.
- **Later:** COD remittance (no API: an uploaded report, or an endpoint if Shiprocket gives one), returns and
  exchanges (phase 4), other couriers.

## Design (mockup)
### The queue, by next step (Orders)
**Couldn't deliver** (failed delivery; first when it has any, with a count) · To confirm · To pack · **To ship**
(packed) · **On the way** (shipped, no outcome) · **Coming back** (RTO, not yet back) · On hold · Not paid · All. Each
tab is an `order-state.ts` state set (packed; shipped; ndr; rto), so no stored status. Today's tiles gain Couldn't deliver
when it has any.

### Ship, on a packed order (S·1)
- One panel under the order's summary. Weight and box are filled from the pieces (variant weight; a default box set
  once in Settings), editable. The courier is chosen (Shiprocket's recommended), with its price and days, and two other
  picks one tap away (cheapest, fastest); the full list below.
- **One primary button that changes with the step:** "Ship ₹62 via Delhivery Surface" → "Print label" →
  (pickup booked for tomorrow by default) → done. Cancel shipment while it has not been picked up.
- Before shipping a COD order: this phone's past orders here (delivered, came back), from our own orders.
- An order Shiprocket refuses (pincode not served, weight) says why and keeps the order To ship.

### Ship it yourself (S·0)
On a packed order: "Shipped another way" (courier name, tracking number, both optional) → On the way → Delivered or
Came back. One tap each, Undo until anything later happens. Orders shipped with Shiprocket are never marked by hand.

### Failed delivery (S·2)
A row per failed attempt with the courier's reason, the attempt count, and the shopper's phone (Call). Re-attempt asks
for one of: a date, a new phone, or a corrected address with a landmark; Return sends it back. Both go to Shiprocket
through the outbox. A row left untouched for 24 h is flagged.

### Settings › Shipping
- **Shiprocket:** three steps, each with what to tick in Shiprocket: (1) make an API user (modules: Orders, Courier,
  Tracking, NDR, Statement; buyer details Allowed) and paste its email and password; (2) choose the pickup location;
  (3) paste Frrata's webhook address and its key into Shiprocket › Settings › API › Webhooks. "Test" proves each step.
- **Shipped another way:** "A delivery costs you about ₹__" and "a return about ₹__", used for orders not shipped
  with Shiprocket.
- **Default box:** length × breadth × height.

### The shopper (S·2)
The shop's order page (`/orders#t=…`): the expected date first, a progress bar (Packed · Shipped · Out for delivery ·
Delivered), the courier and tracking number (a link), the latest scans, the pieces, and the way back to the shop. A
failed attempt says what happens next. No cost, ever.

## Design review
- **UI/UX:** each screen has one primary action (Ship / Print label / Re-attempt / Mark delivered). States: not
  connected (the setup card, and "Shipped another way"); connected but the webhook not seen yet ("tracking starts
  when Shiprocket sends its first update: test it"); rates loading, none served, a refusal with its reason; a label
  not ready; pickup booked, missed; Shiprocket down (the order stays To ship, nothing half-done: see Reliability);
  a token expired (re-login by itself; if the password changed, a banner on Shipping); no permission; phone (sticky
  primary action; cards; no tables). Words: "ship", "on the way", "couldn't deliver", "coming back", never "AWB"
  without "tracking number".
- **Tenancy:** new tables, each with `tenant_id`, grant, RLS and policy in its migration and a proof in
  `verify:tenancy`: `ship_connections` (sealed email and password, the cached token sealed, its expiry, the pickup
  location, the webhook key's hash), `shipments` (one per order's parcel: provider, Shiprocket order and shipment ids,
  AWB, courier, quote, label path, pickup date, latest status id and time, ETA; composite FK to the order). The webhook
  finds the tenant **from the key in its URL** (`/api/webhooks/ship/<key>`; the URL may not contain "sr" or "kr", and
  keys avoid them), never from the body; then every AWB in the payload must be a shipment of that tenant, or the event
  is dropped (Shiprocket sends every event of the account, including orders not shipped through Frrata). The tenant id
  is never taken from a form: Settings acts on `requireMember`'s tenant.
- **Modularity:** `src/domains/shipping/` (pure): the status-id map to our milestones, the package from the pieces,
  the courier pick (recommended, cheapest, fastest), the re-attempt validation, rupees→paise at the edge.
  `src/lib/modules/ship/` (the module, `asModule('ship')`): the Shiprocket client (`fetchWithTimeout`, login and token
  cache, 429 backoff), the outbox kinds (`ship.create`, `ship.pickup`, `ship.cancel`, `ship.ndr`, `ship.track_sweep`,
  `ship.statement_sweep`), the webhook processor. `src/lib/orders/outcomes.ts`: the ONE writer of shipped, NDR,
  delivered and RTO dates, called by the console (actor member) and by the processor (actor system). Core never
  imports the module (`check:modules`); the order page gets the Ship panel through a `<Slot>`.
- **SSOT / DRY:** order state stays derived from its timestamps (`order-state.ts`); `shipments` holds what only the
  courier knows (AWB, ETA, scans' latest), never a second order status. Freight: `orders.forward_shipping` (exists),
  and new `orders.rto_shipping`, `orders.cod_fee` (typed, paise), written by the statement sweep (actual) or, for
  orders shipped another way, left null so `kept.ts` uses the shop's estimate; `profit.ts` reads them. The quote is
  shown, never stored as cost.
- **YAGNI:** no other courier aggregator; no multi-parcel orders (one shipment per order; a split waits for a merchant
  who needs it); no COD remittance; no returns or exchanges; no branded tracking page apart from the shop's order page;
  no label designer.
- **KISS:** Shiprocket's own recommended courier as the default (no courier scoring of our own); one webhook route;
  one writer for dates; a daily sweep for what the webhook misses.
- **Scalability:** a festive peak of 10× (~5,000 shipments a day across shops): webhooks are a row and a 200 (rows
  before requests), processed by the outbox drain; the tracking sweep asks for ≤ 50 AWBs a call, only for shipments
  quiet for 24 h; the statement sweep pages by date. Rate limit ~180 per window per account: per-tenant backoff.
- **Performance:** the Ship panel's rates are fetched once when the panel opens (cached on the shipment for 10 min);
  console lists read our tables only (no Shiprocket call on a list). Indexes: `shipments (tenant_id, awb)` unique,
  `(tenant_id, order_id)`, and the tabs' partial indexes on orders.
- **Reliability (ADR-0006):** each Shiprocket write is an outbox row first, in the transaction that records the
  intent on the shipment; the panel shows "Shipping…" until the row is done and the AWB is back. Creating the order
  and assigning the AWB are separate idempotent steps keyed by our shipment id (a retry finds the Shiprocket order by
  our `order_id` before creating another). **Webhook: an ADR-0006 exception** (Shiprocket has no signature): the key
  in the URL is a 32-byte random value per shop, compared in constant time and stored hashed, plus Shiprocket's
  `x-api-key` set to a second value; the body is stored raw in `webhook_events` under `shiprocket:awb:status:time`
  and answered 200 at once. Events apply in `current_timestamp` order: an older status never overwrites a newer one.
  A sweep tracks shipments quiet for 24 h. Cron on Hobby is once a day; the outbox also drains in `after()`.
- **Security:** Settings actions `asMember(CAN_MANAGE)` (owner, admin); Ship, Cancel, Re-attempt and Return
  `asMember(ROLES[x])` from `work.ts` (packers ship; managers re-attempt, return, mark delivered and came back);
  partners never ship. Credentials: sealed with a new `SHIP_KEYS_SECRET` (server only, like `PAYMENT_KEYS_SECRET`),
  never returned to the browser, never logged; a wrong password is reported as "Shiprocket did not accept these" with
  nothing stored. The label is fetched by the server and served from our route with the member check, never a public
  Shiprocket URL in the page. The webhook route accepts ≤ 64 KB JSON.
- **Unit economics:** Frrata pays nothing per shipment (Shiprocket bills the merchant's wallet). Frrata's fee base
  becomes real (per delivered order; hand marks billable, call E). The merchant pays Shiprocket's plan (free to ₹799 a
  month) and freight; Frrata saves them the Shiprocket-for-Shopify channel fee and a separate tracking tool. Run
  `unit-economics-reviewer` on the plan.
- **Blast radius:** `order-state.ts` (unchanged states, new manual writers), the queue tabs and their counts,
  `order_events` kinds (shipped, ndr, delivered, rto, undone), the order page (a slot), the shopper's order view and
  `/api/shop/orders/view`, Best sellers (delivered units: refresh the catalogue after a delivery), `erase.ts`
  (shipments of erased orders: AWB kept, address never stored here), Today's tiles, cancel (a shipped order can't be
  cancelled; cancel before pickup cancels the shipment first), invoices (unchanged; an RTO credit note waits for
  phase 4 and a CA), RTO Shield (M2·3 reads shipments' outcomes later). Footguns: a webhook for an order shipped from
  the Shiprocket panel directly (unknown AWB: dropped); an AWB reassigned (keep one shipment, update its AWB with
  history); a merchant who changes the API user's password (token refresh fails: banner, ships pause, nothing lost).

## Owner calls
- **S1. Connect with the merchant's own Shiprocket API user** (recommended: there is no platform programme; Frrata
  seals the credentials as it does Razorpay keys) · wait for written partner terms from Shiprocket (business track
  item 3, open since 6 Nov). Either way, ask Shiprocket in writing before the first real merchant connects.
- **S2. Tracking by Shiprocket's webhook, with a daily sweep** (recommended; the merchant pastes our address once; an
  ADR-0006 exception for an unsigned webhook, held by a secret key in the URL and a token) · the sweep alone (no setup
  step, but dates arrive up to a day late and the failed-delivery work loses its same-day window).
- **S3. Two shipping costs for orders shipped another way** (recommended: "a delivery about ₹70, a return about ₹70";
  Shiprocket orders use actual freight) · label those orders "before shipping costs".
- **S4. Shiprocket's recommended courier as the default** (recommended: Shiprocket weighs price, speed and the
  courier's record; ours would need data we don't have) · always the cheapest.

## Out of scope (and the signal that brings each back)
- COD remittance: Shiprocket adds an endpoint, or merchants ask to upload the report.
- Returns and exchanges by the shopper: phase 4 (fit and returns).
- Couriers other than Shiprocket: a merchant on another aggregator asks twice.
- Split shipments: an order too big for one box.
