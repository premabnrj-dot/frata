# U·4 · Home becomes "Today"
Status: built · Date: 2026-11-07 · Mockup: https://claude.ai/artifact/L5qYPvRitAarnX4DxJvdSd · Approved: owner, 2026-11-07 (calls 1–4 as recommended: 1a, 2a, 3a, 4a)

Parent: `docs/specs/2026-11-07-ux-foundation.md` § U4 (owner call 5, 2026-11-07: "yes, as U4 after the queue").
1D·4b (queue tabs, `queueCounts`) and 1D·4c (the order page) are on main, so U·4 can link to both.

## Goal

Ranked problem **(1) COD/RTO losses**. A COD order confirmed within the hour ships before the buyer cools; a
high-risk one is caught before it ships. Today, once the shop is open, Home shows only a finished checklist
(stress test S11): the merchant learns nothing from it and must open Orders to see whether anything waits.

**Measure.** From Home, **one tap opens the oldest order waiting to be confirmed** (e2e). Later, when there is
traffic: the median time from placed to confirmed, read from `confirmed_at − placed_at` (no new column).

## Job

A merchant opening the Console on their phone between other work wants to know, in one look, whether anything
needs them now (orders to confirm, to pack, on hold) and to reach it in one tap.

## Design (see the mockup)

**Before the shop opens:** unchanged. The checklist is Home.

**Once it is open, top to bottom:**
1. Header: "Today" with the shop and address beneath (as now).
2. **Three tiles**, in this order (the first gets the most attention): **To confirm**, **To pack**, **On hold**.
   Each is one link to its queue tab and carries one line beneath its count:
   - To confirm: "N high risk" (a danger badge) when N > 0, else the oldest wait ("oldest 3 h").
   - To pack: "oldest confirmed 9:20 today".
   - On hold: "oldest since yesterday".
   - Zero stays in place and says so: "Nothing to confirm" (a quiet tile, still a link).
3. **The next order**, under the tiles when one waits: "Next to confirm: **KS-1043** · Ananya Sharma · ₹2,490 ·
   3 h ago · High risk". One link straight to the order page. This is the one-tap measure.
4. **Last 7 days**: orders placed and their value, with one small bar per day (IST days). No change-against-last-week
   figure while there are fewer than 20 orders a week: "+100 %" from 1 to 2 is noise.
5. **Setup left** (only if a checklist step is still open after opening): one line, "2 setup steps left", linking to
   the checklist, which moves to `/console/setup`.

**States.** Work waiting · all zero ("You're up to date", with the three zero tiles) · first days with no orders yet
("No orders yet. Share your shop:" the address with Copy) · risk could not be read ("Risk unavailable" on the tile,
never "0 high risk", as rule 7 of § Work queues says) · counts could not be read (the tiles say "Couldn't count"
and still link; never a 0) · phone (tiles become three full-width rows, count on the right) · staff (the same; all
three queues are staff work) · a support session (the same, under the support banner).

## Owner calls (recommendation first; owner 2026-11-07: all four as recommended)

1. **"N high risk" on To confirm.** (a) **Count over every waiting COD order** with `riskFactsFor` + `risk()`, one
   query, measured on the stress fixture (300 orders); if it exceeds 150 ms at 500 waiting, ship (b) instead and say
   so. (b) Leave risk off the tile; show the oldest wait only. A partial count ("2 high risk in the newest 50") is
   not an option: it is the S1 bug again.
2. **Last 7 days**: (a) **orders and value placed, with day bars** (as specced). (b) Numbers only. "Delivered" and
   "Returned to you" join when the shipping connector sets `delivered_at`; until then they would always read 0.
3. **The next order line**: (a) **yes**, it makes "one tap to the first order to confirm" true. (b) No; the tile
   opens the queue and the order is a second tap.
4. **Setup steps left after opening**: (a) **one line linking to `/console/setup`**. (b) Keep the whole checklist
   under the tiles.

## Design review

- **UI/UX.** The job: see whether anything waits and reach it. The primary action is the next order line (or the first
  non-zero tile). Every state above is drawn in the mockup at 390 and 1280. Each tile is a real `<a>` named by its
  count ("To confirm, 6, 1 high risk"); tab order follows the reading order. Built from `PageHeader`, `Section`,
  `Badge`, `<Money>`, `<Name>`. Tiles and the next-order card are primitives (`CountTiles`, `CardLink`): `check:scale` keeps card frames in
  `src/components/`.
- **Tenancy.** No new table. Every read goes through `src/lib/queries/` with `withTenant`, and the tenant comes from
  `requireMember()`. A new read, `todaySummary(tenantId, now)`, gets the three oldest-waits, the next order and the 7-day
  placed figures in one query. `tenancy-auditor` runs on it.
- **Modularity.** The 7-day bucketing (IST day boundaries) and the tile wording are pure, in `src/domains/orders/today.ts`
  with unit tests. The read goes in `src/lib/queries/today.ts`, the rendering in `console/_components/today.tsx`.
- **SSOT/DRY.** Counts come from `queueCounts()` only. The nav pill and the tabs read the same predicates, so pill,
  tab and tile agree (an e2e assertion). Risk comes from `risk()` only. Placed means `placedSql`. Money is paise.
  The oldest wait is derived from timestamps.
- **YAGNI.** Left out: profit tiles (Phase 3, `profit.ts`), delivered and returned (shipping connector), a date picker,
  custom widgets (Stripe has them; nobody asked), any suggestion card (the Shopify lesson).
- **KISS.** One page, three links, one line, one small chart in CSS (no chart library: seven `<span>` bars scaled with
  `scaleY`, which is `check:motion`-clean).
- **Scalability / performance.** Two queries on Home, `queueCounts` and `todaySummary`, plus risk for the
  waiting COD orders if call 1(a). Budget: Home under 1 s on the stress fixture, measured in the ux flow (the same
  way the U·0 net times pages).
- **Reliability.** No outbound call. A failed read shows "Couldn't count", logs only `err.name`, and never shows 0.
- **Security.** Read only; no action. `check:actions` is unchanged.
- **Unit economics.** None: no change to fees or costs. `unit-economics-reviewer` is not needed.
- **Blast radius.** `console/page.tsx` (Home), the checklist moving to `/console/setup` (its links in the checklist
  and the "Open my shop" path, plus the 90-ux and checkout flows that open the shop from Home), and `queueCounts`'s
  new reader.

## Out of scope

Profit per order on Home (Phase 3). NDR and RTO tiles (with the shipping connector). Admin's Home (its own job).
Notifications (WhatsApp and push alerts for a waiting order are a separate feature).

## Plan (approved 2026-11-07; architecture-reviewer and tenancy-auditor run on it, findings folded in)

**The brand moment.** It used to live in the checklist on Home, and once Home becomes Today the checklist unmounts
when the shop opens. A client provider `<OpenMoment key={tenantId} address header>` now wraps both branches of Home (and the
`/console/setup` page). It holds `justOpened` and one `role="status"` region that is always there. `router.refresh()`
keeps client state at the same tree position, so the check survives the switch from the checklist to Today. There is no
storage, so nothing can carry the moment into another shop (tenancy finding 1). `OpenShop` tells the provider; it no
longer draws the check.

## As built (2026-11-07), after impact-reviewer and tenancy-auditor on the diff

- **"Oldest" on Home is when an order entered its tab** (`enteredAt`): for To confirm, the switch to COD or the
  placement (or the payment, when a refund is due). The To confirm list sorts by `placed_at`, newest first, so for a
  switched or refund-due order the two can differ. The tile and the next order always agree: they read the same row.
- **One snapshot:** `todaySummary` runs in `withTenant(…, { snapshot: true })`, read-only repeatable read.
- **Closing the shop** lives on `/console/setup`, and Today always links there: "N setup steps left", or "Setup, or
  close the shop for now". The e2e reaches it by a tap.
- **Accepted gaps (owner can overrule):** the risk-timeout path ("Risk unavailable") and the "Couldn't count" page
  have unit tests of their line logic but no end-to-end test, because neither can be caused from the browser stack
  without a fault-injection hook, which is not worth adding here. The 1 s Home budget is asserted locally, not in CI
  (CI machines are slower and shared).
- **Known debt, pre-existing:** "online payments connected" is decided twice. `setupFor` reads `PaymentStatus`
  (connected, live, webhook seen); `openShop` calls `canTakeOnline(keysFor)`, which also needs the key secret to
  decrypt. They can disagree only if a stored secret cannot be decrypted. This belongs to the payments owner (one
  predicate in `src/lib/payments/keys.ts`). `BUILT` is now one copy (`BUILT_PAGES`).
