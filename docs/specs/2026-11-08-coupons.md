# O · Offers: coupons, sales and the prepaid saving
Status: draft · Date: 2026-11-08 · Mockup: https://claude.ai/artifact/HVFMBJzJoH9tTUh9d1zxHT · Approved: —

Research: `docs/research/2026-11-08-coupons.md` (products, law and tax, the codebase; every fact dated there).
Reviews on this spec (2026-11-08): architecture-reviewer, tenancy-auditor and unit-economics-reviewer, folded in
below (§ Design review).

## Goal
- **Problems moved:** (1) COD/RTO losses (a prepaid saving moves orders off COD); (4) mobile checkout conversion (the
  best offer is applied for the shopper, with no code hunting); (6) true profit per order (every offer is judged on
  the profit its delivered orders left).
- **Measures:**
  - The prepaid share and delivered rate, in a 4-week 50/50 test of the prepaid saving in 2–3 design-partner shops
    (split by a stable shopper key). The saving passes only if the shift to prepaid beats that shop's break-even
    (§ Unit economics).
  - The share of merchants running at least one offer.
  - Zero orders where the charged total ≠ the quote.

## Job
A founder starts a festive offer from a phone in under a minute, picking a style ("10% off above ₹1,999", "₹50 off when
you pay online", later "Buy 2, get 1"). The shopper sees it in the bag and at checkout without typing a code. A week
later the founder sees whether it made money on delivered orders. Every merchant has this, on every plan.

## Research (summary)
- **Shopify:**
  - What it does: three classes applied in order (product → order → shipping); per-code limits.
  - Taken: the classes and their order; limits per code.
- **Stripe:**
  - What it does: the terms are separate from the shopper-facing strings. Codes are unique, case-insensitive, never
    reused.
  - Taken: `discounts` (the terms) and `discount_codes` (the strings, limits never looser than the terms).
- **Razorpay Magic Checkout:**
  - What it does: auto-applies the best coupon; greys out locked coupons and says what's needed; prepaid-only coupons;
    a prepaid discount that excludes fees; a money budget.
  - Taken: all of these.
- **Baymard (Jun and Nov 2026):**
  - What it says: a visible code field sends shoppers off to hunt for codes; show free-shipping qualification in the
    cart summary.
  - Taken: "Have a code?" collapsed behind a link, and a progress line in the bag.
- **India's law:**
  - What it requires: no fake urgency or drip pricing; the prior price is the lowest in the last 30 days, from 1 Jan
    2027; never above MRP; no purchase-linked games of chance.
  - Taken: the rules in § Rules.

## Design (see the mockup)

### Where it lives
Offers are **core checkout pricing**, in every plan. It is not a module: `quote.ts`, `place.ts` and `payment.ts` are
core, and `check:modules` forbids core importing module code.
- The engine is `src/domains/checkout/discounts.ts`. It is pure, and named "discounts" in code because "offer" already
  means the COD offer and the module offer.
- Reads are in `src/lib/queries/discounts.ts`; changes are in console `actions.ts`.
- Merchants and shoppers see the word **Offers**.
- The console nav item "Offers" sits under Store, after Look.
- Storefront components render offers directly, as core.
- The themes sections (themes T0) take optional display props from core: `offer` on the announcement bar, `badge` on the card, `offers` in the PDP buy box after the price, `progress` in the bag summary. Each renders nothing without its prop.

### Offer styles (one engine)
| Style | Class | Phase | Shown as |
|---|---|---|---|
| % or ₹ off the order, with a minimum | order | O·1 | bar, bag progress, checkout list |
| Free shipping above ₹X / with a code | shipping | O·1 | bag progress |
| Pay online, save ₹X (or %, capped) | payment | O·1 | checkout method chip, PDP line |
| First order | order (eligibility) | O·2 | welcome bar, checkout |
| % or ₹ off products or collections | product | O·2 (needs price history) | card badge, PDP, struck price |
| Buy X, get Y | product | O·2 | card badge, PDP, bag nudge |
| Tiered by bag value | order | O·2 | bag progress to the next tier |
| Free gift above ₹X | product | O·2 | bag progress, a gift line |

How an offer is triggered and what it needs:
- **Trigger:** automatic, one shared code (O·1), or unique and creator codes (O·2).
- **Conditions:** minimum bag value or quantity; products and collections (O·2); prepaid only; a window.
- **Limits:** total uses, uses per phone (soft: phones aren't verified), and a ₹ budget.

### Business types → what they reach for
| Shop | Reaches for | Avoids |
|---|---|---|
| Instagram-first D2C, COD-heavy | pay-online saving, first order, creator codes, free shipping above AOV | blanket % off |
| Premium / designer / made-to-order | free gift, private codes, early access | % off on the card |
| Value / fast fashion | Buy 2 get 1, tiered bag value, collection sales with a real end | stacking everything |
| Kidswear, innerwear, basics | multi-buy, repeat-buyer code after delivery | first-order only |
| Ethnic / festive / wedding season | scheduled windows, a free-shipping week | fake countdowns |
| Partner building for a client | drafts the same styles; the owner starts them | — |

The create flow opens on "Popular for shops like yours" (from the shop's category and AOV). It is a default, never a
lock.

### How offers combine (the engine, pure)
1. **Eligible** (in the quote, no counting):
   - in its window and not stopped;
   - the bag meets its conditions;
   - the method qualifies.

   The quote **never takes a phone**. First-order and per-phone limits, total uses and budget are checked only at
   placement.
2. **Best per class,** in order: product → order → shipping → payment.
   - At most one per class: the one that saves the shopper most.
   - A code replaces the automatic offer of its class when it saves more. One code per order.
   - The prepaid saving combines unless the merchant says no.
   - "Best per class" is stated as such; it is not a global optimum across classes.
3. **Thresholds:** free shipping and the COD minimum and maximum are tested on the bag **after product and order
   offers, before the payment class**. Paying online never loses free shipping.
4. **Spread:** an order or payment discount is spread across lines by value, to the paise (largest remainder).
5. **Per method:** each line's discount, taxable value, **GST rate (on the discounted price)** and GST, and
   `merchandiseNet`, are computed **per payment method**, in `MethodTotal`. Placement writes the chosen method's
   figures. A ₹2,650 piece can be 18% on COD and 5% online at ₹100 off. CA to confirm.
6. **Locked:** listed offers the bag misses, each with what's needed ("add ₹420", "pay online").

**One repricer:** `reprice(stored lines, stored discount terms, method)` is the only way an existing order is priced
again. It is used by switching to COD, retrying a payment and marking an order paid after a switch.
- On a switch, the shopper is told first: "Paying cash loses the ₹50 saving. New total ₹3,145."
- A retry reprices from the order's own stored terms, not today's offers, and rechecks limits under the lock,
  excluding the order's own use.

### Data (all tenant tables: grant, RLS, policy and a `verify/tenancy.sql` row in one migration)
- **`discounts`** (the terms):
  - class, style, value (bps or paise), cap, minimum, method;
  - window (`starts_at`, `ends_at`), `paused_at`, `ended_at`, `stopped_at` (the Admin kill switch);
  - limits: total, per phone, budget in paise;
  - `listed` (shown in lists) and `combines_with_payment`.
  - Status (Live, Scheduled, Paused, Ended) is derived from timestamps.
- **`discount_codes`:**
  - tenant_id, discount_id, code, own limits (never looser than the terms), `link_only` (O·2).
  - Unique on `(tenant_id, upper(code))` **forever** (Stripe's rule; "live" can't be an index).
  - Shared codes are 4–20 characters. Generated unique codes are at least 10 random characters.
- **`order_discounts`** (what an order got, typed, never jsonb):
  - tenant_id, order_id, discount_id, code_id, class, amount, and the terms copied as columns.
  - Composite foreign keys on `(tenant_id, …)`, and a partial index on `(tenant_id, discount_id)`.
  - Uses, per-phone limits, budget and analytics read this table, joined to order state.
- **Orders:**
  - `orders.discount` is a snapshot of Σ `line_discount`, kept by the `orders_total` CHECK and a verify check.
  - `line_discount` is written.
  - `checkout.onlineTotal` moves to a typed column (O·0), because Razorpay charges and matches against it.
- **`price_history`** (its own catalogue task in O·0):
  - the **effective** price per variant per change, including automatic product offers;
  - it derives the 30-day prior price for every struck price, today's `compareAt` included;
  - MRP becomes its own field.

### Uses, locks and limits
- A use is taken at placement, inside the order's transaction. Only offers **with a limit** are locked (`FOR UPDATE`),
  after the variants, in id order. A sitewide offer with no limit locks nothing, so festive checkouts never queue on
  one row.
- **The budget is never exceeded.** An order that would cross it doesn't get the offer, and the shopper sees the usual
  `changed` answer with the new total.
- A use is released when its order is cancelled before dispatch, or its online payment is abandoned. This is derived
  from order state. A late capture after abandonment can exceed a limit; that is accepted in writing, as `refund_due`
  already accepts it for stock.
- `requestHashOf` includes the code, so a retry with a new code never returns the old order.

### Showcase (what the shopper sees)
- **O·1:**
  - the announcement bar (one offer the merchant picks; an end date only if real);
  - the bag progress line and the applied offer;
  - at checkout, the best offer applied, listed offers and locked ones greyed with what's needed, "Have a code?"
    collapsed, the saving on the method chip, and the total saved;
  - the discount on the order page, invoice and WhatsApp.
- **O·2:** the card badge and the PDP offers block.
- **Caching:** the public display data (label, badge, real end) is cached with the shop (`shopCached`, with the
  tenant in the key, tag `shop:<id>`, purged on every offer change) and can be up to 60 s stale. Every price comes
  from the quote.
- **States:**
  - "Offer ended. New total ₹X" (the `changed` answer before paying);
  - "This code doesn't work here" (one answer for every failure);
  - "Paying cash loses the ₹50 saving";
  - "Free shipping kept";
  - no offers (nothing shown);
  - a budget used up (the offer vanishes from the list).

### Console (merchant)
- **The Offers list:** Live, Scheduled, Paused, Ended. Each row: uses, ₹ given, delivered rate, and **₹ kept per
  delivered order** (from O·1, as the Goal's measure).
- **New offer:** pick a style, then one short form with a live shopper preview, then start now or schedule.
  - The form warns when an offer would mostly go to orders that already qualify: "62% of last month's orders were
    already above ₹1,999: about ₹18,000 would go to them."
- **An offer:** its codes, limits, pause, end (final; never deleted; duplicate to run again), and its numbers.
- **States:** no offers yet (style picker), a budget nearly used, a window ended, stopped by Frrata (with the reason).

### Offer analytics (per offer and per code)
- **O·1:** orders, ₹ given, delivered rate, ₹ kept per delivered order.
- **O·3:**
  - AOV with vs without, prepaid share, new vs repeat buyers, RTO against the shop's non-offer orders;
  - discount ₹ per delivered order;
  - a creator leaderboard by delivered orders;
  - leak alerts (a code used mostly without its link, or at over 3× its 7-day pace).
- True Profit's "by campaign" view and shop analytics (A·1) read `order_discounts`: a code is a source.

### Who controls what
| Can… | Admin | Owner / admin role | Staff | Partner | Shopper |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Draft and edit offers | support mode only | yes | no | yes | — |
| Start, pause, end | support mode only | yes | no | **no**: owner starts | — |
| Codes, limits, budget | support mode only | yes | no | drafts | — |
| See offer analytics | totals only | yes | yes | yes (partners already work orders) | — |
| Export CSV | — | yes | no | no | — |
| Stop an offer or all of a shop's offers (kill switch) | yes, with a reason, logged | own: pause/end | no | no | — |
| Guardrails, plan limits, templates | yes | — | — | — | — |
| See listed offers, apply one code | — | — | — | — | yes |

- **Admin's kill switch** sets `stopped_at` through a column-level grant to `frata_admin` (`stopped_at`, `stopped_by`,
  `stop_reason` only). It has no read on orders or `order_discounts`.
- **Platform totals** (offers live, ₹ given) come from one aggregates-only SECURITY DEFINER function, like
  `app_admin_order_counts` (`search_path ''`, EXECUTE revoked from public).
- **Editing a shop's offer** happens only in audited support mode, through `asMember`.

### Rules, limits and validity
- **Money:**
  - All amounts are paise; percentages are bps.
  - A discount never takes a line below ₹0.
  - Shipping and the COD fee are discounted only by a shipping-class offer.
  - Never above MRP.
- **Window:**
  - Start and end are stored as instants and shown in IST.
  - "Ends in" is shown only within 48 h of a real end, and never restarts per visit.
  - An ended offer never comes back; duplicate it instead.
- **Codes:**
  - Case-insensitive, unique per shop forever, and never looked up outside the host's shop.
  - A creator link lives on the shop's own host, with a host-only cookie. There is never a platform resolver.
- **Guessing:**
  - Code checks have their own fail-closed limit per IP and per shop (tighter than `quoteIp`).
  - Every typed code that doesn't apply gets the same answer, whether it exists or not.
  - Only **listed** offers show locked reasons.
  - Unlisted and unique codes never appear in lists.
- **Merchant numbers stay on the server (ADR-0003):**
  - `publicQuote` maps offers to named public fields only (label, amount saved, what's needed).
  - Budgets, use counts and ₹ figures never reach the browser, and are added to `check-client-bundle`.
- **Prior price (from 1 Jan 2027):** a struck price is the lowest effective price in the last 30 days, from
  `price_history`.
- **Returns (Phase 4):** a returned line refunds what was paid for it. Cancelling a whole order releases the use.
- **Wording:** "Pay online, save ₹50". Never a COD surcharge, and every fee shown before the shopper commits.
- **WhatsApp:** codes go only in marketing templates.
- **No games of chance:** no spin-the-wheel or scratch cards.

### Phases (each with its own mockup if it changes what anyone sees)
- **O·0 Foundation** (nothing visible):
  - the regression net (checkout e2e at every method; quote tests at the GST slab and on switch and retry);
  - `MethodTotal` carries line tax;
  - the one repricer;
  - the `orders.discount` snapshot, the CHECK, `line_discount`, a typed `online_total`;
  - `order_discounts`, the invoice's gross, discount and taxable columns;
  - `price_history` with MRP;
  - CA sign-off on GST order.
- **O·1 Core:**
  - styles: % or ₹ off the order with a minimum, free shipping, the pay-online saving;
  - automatic offers or one shared code; window, limits and budget;
  - the bar, bag progress and checkout list;
  - the console list and create flow, with ₹ kept per delivered order;
  - the 2–3 shop test of the saving.
- **O·2 More:**
  - styles: first order, product and collection offers with struck prices, BXGY, tiers, free gift;
  - unique and creator codes with links;
  - the card badge and PDP block (after themes T0).
- **O·3 Analytics:** the full set above, and leak alerts.
- **O·4 Admin:** guardrails, plan limits, templates, kill switch, platform totals.

## Owner calls (recommendation first)
1. **Offers in every plan (core)** · an add-on. Shopify includes discounts on every plan, and GoKwik's fee includes
   prepaid incentives, so an add-on would make Frrata dearer than the baseline. Plan limits apply only to volume.
2. **One code per order, plus automatic offers, best per class** · several codes.
3. **Free shipping and COD limits tested after product and order offers, before the payment saving** · on the full
   amount.
4. **Auto-apply the best offer and list the rest** · a code field only.
5. **The Frrata fee on what was paid, after the discount** · before it. This is already the documented base. It costs
   Frrata ₹2.40 per delivered order on Launch for a ₹1,200 order at 10% off.
6. **Partners draft offers; the owner or an admin starts them** · partners start them too · owner only.
7. **Price history now (O·0)**, covering today's struck prices too · when the rule starts on 1 Jan 2027.
8. **The prepaid saving lives in Offers (every plan)**, and RTO Shield's "Prepaid nudge at checkout" line becomes "it
   reads your pay-online offer" · keep it inside RTO Shield (plan-billed).

## Design review (2026-11-08)
- **UI/UX:**
  - One primary action per screen: "New offer"; on an offer, "Start" or "Schedule".
  - States are in § Showcase and § Console.
  - Phone first: the list becomes cards; the form is one column with a live preview under it.
  - Keyboard: the style picker is a radio group, and the code field opens with Enter.
  - Norman's checks:
    - the shopper preview is the signifier;
    - ending is final, so pause comes first, and ending asks for confirmation with the count of live codes;
    - the "already qualify" warning prevents the mistake rather than reporting it.
- **Tenancy:**
  - Every lookup runs inside `withTenant(hostTenant)` with an explicit `tenant_id`.
  - Codes are matched on `(tenant_id, upper(code))`; the phone check runs only at placement, on `(tenant_id, phone)`.
    There is no lookup across all shops, and no definer function for phones.
  - Composite foreign keys stop an order pointing at another shop's offer.
  - Admin gets a column grant for the kill switch and a definer function for totals.
  - Run `tenancy-auditor` on the O·0 and O·1 plans.
- **Modularity:**
  - core: `src/domains/checkout/discounts.ts` (pure), `src/lib/queries/discounts.ts`, console `actions.ts`;
  - storefront components render offers as core;
  - True Profit and Analytics read `order_discounts`.
- **SSOT / DRY:**
  - The terms live once, in `discounts`; an order's discounts once, in `order_discounts`, copied at placement
    because the order must keep the terms it was sold on.
  - `orders.discount` is a declared snapshot of its lines, with a verify check.
  - Status is derived; uses are derived from `order_discounts` and order state.
  - The prepaid saving has one home (call 8).
- **YAGNI:** cut are manual discounts on assisted orders (assisted orders don't exist yet; back when they do), a
  global best-combination search (back if merchants run over 25 automatic offers), and A/B testing offers.
- **KISS:**
  - O·1 has three styles, one code per order, and one number (₹ kept per delivered).
  - It adds three tables plus price history, with no new dependency.
- **Scalability:**
  - The quote checks eligibility without counting.
  - Placement counts only offers with limits, on an indexed `(tenant_id, discount_id)`.
  - No sitewide row lock.
  - Price history grows by one row per price change, not per day.
- **Performance:** offers for a shop are read once per quote (cached per tenant for 60 s, purged on change). The
  console reads `order_discounts` joined to order state for 30 days, indexed.
- **Reliability:**
  - No outbound calls, except WhatsApp codes, which use the outbox (marketing templates).
  - The one repricer means a switch, a retry and a payment can never disagree with the order.
- **Security:**
  - Console changes go through `asMember(CAN_BUILD)` for drafts and `asMember(CAN_MANAGE)` for start, pause and end
    (`check:actions`).
  - Code checks have their own limit, and every failure gets one answer.
  - Zod on every input.
  - The Admin kill switch is logged with a reason.
- **Unit economics** (unit-economics-reviewer, 2026-11-08; all hypotheses):
  - **Fee after the discount:** Frrata gives up 2% of the discount on Launch (₹2.40 on a ₹1,200 order at 10% off),
    and ₹1,440 a month for a Growth-shaped shop at 10% off on every order.
  - **Pay online, save ₹50:** costs ₹45 on every prepaid order, including those that would have paid anyway. It gains
    ₹215–345 per order that switches. Break-even needs 4–8 more prepaid orders in 100 (at 30–40% prepaid and 21–30%
    COD RTO). If switchers are low-risk COD buyers (10% RTO), break-even needs about 23 in 100. Hence the test.
  - **10% off above ₹1,999:** a ₹2,490 order loses ₹235 kept and needs 1.3× the delivered orders, so the "already
    qualify" warning matters.
  - **Buy 2 get 1:** +₹368 against a one-piece buyer, −₹990 against a buyer who would have paid for two.
  - **Core, not add-on:** confirmed. Frrata's own cost to run it is small and not yet costed.
- **Blast radius:**
  - quote, place, payment, amounts, invoice, `publicQuote`, `buy-box.tsx` (`codFor` on the discounted price), the
    storefront bag and checkout, the console order page, risk (median of totals), RTO Shield's feature list,
    `check-client-bundle`, the themes slots;
  - e2e for checkout, switch, retry and invoice.

## Out of scope (and the signal that brings each back)
- **Spin-the-wheel and scratch cards:** never, while a purchase-linked game is an unfair trade practice.
- **Gift cards and store credit:** a stored value, with RBI PPI questions; when merchants ask twice.
- **Loyalty points:** when repeat-buyer codes show demand.
- **Bank and card offers:** when Razorpay's Offers docs are readable and a merchant asks.
- **Manual discounts on assisted orders:** when assisted (DM) orders exist.
- **Discounts on marketplaces:** never; offers are for the shop's own storefront.
- **A/B testing offers:** when a shop runs more than about 5 offers a month.
