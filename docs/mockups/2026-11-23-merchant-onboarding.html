In short

Sign-up drops from 5 steps to 3

Brand name, email code, what you sell. The PIN code moves to Settings › Store as the pickup address. The plan is chosen when the store opens.

Setup shows one step at a time

Home leads with the next step only. Short steps open in a popup, or a sheet on a phone. From 16 things typed across 12 screens to 12 across 5.

Retire themes completely. In four stages.

The direction is right and the cost is lowest now. 7 stores exist, 4 have a published look, 2 are open. Themes cannot go until a store owner can get a website without a partner. Nothing builds or hosts a website yet.

Store owners get the website builder, not the partner workbench

First a starter with their brand and content, free, no agent. Later "Ask for a change", priced before it runs. The workbench stays the partner's tool.

Categories become packs

Today category logic sits in about 8 scattered checks and fashion wording. One pack per category carries fields, tax codes, what the law asks on the page, and words.

Needed from you

Eleven calls at the end, each with a recommendation. The first build phase needs calls 1 to 4 only.

Step by step

  1. Brand name. The address is made from it and checked as you type. "Change address" opens a popup; most people never need it.
  2. Email, then the 6-digit code on the same screen. Someone already signed in skips this step.
  3. What you sell. One tap or none. It picks the category packs (see Categories).
  4. Created. The line under the preview runs once (1.3 s, the same sweep the store uses to open). Then Home, on the first setup step.

What moved

  1. PIN code → Settings › Store, as the pickup address. Asked where it is first used.
  2. Plan → the "Open your store" popup. The trial counts from opening, so nothing is lost.
  3. Brand name comes before the email so the first screen already shows their store.

States

Address taken (the chip turns amber and "Use this name" waits), wrong code, a code sent again after 30 s, already has a store (opens it), too many tries (a toast, try in an hour), no connection (a toast with Try again). Step by step

  1. First product in a popup: photo, name, price, sizes from the category pack. "Add more details" opens the full editor with what was typed.
  2. Business details in two short views: legal name, GSTIN and state, then the grievance officer, filled with the owner's own name, phone and email.
  3. How shoppers pay starts done. Pay on delivery is on up to ₹3,000, as today. The row opens the popup to change it. Razorpay is a button to its own page, because keys are tested before they are saved.
  4. Website. Section 3.
  5. Open your store. The plan is chosen here. Then one short screen: the address, Copy link, Share on WhatsApp.

Pickup address

Not a setup step. Nothing a shopper sees reads it today. It is a block in Settings › Store, and Delivery Zones and shipping ask for it in this popup when they first need it. The PIN code fills the city and state.

Popup or page

Today a popup only confirms what loses work, money or stock (Feedback rule 4). This adds one kind: a setup popup, one short job of up to five fields that saves at once. A job with a test step, a long form or many items stays a page: the product editor, Razorpay, GST, the website. A popup never opens another popup. Back closes it. On a phone it is the sheet.

Motion

All from the approved set: popups 200 ms, sheets 320 ms on the spring, the tick drawn in 320 ms, the next step sliding in 16 px over 200 ms, the ring filling in 320 ms. Only opacity and transform move. No confetti.

States

New, part done, ready, open (the card leaves Home), a step refused (the popup's own error line), no permission (Staff see the list without actions), a support visit (read only), a partner-built store before handover (the partner's steps show as done by them).

Step by step

  1. Pick a starter. Three, drawn with the store's own products. No gallery of themes, no settings page.
  2. Brand. Colour, type and logo. These are the Brand record that checkout, emails and invoices already read.
  3. Build. About 2 minutes, named steps, and "Carry on with setup". Home's Website step shows Building.
  4. Ready. It goes live when the store opens. After opening, a new version waits for Publish.
  5. Later changes. Words, banners, menus and pages are edited in Content, free and live. A change to the layout is "Ask for a change". The agent shows what it will change and the price first. Undo returns the credits.

Should store owners use Studio?

Yes, a version made for them, in the console. The partner workbench stays the partner's. Chat, files, versions and client budgets are a builder's tools. More categories
