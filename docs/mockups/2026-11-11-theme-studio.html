Mockup v1 · 2026-11-11 · Spec docs/specs/2026-11-11-theme-studio.md · for the owner's approval

  • Frrata's team builds gallery themes in Admin; partners build themes for their own clients in their portal. Same studio, same blocks, no code.
  • A theme: design system, header and footer, home, product and collection pages, About, Contact and FAQ. Owners fill it in Look, as today.
  • Every theme passes the same checks before it reaches a shop: contrast, headings, photos, limits, the legal block. Bag and checkout stay one tested layout.
