Step by step

  1. A store not yet open: Apply makes the change live, as today, with or without an owner. A partner’s own save does the same.
  2. An open store: Apply sends the change to the owner. The card reads Sent to the owner.
  3. The owner or a Manager sees it on Console › Brand’s review card and in Notices, the same as a partner’s own save.
  4. They Approve or Decline. The card turns to Approved, or Declined by the owner.
  5. One change waits at a time. A newer one, from Studio or from Brand, takes its place. The older card reads Replaced by a newer change.
  6. The agent can’t approve. Only a person can Apply, and only the owner or a Manager can approve.
