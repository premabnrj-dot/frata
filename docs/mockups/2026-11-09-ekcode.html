Scenario

  1. Shop · Continue with EkCode
  2. ekcode.in · phone
  3. Code from WhatsApp
  4. Create (first time only)
  5. Continue to the shop
  6. Back at the shop, filled in

Any 6 digits work. The code auto-submits on the sixth.

Why each choice

  • A real page on ekcode.in, not a pop-up or iframe: the shopper sees EkCode's address in the bar, and it works in Safari and Instagram's browser.
  • The shop's name stays in the header, so the shopper always knows where they go back to.
  • One field per screen, the keypad numeric; the number checks itself as it is typed (10 digits, starts 6–9).
  • The code fills itself (one-time-code: iOS 26 keyboard, paste) and submits on the sixth digit; no Verify button.
  • "Open WhatsApp" on a phone, because a web shop gets a copy-code button only (Meta).
  • Email appears only when needed: at once if the number isn't on WhatsApp, after 20 s if the code hasn't arrived.
  • Consent is its own tap, never pre-ticked (DPDP).
  • The shopper sees exactly what the shop gets and chooses the address; "Not you?" is one tap everywhere.
  • Guest checkout never goes away.

Your calls

1 · "Save this address to EkCode" at checkout Ticked · you, 9 Nov

For a shopper who just created EkCode and types a new address. Recommend: ticked, because they created EkCode to have their addresses kept, and the notice they agreed to says so. The data law forbids pre-ticked consent; this is the use they already agreed to. Counsel confirms with the notice.

2 · Blue tick for EkCode's number Agreed

Meta asks for a verified business, 30 days on the platform and two-step verification. Recommend applying 30 days after the number goes live.

3 · EkCode needs its own name online Agreed

A domain for the sign-in and the shopper's page (ekcode.in here, to buy), an email sender on it for the fallback code, and EkCode shown as a brand of your verified business so Meta approves "EkCode" as the WhatsApp name. The company behind EkCode is named once, in its privacy policy: the data law needs it.
