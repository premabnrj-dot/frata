The answer

Yes, remove the fashion-only limit. Open categories in waves, each one only when its compliance pack is built.

The label doesn't win

Every competitor is horizontal. Depth in a workflow wins, not a category name.

Fashion is the hard pool

Biggest by count, slowest growth, worst RTO.

Partners need one stack

Agencies build for many categories. Fashion-only makes them run two platforms.

But law differs by category

FSSAI, Legal Metrology, BIS, CDSCO, HUID. Open a category before its pack and it's a legal exposure.

Fix now, fashion included

A product's HSN can't be set today. Every line uses the shop's 6211 (garments, 5%). Bags (18%) and jewellery (3%) are taxed at the garment rate. Phase C0 fixes it.

Why it's dearer sometimes

Shopify charges nothing on COD orders. Frrata's 2 / 1 / 0.5% is on every delivered order, so high order values and COD-heavy small shops pay more.

Fix: cap the fee per order

Launch 1.5% up to ₹25 · Growth 1% up to ₹20 · Scale 0.5% up to ₹15. About 4% less revenue for Frrata (break-even ~471 → ~490 shops, scaled; the 471 itself needs rebuilding). Say "less than Shopify + checkout + WhatsApp tool".

What changes, for whom

Merchant

  • Sign-up asks the category; it sets HSN, returns text, product fields, samples and theme ranking
  • Product editor: HSN with its rate, unit, weight and size, the category's legal fields; size chart only for apparel
  • One variant by default; options by category (Size, Shade, Weight, Pack of)
  • Returns policy worded by category
  • The GST module

Partner / developer

  • One workspace for every client, any category
  • A category-aware setup checklist to hand over
  • Same 20% share
  • Category packs documented

Frrata Admin

  • A shop's category and wave on its page
  • Waves switched on per shop for design partners first
  • Category filter in Merchants and Usage
  • Restricted goods list; review queue for "Other"
  • Onboarding minutes per shop
