In short

What

One Admin page for what Frrata pays. Repeating costs, one-time costs, and costs that change every bill.

Why now

Monthly burn is the cost side of break-even and funding need. Today it is typed into docs.

What it costs us

Two tables, one page, no integrations, no outbound messages. Reminders ride on Today.

What we need from you

Approve these screens, then the five calls at the end.

Where costs live today

There is no Costs screen to screenshot. This is what exists.

WhereWhat it holdsWhy it falls shortProposed
InfrastructureVercel and Mux usage, estimated from vendor ratesAn estimate of usage, not the bill paid. Other vendors absent.Stays. Costs holds the bill paid.
BillingWhat merchants and partners pay FrrataIncome only.Stays. Costs is the other side.
Docs and rates fileTyped plan prices, datedNo due dates, no history, no total.Burn read from Costs.
Bank and card statementsThe truthNobody sees renewals coming.Due list and Today reminders.

What to take

From product knowledge, not re-read in the last 12 months. Treat as unverified until the research agent confirms.

Stripe dashboard · upcoming invoices

Take: a short list of what is due, each with one action.

Skip: proration and plan graphs.

Zoho Books · recurring expenses

Take: a template that repeats and a payment logged against each due date.

Skip: bills, vendors as a module, approvals.

Subscription trackers

Take: one monthly total and a renewal date on every row.

Skip: email scanning and card linking.

Linear · side panel

Take: a row opens a panel, the list stays put.

Skip: boards and views.
