What changed from the first mockup

The line read "Free for 30 days. First charge ₹1,499 + GST on 23 Dec." That was not true. The fee on each delivered order is charged during a trial, and the plan is billed on the 1st, by days. So the line says only what is true: the plan is free until a date, then its monthly price.

An order shipped during the trial is charged the fee rate of the plan in force when the trial ends (your decision, 23 Nov).

What is absent

No estimate for this month, no invoices, no "Change plan". The plan is chosen in "Open your store?". A store that never opens pays nothing; it is deleted after 180 days, with two notices (your decision, 23 Nov; its own later task).

After opening

The page is as it is today. A store opened on 10 Dec sees nothing billed in December. Its 1 Jan invoice has one extra line: "Your plan from opening last month, by days".
