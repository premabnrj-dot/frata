The Admin rules

  1. Today first. Admin opens on what needs someone, oldest first, one action each. The nav counts the same things.
  2. A waiting task leads its page. One band under the header, with its one action. It goes when the task is done.
  3. One job in view. A record's other parts sit in tabs; a list's first view is "Needs you".
  4. One primary. The header holds the title, at most one primary and More.
  5. Forms open when asked. Add, edit and change open a dialog from a button. No form stands open on a page.
  6. Rare and final in More. Each asks in a dialog, with the reason the other side reads.
  7. Reference folds. Tables you look at now and then are folded; counts that matter stay open.
  8. Your own request says who approves it. No button that would fail.
  9. On a phone, the list and the record are two pages and dialogs are sheets.
