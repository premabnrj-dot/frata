Free trials on plans

Onboarding by trial: a brand signs up, picks a plan, opens its shop, and has 30 days of selling to see its first delivered order and what it kept, before it pays anything. Nothing on this page is built yet. Prices below are the current plan prices; dates are an example shop that signs up on 8 November.

Decided by the owner, 8 Nov 2026 (spec: docs/specs/2026-11-08-trials.md). The screens show the decisions.
  1. Q1 Fee during the trial. Decided: only the monthly plan price is free. The per-order fee and add-ons are charged as usual.
  2. Q2 The end without autopay. Recommended: 7 days of grace, then the shop pauses (no new orders; orders on their way finish normally; data kept 180 days). Or: pause at once.
  3. Q3 Length. Recommended: 30 days from the day the shop opens, never past 45 days from sign-up. Or: a flat 14 days from sign-up.
  4. Q4 Phone check at sign-up by a WhatsApp code, so one business gets one trial.
  5. Q5 Messages. Decided: WhatsApp only, for the code and every reminder. No email, no SMS.

The trial's life

One example shop, drawn to scale. Building the catalogue does not use up the trial: the 30 days start when the shop opens.

If autopay is set up at any point, the trial simply ends into the paid plan on 12 December; the first bill charges the plan by days from then. An upgrade during the trial starts when the trial ends (decided 8 Nov).
