Decided

Merchant's brand to shoppers, Frrata's to merchants and partners · email at checkout, no consent box · Amazon SES from the start · charge above cost in a prepaid wallet.

For you to approve

  1. Buy frrata.shop for shops' order emails; Frrata's notices from notify.frrata.com.
  2. Marketing only to shoppers who tap "Email me new drops" (no box; ready for DPDP from 12 May 2027).
  3. No Frrata mark in shoppers' emails on any plan.
  4. The invoice as a link, not an attachment.
  5. New-order email off by default; push (P·1) instead.
  6. Wallet packs ₹500 / ₹1,600 / ₹5,000 (10 / 8 / 5 paise an email) + GST, 3.5–7× our cost; included each month: Launch 5,000, Growth 10,000, Scale 25,000, so small shops pay no more than on Shopify.
