In short

  • What: checkout, the bag link, the order page and the policies of a code-site store wear its brand record: logo, colours, fonts, corners, spacing. Lines gain Remove and a stepper (O13).
  • What stays Frrata’s: every field and its label, the steps, prices, payment, the pay button’s words, the privacy line and the legal block. No partner pixels or scripts (O12).
  • What a partner sets (Console › Brand › Checkout): logo left or centred, the back link, one or two rows of policy links, which trust blocks show and their order, and two short lines (O14). Trust words come from the store’s settings.
  • Cost: one table, one tab, the pages reading the record (plan P2·1–P2·6). Theme shops unchanged.
  • Needed from you: approve these screens and the calls below.
