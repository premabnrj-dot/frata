Your calls

  1. The four starting zones are Metro (8 metros), Tier 2 (state capitals and 40 more cities), Rest of India and Special (North East, J&K, Ladakh, the islands), filled in from India Post's directory. The brand edits them and adds its own. Recommended.
  2. Today's settings become the Rest of India zone when the app is turned on, so nothing changes for shoppers until the brand edits a zone. Recommended.
  3. The date shown is the latest day of the zone's range ("by Tue, 18 Nov"), never the earliest. Recommended: a promise kept beats a promise early.
  4. The PIN code is remembered on the shopper's phone for the next product (their own browser only). Recommended.
  5. Prepaid-only suggestions and numbers by zone come in PD·2 with True Profit; courier serviceability in PD·3 with Ship.
