Own domains, v2: fewer steps, nothing to press twice

  • One list, one way in. Settings › Shop address shows every address the shop has as rows; the main one carries a chip. "Connect a domain" opens a dialog (a sheet on a phone), not a page.
  • Records appear at once (a call the person waits on, ADR-0008). We read who hosts the domain's DNS ourselves, so there's no "Where did you buy it?" question, and we open that provider's DNS page with one button.
  • The dialog checks for you every 15 s while it's open. Each record flips to Found on its own, and the dialog moves to Securing, then Connected, without a press. "Check now" stays for impatient people. On Hobby the background check is daily; on Pro it moves to every 10 minutes.
  • Send the records to whoever manages your domain on WhatsApp, already written out.
  • Razorpay must approve the domain first (found 9 Nov): live payments fail from a website not registered on the shop's own Razorpay account. Connected shows "Open Razorpay"; the row keeps "Razorpay approved it" (Razorpay takes 24–48 h), and Make main appears once it's ticked. Checkout offers online payment only on an approved address. Razorpay has no API to check this, so we take the merchant's word. The same applies to name.frrata.shop, so it goes on the "Ready for live payments" list.
  • Renaming a shop on live payments never stops checkout (your call, 9 Nov): the new name opens the shop beside the old one, Razorpay reviews it live, and Make main switches over once "Razorpay approved it" is ticked. A shop in test mode renames in one step, as today.
  • Make main works like Publish: a one-line confirm, a 1.3 s "Going live" line, then a popup with Copy, View shop and Undo for 8 s. Until Pro, the frrata name keeps opening the shop instead of redirecting.
  • Problems sit on their row as a chip and one line with the fix. The red banner on every console page shows only after 24 h of failed checks. When the failure is on our side (hosting API down), the row says "Checking paused", never red.
