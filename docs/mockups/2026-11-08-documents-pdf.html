Your shop's paperwork, as branded PDFs

Packing slips, tax invoices and credit notes download as PDFs in the shop's own logo and colour, one order or fifty at once, on A4 or a 4×6 thermal label. The numbers stay the ones issued at packing. Dashed outlines mark what is new.

Why not Ekatan's way

Ekatan drew its PDFs with jsPDF: pure document data, shared blocks, one builder per document, one file function for download and email. We keep that shape. We change the library, because jsPDF breaks Hindi. Tested today:

Under the hood

  • One source of numbers. The slip and invoice data you print today feed the PDF; nothing is recomputed.
  • An issued invoice is stored once. Rendered at packing, kept privately, and every download serves the same file, even after you change your logo.
  • No costs, ever. Document inputs carry no cost fields; a check fails the build if one appears.
  • Nothing fetched from outside. Fonts ship with Frrata; images come from your shop's own storage.
