How Frrata bills its merchants

One bill a month: the plan in advance, the fee on delivered orders in arrears. The merchant sees it growing every day, pays by UPI Autopay without lifting a finger, and gets a GST invoice their CA accepts. Frrata's team runs the month and handles only the few that fail. The shop never stops for an unpaid bill.

How a month works

All monthOrders become billable as they're delivered (or 30 days after shipping with no outcome). "This month so far" updates daily.
1stFrrata issues the invoice: this month's plan + last month's fee + credits. GST invoice PDF.
1stNotice to the merchant: amount, date, why. (RBI: ≥ 24 h before every debit.)
3rdAutopay debit. Over ₹15,000, the merchant approves it, or eNACH collects.
4th–6thUp to 3 retries. A banner and a pay link if they fail.
Day 14Unpaid: your call (below). Never: the shop, checkout or orders stop.

Your decisions, built in: a packed order never shipped is never billed · Lost needs no proof and is not billed · a late Came back or Lost is credited on the next invoice.

UPI Autopay Recommended

Approve once in your UPI app. Debits up to ₹15,000 go through by themselves.

Card

Saved by the bank, never by Frrata. Over ₹15,000 you approve each month.

Bank mandate (eNACH)

For bills over ₹15,000: no approval each month. Takes 1–2 days to set up.

What else billing needs

Built with it

  • One fee rule for every order, shared with Analytics (the plan in force when the order became billable).
  • Credit notes with their own series; adjustments for late outcomes, waivers and upgrades.
  • Partner share statements from paid invoices (B5).
  • Every debit, notice and email is a queued row first (ADR-0006).
  • Shadow billing (B0): a full month of draft bills before any money moves.

Skills, agents and checks

  • New skill frata-billing: the month, snapshot and credit, dunning, GST rules, who sees money.
  • New agent billing-reviewer: any change to plans, fees, bills or GST.
  • ADR-0008: how Frrata bills merchants.
  • Updated: frata-integrations (done today: the dated mandate and gateway facts), frata-unit-economics, frata-architecture.
  • Checks: issued invoices can't change; the billable rule in TypeScript and SQL agree; a golden month fixture.

Your calls

1.Calendar month: plan in advance, fee in arrears, one invoice on the 1st. (Recommended)
2.Collect through Razorpay Recurring Payments: UPI Autopay first, card, eNACH above ₹15,000, a pay link as the fallback. Frrata works out the bill; Razorpay only collects. (Recommended; Cashfree is the alternative.)
3.Unpaid after 14 days: owners and admins see a notice they must acknowledge, and the console keeps working. The shop never stops. (Recommended; or also pause paid modules.)
4.Frrata's GSTIN, registered address and SAC code, from you and your CA, before the first real invoice (B2).
5.Partners are paid their share monthly, from paid invoices only. (Recommended)
