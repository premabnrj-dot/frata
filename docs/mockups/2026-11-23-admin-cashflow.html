In short

What

One Admin page for the whole cash picture: what is in the bank, what came in, what went out, and where the balance goes over the next six months.

Where the numbers come from

Money in from Billing. Money out from Costs. Everything else is typed once as an entry. The balance in the bank is typed with its date.

Why now

Costs gave us what we spend. This adds what we hold and what we earn, so runway and break-even are one glance, not a sum.

What we need from you

Approve these screens, then the calls at the end.

Where cash facts live today

There is no cash screen to screenshot. This is what exists.

What to take

From product knowledge, not re-read in the last 12 months. Treat as unverified until the research agent confirms.

Float and Fathom · cash forecast

Take: opening cash, money in, money out, closing cash, by month.

Skip: scenarios and several companies.

Mercury and Brex · runway line

Take: one runway figure with the date cash runs out.

Skip: card spend controls.

Zoho Books · bank reconcile

Take: the typed balance against what the books say, and the difference.

Skip: bank feeds and matching.

Razorpay dashboard · settlements

Take: money in shown by day with its source.

Skip: payouts to merchants, which Frrata does not make.

Your calls

1 · Who sees CashflowMaster admin only. Recommended.
2 · What counts as money inBilling payments you captured, less refunds, plus entries. Partner credit purchases join once a read for them exists. Recommended.
3 · GSTShow GST to pay as its own line: next month’s from the invoices you issued, later months estimated from billing. Recommended. Your CA confirms the rule.
4 · Forecast lengthSix months, with billing at the average of the last three months. Recommended.
5 · Low-cash floorOne typed figure. Falling below it shows on Today. Recommended.
6 · Costs from the vendorsThe vendor’s figure fills the bill for you to confirm. Recording turns on for a vendor only after you have matched one month. Vercel first, then Anthropic with an admin key. Supabase stays typed. Recommended.
Not builtBank feeds, scenarios, several companies, payouts, and partner payments.
