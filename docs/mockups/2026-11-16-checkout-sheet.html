In short

  • Checkout rises from the button. The shopper taps Checkout in the bag. Checkout opens as a sheet from that button, and the pay bar lands where the button was. They pay, see the order and close. They are still on the same page.
  • One address, one look. The website, the sheet and the secure form share the brand’s address (P5) and its brand record (P1). Nothing new to design per store.
  • Safe as approved. Every personal field, the price for the address and Place order stay in Frrata’s secure form (P3). The website’s code never sees them.
  • Light. Nothing loads until the bag opens. Then checkout opens in under 400 ms.
  • Needed from you: approve the motion and the screens below, and the nine calls at the end.

What moves
  • Checkout → the pay bar. The button stays where the thumb is. Its words change to Place order · ₹2,790.
  • The bag’s title → the brand’s logo. The lock and Secure checkout sit inside the secure form, where the website’s code can’t draw them.
  • The bag → checkout. The website’s drawer closes under the sheet as the sheet rises (320 ms). The lines are in Your order, with the stepper and Remove.
  • Placed: the button draws a check, the order view replaces the form, and the button reads Continue shopping.
  • Closing lowers the sheet. The page shows an empty bag and a toast with View.

Instagram’s and Facebook’s in-app browsers, or Razorpay unable to run in the secure formThe page, as above. Frrata’s server decides.
Back gesture, Esc, CloseThe sheet closes. What was typed stays until the shopper leaves.

Your calls

CallRecommendation
1 · The motionCheckout rises from the Checkout button, the pay bar landing where the button was (drawn).
2 · ComputerA 560 px panel at the right, fading in (drawn), rather than a centred box.
3 · PhoneFull height with an 8 px strip of the page above and a handle (drawn). Swiping down from the top of the sheet closes it.
4 · The confirmationIn the sheet, with Continue shopping back to the same page. The order page stays for the receipt and WhatsApp links.
5 · Where the sheet’s code livesServed by Frrata on the brand’s address, so a fix reaches every website at once. The kit gives one button.
6 · WhenWith one address (P5). Until then the pilot keeps the bag link to the branded checkout page (P2).
7 · A tap the shopper didn’t meanPlace order works only after the browser confirms the form has been fully in view for 0.8 s. Where it can’t confirm that (Safari), a saved address needs one Deliver here tap first. The website can’t change the bag once checkout opens.
8 · What the website learnsOnly that an order was placed, so it can empty its bag. Never the number, the amount or the address.
9 · One checkout layoutThe secure form draws all of checkout, on the full page and in the sheet: the top bar, the bag, the fields, payment, the confirmation and the legal block. This changes approved P3, where the bag and payment choice sat in Frrata’s page around the form.

Not drawn: a sheet on theme stores, partner-styled sheet edges, saved cards.
