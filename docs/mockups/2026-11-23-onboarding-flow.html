Onboarding that builds your store as you answer

Proposal and live prototype · 2026-11-23 · Draft, awaiting owner sign-off · Spec docs/specs/2026-11-23-onboarding-website-and-categories.md

In short

Onboarding

One question at a time, over the checklist that exists. A small store sketch builds as each answer lands. It needs no hosting, no Studio and no theme work, so it can start first.

Website and themes

Retire the theme catalogue and customizer by a gate, not a date. Keep one Starter renderer until hosted code is priced. Merchants reach Studio through a guided, metered step later, not as the default.

More categories

One category pack replaces four copies of the same list. Waves: finish beauty and wellness, then home, food, electronics, each behind its own owner and CA call.

Needed from you: eight decisions at the foot of this page. Nothing is built before you approve this mockup.

States drawn for every step

Drawn: phone, and the error on step 1 (clear the store name and tap Save and continue). The other states are drawn at build. An error shows a field line, then one toast. Staff and Accountant see the checklist, not the flow. A partner-built store starts at what is done. A support visit is read only.

Motion, all from the existing set

Step to step 320 ms · sketch rise 200 ms, three at most · progress by scaleX · open check drawn once, 320 and 200 ms (the existing Open moment) · reduced motion: a 120 ms fade. No single animation runs over 400 ms. Nothing animates a width or a layout. Here the colour and font change at once. The build crossfades two layers on opacity.
