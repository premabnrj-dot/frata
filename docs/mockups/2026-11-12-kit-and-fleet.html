Kit and fleet: how Frrata builds, guards and keeps every site

Studio is what partners see. This is what Frrata's own staff and agents do behind it: build the kit and the starters, set the controls every site runs inside, and ship fixes to a thousand sites without breaking one.

Your handover rule

Owner, 12 Nov: "If the store isn't handed over, there is no need of owner approval during that time for partners."
Build mode

Before handover

The partner publishes after the checks pass. No approval step. The published version shows on a signed preview; the public address says "Opening soon" and stays out of search. The shop takes no orders: the database already refuses to open a shop with no owner.

Handover

The owner claims the shop

With a verified email. The checks run again; the owner sees that exact version and the partner's scopes, and accepts it. The partner's routines lose publish rights. Domain and payments are the owner's to connect.

Owned

After handover

The owner's approval on the exact version, unless the owner gives that partner more.

A partner removed before handover: the site goes to maintenance. A shop that ever had an owner never returns to build mode.

The three update lanes

Lane 1 · Patch

Security and legal fixes

Only fixes signed by the security owner and a second person. Frrata builds the affected sites, runs the checks and a smoke test of each site (pages load, legal block shows, buy opens checkout). A site that changes is held, with a 12-hour clock. The rest deploy in waves, rolled back automatically to a kept version, never below the new minimum. Patched or mitigated within 24 hours; owner and partner told after.

Lane 2 · Minor

New parts

"Update available" in Studio. The agent applies it on a draft with its skill. The usual checks and approval.

Lane 3 · Major

Breaking changes

A codemod and a migration skill. Frrata does it for shops with no partner. The old major gets 12 months; then the site moves to Starter 0 or maintenance, never served unpatched. No minor or major rollouts in festive weeks.

Prices, stock, content and the legal block's words never need a release: the API serves them at runtime.
