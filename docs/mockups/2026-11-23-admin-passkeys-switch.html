Your calls · Recommended: yes to all four

1 · Off means off everywhere

No passkeys anywhere while off. That covers Admin, the partner workspace, money and team actions, handover, Studio and Give key.

2 · Off until switched back on

Passkeys stay off until an Admin turns them on. Every Admin page shows a banner.

3 · Your passkey to turn them off

Turning passkeys off needs your passkey, so a stolen inbox can't do it. Turning them on needs none.

4 · No new passkeys while off

A new passkey still needs its full check. An email code alone can't make one.

While passkeys are off, one inbox is the only lock. A staff inbox opens Admin for every merchant. A partner's inbox opens that partner's stores.
