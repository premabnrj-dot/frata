In short

WhatSettings › People gets "Add a partner". The owner types the partner's code. The partner accepts. Changing partner is Remove, then Add.
Why nowYou asked. A store started by its owner, like House Of Ayuda, can never get a partner today.
CostOne table, one code per partner, four emails. With call 2 as recommended, Frrata keeps its full fee.
Needed from youThe five calls below, and approval of these screens.

What to take

Shopify

Collaborator requests. The owner approves in Users, and can require a collaborator code.

Take: a code the owner gives in, and the owner's yes.

Razorpay partners

The partner sends a link and the account holder accepts.

Skip: that is our hand-over, the other way round.

Linear

An invite is a pending row with an expiry and a Revoke.

Take: a pending row with Cancel and a date.

Your calls

#CallOptionsRecommended
1How the owner names the partner(a) The partner owner's email. Frrata emails an invitation.
(b) A partner code from the workspace, typed by the owner.		(b) Code. The owner sees the partner's name before sending. Nobody can test emails to learn who is a partner. A wrong code says the same thing every time.
2Share for a partner who didn't bring the storeA. 20% for 24 months, as today: ₹875.80 a month, ₹21,019.20 over the term.
B. 10% for 12 months: ₹437.90 a month, ₹5,254.80 in all.
C. No share. The brand pays the partner for the work.		C. The share pays for bringing a new brand. A brand that swaps partners pays twice. The old share runs 90 more days. Before the build we ask 5 partners if they would accept.
3Who may send the invitationOwner only, or owner and ManagerOwner only, like Remove. A Manager sees the row, without buttons.
4How long an invitation lasts3, 7 or 14 days7 days, like the hand-over.
5"Change partner" as one stepNow, or laterLater. Remove then Add covers it. It comes back if owners ask.

Figures from shareExample(): a Launch brand, 120 delivered orders a month at ₹1,200. Frrata collects ₹4,379 a month from it. The brand pays the same under A, B and C.
