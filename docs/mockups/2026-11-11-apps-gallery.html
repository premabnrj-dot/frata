What changes, as rules

  • One card for every app: icon tile, name, the job in one line, and one footer line — price or "Included", plus its state. No ratings, no install counts: these are Frrata's own apps.
  • Your apps first as compact rows with a state ("Working", a meter at 80%), then the gallery grouped by the job: Talk to shoppers · Get paid and ship · Sell more · Run the books.
  • Filters with counts (All · In your plan · Add-ons · On Growth · Coming next) and search; an empty search says so and offers Clear.
  • One featured card at most, chosen from the shop's own numbers (the pricing ladder's nudge), dismissible and staying dismissed. Never a pop-up, timer or animated button.
  • An app's page, not a dialog: header with a one-line benefit; three features, each a drawn 16:10 picture of the app working (never a screenshot with real shoppers); a side column with the price (GST, trial, first charge date), works with, what it uses, billed by others; three questions.
  • One button that matches the state: Turn on · Start free trial · See Growth (with the shop's own rupees) · Open · Tell me when it's ready. A higher-plan app is shown, locked, never a button that fails.
  • Once on, the same home for every app: state chip with the last activity, tabs (Overview · its own · Usage and billing · Settings), up to four numbers, a usage meter where there is a limit, what it uses, and Turn off at the foot with a confirm.
  • Phone: one column, rows instead of cards, the price and button in a bar at the bottom of an app's page, pictures swipe.

Your calls

  1. App pages replace the details dialog (a page per app at /console/apps/<app>; the gallery opens it). Recommended: room for pictures and questions, and a link a partner can send.
  2. Coming-next apps stay in the gallery, muted, with "Tell me when it's ready" (we learn which to build first). Recommended. The alternative hides them until built.
  3. The featured card from the shop's own numbers (one at a time, dismissible). Recommended.
  4. Groups renamed by job: Talk to shoppers · Get paid and ship · Sell more · Run the books (today: Talk · Deliver · Grow · Run).
  5. Pictures are drawn in the console's style (like the website's product pictures), not screenshots. Recommended.
