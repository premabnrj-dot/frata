In short

  • What to take. The owner picks, per partner, Publishes freely (the default) or Ask me first, on the partner’s row in Settings › People.
  • Publishes freely: a version goes live and the owner gets one Notices line with what changed, Preview and Undo. Undo is one tap and a confirm.
  • Ask me first: every version waits; the Notices line opens Review and publish for that exact version.
  • Needs your yes: a version that adds an outside service or changes the domain waits whatever the choice.
  • Why now. V2b is approved and follows V2a. Cost: none. From you: a yes on the screens and the calls.
