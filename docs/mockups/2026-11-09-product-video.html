What a video is for

Fit · ranked problem 2See it on a personThe model turns once, walks three steps. Shown with “model 5 ft 6 in, wears S” from the Fit section, beside the size picker.
FabricSee how it movesChanderi's sheen, a dupatta's sheerness, a lehenga's flare. The one effect with current evidence: handling the cloth on video sharpens how shoppers judge stiffness and stretch (IJRDM, Dec 2025).
DetailClose-upZari, embroidery, a hand-block print; 5–10 s, often part of the same video.
ReuseReels you already madeThe vertical reel from Instagram, uploaded as the file. Import from Instagram fails for reels with licensed music (Meta, 30 Jul 2026), so upload is the main way.
StylingHow to wear or drapeA saree drape, three ways with one dupatta; landscape or vertical, up to a minute.

Phases

PV·1 · One video per productUpload from the phone (MP4 or MOV, 6–60 s, up to 500 MB); reel or landscape found from the file; cover frame; the gallery tile, “See it on a model”, the viewer on phone and computer. Built on the Mux setup the Look's loops already use.
PV·2 · Watch and shopA Look section of reels from the shop's products, on Home and collections; silent preview images; swipe to the next product in the viewer; a video mark on product cards.
PV·3 · More placesGoogle and Meta get the same file; up to 3 videos and a video per colour; import from Instagram when the reel's music allows; captions.
