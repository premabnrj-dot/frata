In short

What you get

The store’s own team places orders on a store that has not opened. They go through the real checkout, pay with Razorpay’s test keys or choose Pay on delivery, and see the real order page, email and console queue.

The one idea

Every order placed before a store first opens is a test order. Nothing is stored to say so. At opening they are all deleted in the same step. After that no test order exists, so no report ever has to leave one out.

Who can reach checkout

Only the team. Start testing in the console opens the store for that browser for 12 hours. Everyone else still sees Opening soon.

What it will not do

It moves no stock, takes no invoice number, sends no WhatsApp and books no courier. It cannot be packed. It works only before the first opening, so not on the pilot, which is open.

What I need

Your answers to the six calls at the end.

#CallRecommendation
1Every order before the first opening is a test order, deleted at openingYes. No mark is stored, so no report can ever count one.
2Test orders cannot be packed or shippedYes. It keeps invoice numbers, couriers and Frrata’s fee out. Bring it back if partners ask to demo the full journey.
3The order email is sent on a test orderYes. Developers and owners want to see it. The subject says “Test order”.
4Who may start testingAnyone who can preview the store: owner, Manager, staff, the partner’s people and Builders.
5A limit of 50 test orders a store, and a 12-hour passYes. It keeps opening day’s clean-up small.
6Test orders on a store that is already open, like the pilotNot now. It needs a stored mark and a filter in about twenty reports. Use a second store that has not opened.
