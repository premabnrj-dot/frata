Proposed motion system

Keep the three durations and three easings we have. Add two curves: a spring with a 4% overshoot, only for things that travel across the screen (sheets, the bag), and a snap with no overshoot, for toggles and the bag count. Opacity and colour keep the plain easings. Nothing bounces on a desktop dialog, a number or in Admin.

Rule changes

  • Rule 22, what moves: add the spring and snap curves. A shadow, filter or layout property never animates.
  • Rule 24, page transitions: when we're on Next 16.4+ and React 19.3, pages fade (120 ms out, 200 ms in) while the shell stays still. The website and shops can fade now, through the browser's own page transition. Nothing ever slides.
  • Rule 25, reduced motion: it removes movement, not meaning. A slide or scale becomes a 120 ms fade, and a countdown becomes words.
  • New: one Switch, one Sheet. One toggle everywhere. Every sheet or drawer, including More and the bag, uses the dialog's motion, with an exit and the standard backdrop.
  • New: shop motion uses the same tokens under the theme's motion setting. Photos fade in over a tile, and the motion check covers the shops.
  • New: no Unicode glyph as an icon, and two menu items never share an icon.

The e2e screenshots will also be taken with reduced motion on. One today caught the publish dialog half faded.

A 600-product catalogue caches at 1.95 MB, and Next refuses anything over 2 MB. One shop-wide offer pushes it over, and every shop page then hits the database. All-products and collections render every product (3.9 MB of HTML).P1 P2 HighRepeated and deadlock-prone queries. A cold product page runs 61 statements because its parallel parts each fetch the same data. One offer path opens a second connection inside a transaction, which can lock up a busy pool for up to 30 s.P3 P4 LatentThe message queue sends 25 a day. Fine today, with no sender, but it must change before WhatsApp COD confirmations ship.P5 MedSign-in cookies can be read by page scripts, aren't marked Secure, and last 400 days.
