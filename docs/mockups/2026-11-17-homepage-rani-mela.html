Changes to today's rules

  1. Rani fills buttons on public pages. Today rani is never a button fill.
  2. Marigold replaces haldi and peacock joins as the good-news colour.
  3. DM Sans replaces Inter for public-page text. Bricolage stays for headlines, at 800.
  4. Hindi lines in Mukta, one per section at most.
  5. The ink stage, selvedge and pictograms give way to white pages, colour tiles and the mark's shapes.
  6. Prices and the order card keep reading from the plans and the profit engine. Nothing typed.
