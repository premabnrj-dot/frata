Same SES account

MARKETING is an allowed mail type. A separate configuration set and outbox kind, so campaigns queue behind order emails and never slow them.

Our own lists

SES allows one contact list per account. Subscribers, consent and one-click unsubscribe (RFC 8058) stay in Frrata, per shop.

No open tracking

Apple hides opens. Clicks go through our link, orders through the link's tags; results count delivered orders minus returns.

Sending from the merchant's domain

Gmail judges mail by the domain in From. Every shop on frratamail.app shares one reputation, so one shop's spam complaints would push every shop's order emails toward spam. Campaigns and flows therefore start only once the shop sends from its own domain. Order emails move to it at the same time.

      Your calls

      1. Own domain required for marketing. Recommended. The alternative (marketing from frratamail.app with low caps) puts every shop's order emails at risk from one bad list.
      2. Caps above (daily, first campaign, frequency) as the starting values. Recommended; raised per shop by Frrata Admin.
      3. Flows count against the monthly emails, except Back in stock and "Finish paying" (free, as Shopify keeps recovery free). Recommended.
      4. Imported lists only through a confirmation email. Recommended: a cold list is the fastest way to a spam rate above 0.3%.
      5. Email marketing is an app in Apps (Grow group), on for every plan, so a merchant sees it once their domain is set. Recommended.
      6. Switch SES to à la carte pricing. Your step in the AWS console.
