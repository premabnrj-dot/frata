Your calls

  1. The rule for a suggestion: COD kept below zero at a PIN code over at least 5 COD orders in the last 90 days, the same rule as True Profit's "COD loses money". Recommended. The alternative is a return-rate threshold (e.g. 40%), which ignores what each order earns.
  2. Every shop with Delivery Zones gets them, with or without True Profit: only the PIN codes to act on, never the full by-PIN or by-product tables, which stay True Profit's. Recommended.
  3. A suggestion never applies itself. One click adds a "Pay online only" rule, with the numbers as its reason. Recommended.
  4. "Not now" hides a suggestion for 30 days (one small table). It comes back if the numbers still say so. Recommended; without it, a brand that disagrees sees the same row every day.
  5. Numbers by zone count orders in the zone their PIN code is in today, so they follow the brand's edits. Recommended.
  6. Zone advice: when a whole zone's COD loses money (same rule, over its COD orders), a line under the table offers "Make pay online only", which edits that zone. Recommended.
  7. Figures refresh every 15 minutes, as True Profit's do.
