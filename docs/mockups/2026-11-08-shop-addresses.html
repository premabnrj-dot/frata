Every shop gets name.frrata.shop the day it opens. A brand connects its own domain by copying three records, then makes it the main address. Frrata's team steps in only for disputes, abuse and copied brands.

11 · Connect frrata.shop now (D·0)

Bought at Hostinger, 1 year, under review. That's fine. The registrar doesn't matter; the nameservers do. Once the review clears, point the nameservers at Vercel and everything below works the same as buying at Vercel.

Your steps

  1. Wait for Hostinger's review (up to 24 hours). Don't buy it twice. If it's refused, buy frrata.shop at Vercel instead ($2.99, then $38.39 a year).
  2. Hostinger › Domains › frrata.shop › DNS / Nameservers › Change nameservers › custom: ns1.vercel-dns.com and ns2.vercel-dns.com.
  3. Before we ask for the Public Suffix List: extend frrata.shop by 2 more years at Hostinger (it needs more than 2 years left). Not urgent for launch.
  4. Buy frrata.com now at Vercel ($12.03 a year): Frrata's own domain for the website, the console and notices. Buying at Vercel keeps both domains' DNS in one place.
  5. Tell me when the review clears.

Then I do

  1. Add frrata.shop, *.frrata.shop and www.frrata.shop to project frata.
  2. Set NEXT_PUBLIC_SHOP_DOMAIN=frrata.shop for production and redeploy from main.
  3. Check a shop opens at kesar-studio.frrata.shop with a certificate.
  4. Add the _psl TXT record and prepare the Public Suffix List request for you to file.
  5. Align Admin's name rule with sign-up's.
