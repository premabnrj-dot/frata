In short

  • What to take. The checkout looks like one page. Its personal part, contact, address, EkCode, the priced summary and Place order, is a Frrata frame on secure. the store’s host.
  • The frame shows the lines and the server’s total itself (decision O24, threats T12 and T13). Place order carries the amount, so a page around it can’t show another total on the button that places the order.
  • Four new states: the frame loading, the frame failed with Try again, EkCode in a popup, the frame’s summary with Place order · ₹2,190. Everything else follows P2’s screens.
  • Why now. P3 is approved. No store with real shoppers moves to one address before it ships (decision O7).
  • What it costs. No new price. The frame’s script stays under 25 kB (decision O24).
  • What is needed from you. A yes on the states, and the calls below.
