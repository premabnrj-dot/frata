Frrata Studio, stress-tested: what breaks, and the controls that hold it

Five independent reviews ran on the plan: security and abuse, tenancy, architecture and upkeep, speed at festive scale, and the 3× pricing. The direction holds. As drafted, the plan does not: 6 blockers, about 30 high findings. Below are the scenarios, the control for each, where it's enforced, the corrected prices, and a reordered plan. Nothing is built.

The five verdicts

Security

Fix before V2

V1 (read API) sound. 3 blockers: partner code beside checkout's cookies; "no personal data" enforced only by the site's own code; one partner login can publish to every client.

Tenancy

Fix before approval

Removing a partner leaves their routines, keys and sessions alive. Studio must never use the "current shop" cookie. Repos keyed by an id, not the shop's name.

Architecture

Hosting: rethink

A law change would need 1,000 human publishes. Hosting and repos undecided. Partner credits can't live in shop rows. Brand and attribution would split into copies.

Speed and scale

Over budget as written

No budgets for the API, sites, builds or agent turns. A flash drop's hot product can hold every shop's database connections. Routines on the hour cause a storm.

Pricing (3×)

Hurts Frrata as drafted

A "turn" costs ₹5–150, not ₹6.2; ₹19 a turn breaks 3×. Hosting allowances fail 3× on every plan. Partner share leaks into credits.

Found in today's code, worth fixing regardless of Studio

  • A flash drop can block every shop's checkout. Placing an order locks the product row for ~25–40 ms across ~15 steps; waiters queue up to 10 s each holding a connection; ~15 waiters can exhaust the shared pool. Fix: a 2 s lock wait on place-order, at most ~8 concurrent places per shop, "busy, try again" instead of a queue.
  • The sold-out purge is debounced per server instance, so a drop with 10 instances purges the catalogue up to 10 times every 30 s. Fix: purge the product, debounce across instances.
  • The checkout quote reads four things one after another. Fix: in parallel inside one transaction.
  • Previews still hold the production database URL (open since an earlier review). Must close before any per-shop preview exists.
  • Staff sign in with an emailed code only. Before any kill switch over sites: two-factor sign-in for staff and partners.

Pricing at 3×, corrected

A "turn" is several model calls, each reading and writing the prompt cache, plus sandbox time while the preview stays up. Measured that way a turn costs Frrata ₹5–150, central ₹16–27. ₹19 a turn would sell most work below cost. The fix keeps 3× by construction: a credit is ₹6.33 of measured cost (tokens, sandbox seconds, build minutes), sold at ₹19. Each session shows an estimate before it starts and is charged on actual use, rounded up.

Decisions for you

1. Credits as measured cost. One credit = ₹6.33 of measured cost, sold at ₹19, estimate first, charged on actuals. Accept?
2. The one exception to "a person publishes". Frrata's own patch lane (security and legal fixes, checks pass, no visual change) deploys to every site without a click. Accept?
3. Content stays data. FAQ, policies, menus, announcements stay console data served by the API; code owns layout. Accept?
4. Hosting off Frrata's Vercel team, with an edge router Frrata owns in front of each site. Cloudflare Workers for Platforms is favoured on security (runtime egress control) and cost; data residency in India to confirm. Proceed to the hosting decision record?
5. The today-fixes. Plan and ship them now (they protect this Diwali's drops), separately from Studio?
6. Partner verification. PAN or GSTIN, two-factor, and a linked opened shop before any free credits or publishing?
