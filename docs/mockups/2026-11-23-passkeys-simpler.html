Your calls

1 · A staff passkey counts in the partner workspace

One way. A partner passkey never opens Frrata Admin. Studio asks once.

Recommended: yes.

2 · A partner's first passkey at the first sign-in

Nobody approves it. It must happen within 7 days of joining. Someone with a stolen inbox at that moment could make it. They would reach the stores in the invite.

Recommended: yes.

3 · Staff always need another master admin

For a first passkey and a lost one. The very first admin stays in the database.

Recommended: yes.

4 · Lost passkey, the approver allows and sends a code

Pia for her team, Frrata for a partner owner, another master admin for staff. The code goes by WhatsApp or in person.

Recommended: yes.

5 · If nobody allows it, a Lead saves one after 24 hours

Leads and Builders only, counted from the emails. Partner owners and staff always wait for a person.

Or a partner owner too, if no Frrata admin answers in 24 hours. The security review advises against it. A phished owner's warning email can be hidden, and nobody above them reads it.

Recommended: Leads and Builders only.

6 · A partner owner's new passkey waits 72 hours for risky steps

Hand over, invite, Give key and deleting passkeys.

Recommended: yes.

7 · Give key still needs a partner passkey

A staff person saves one in one tap, then gives keys.

Recommended: yes.

8 · Remove the passkeys-off switch, last

After the rest ships and you pass a check on each browser.

Recommended: yes.

9 · Requests waiting today

Closed when this ships. Each person is emailed to ask again.

Recommended: yes.

Unchanged: a check lasts 12 hours. Money and team steps ask again after 5 minutes.

  • Passkeys for store owners and Managers
  • Signing in with a passkey alone, without the email code
  • Recovery codes
  • One passkey for every role
